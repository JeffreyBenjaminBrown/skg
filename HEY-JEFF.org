* Estimated progress through [[file:TODO/partial-reload-and-magit/11_plan_simplify-cohere.org][11_plan_simplify-cohere.org]]

As of 2026-09-10, this is a rough implementation-plus-acceptance estimate
based on committed evidence in [[file:TODO/partial-reload-and-magit/progress.org][progress.org]]. Mass is relative work size in
points; progress is the estimated completed fraction of that item's work.
The 15 pairs are point estimates, and the total is the weighted mean
sum(progress times mass) / sum(mass).

** Execution checkpoints and starting points: mass 60, progress 75%

Baseline, tracing, model tests, and several working-path checkpoints are
recorded. Final acceptance remains, so this foundation is substantial but not
complete. Its mass is moderate because it establishes shared checkpoints and
verification accounting rather than most product behavior.

** Establish identities and build the executable state model: mass 70, progress 70%

Identity, incident, operation, census, settlement, and restart cases are
modeled and tested. The full live integration and failure matrix remains. Its
mass is high because these identities and invariants constrain nearly every
later protocol path.

** Make state ownership and journal publication exclusive: mass 80, progress 65%

The owner and ordered journal publication have committed focused coverage,
including failure handling. Interactive authority and every writer entry point
still need convergence. Its mass is high because exclusive ownership and
durable ordering cross coordinator, workers, and journal recovery.

** Build the immutable publication boundary on every read path: mass 75, progress 65%

Pinned graph and manifest bases, selected publication, and later-save proof
are committed. Ordinary read fallback guards and complete integration remain.
Its mass is high because one coherent graph/search publication must be adopted
by every read and write boundary.

** Remove TypeDB and adapt its behavioral tests: mass 55, progress 90%

Active fixtures, setup, documentation, and service cleanup are TypeDB-free
with broad passing Rust coverage. Aggregate and platform acceptance still
remain. Its mass is lower because this is a bounded dependency and fixture
migration with limited remaining product design.

** Bound observation and preserve targeted reload semantics: mass 45, progress 60%

Debounce, maximum-age observation, and reload bracketing are evidenced. The
complete targeted and cancellable observation path is unfinished. Its mass is
smaller because observation is a focused subsystem, though correctness depends
on cancellation and reconciliation edges.

** Make ordinary saves durable before enabling early release: mass 85, progress 75%

Async saves, crash recovery, completion markers, retained bases, and
dependency proof have committed evidence. Integration with every release and
recovery boundary remains. Its mass is very high because recoverable writes
span preparation, source effects, publication, restart, and authority release.

** Preserve archive, raw-file, privacy, and undo gates: mass 60, progress 75%

Archive, privacy, raw-file, dirty-view, and native undo checks are covered in
focused suites. Cross-incident ACK and final lifecycle combinations remain.
Its mass is moderate because these are several independent safety gates with
established interfaces and focused coverage.

** Close admission, drain constructors, and publish the fixed census: mass 70, progress 75%

Fixed census, constructor admission, owner revision, and read-only behavior
have focused and TCP evidence. Full both-editor lifecycle acceptance is
pending. Its mass is high because the barrier coordinates admission,
constructors, census data, and publication across both editors.

** Implement refusal, status, and durable query waits in both clients: mass 90, progress 70%

Both clients have request-owned cleanup, status/refusal handling, per-buffer
restrictions, indexed incident retention, and committed durable query waits.
The waits are tested through actual editor maintenance-rebuild behavior.
Scoped incident callbacks remain incomplete. Its mass is very high because
protocol outcomes, durable recipes, status rendering, and two client
implementations must agree.

** Settle buffers after publication with exact base checks: mass 75, progress 65%

Incarnation checks, retained semantic bases, exact census acknowledgments, and
independent restrictions are committed. The complete cross-generation and
callback settlement lifecycle remains. Its mass is high because safe release
depends on exact bases, identities, restrictions, and acknowledgments at
several boundaries.

** Route every maintenance entry through the same transition: mass 70, progress 55%

Coordinator publication and several command paths use the new boundary. Git,
reload, rebuild, save, and report entry integration still needs one common
final transition. Its mass is high because every maintenance entry point must
share the same transition and failure semantics.

** Restore authority before exposing startup and reconnect: mass 65, progress 70%

Fresh session authority and interrupted save/incident reconstruction have
committed TCP evidence. The complete editor and process crash matrix remains.
Its mass is substantial because restart recovery combines durable records,
session identity, editor state, and fresh authority.

** Finish evidence and reports independently of new graph work: mass 80, progress 55%

The committed incident ledger, retained records, census facts, evidence
foundations, and durable query waits are in place. Scoped callbacks, ACK
delivery, and report recovery remain incomplete. Its mass is high because
independent reports and evidence require durable delivery, recovery, and
cross-incident coordination.

** Verify behavior, then remove superseded paths and update documentation: mass 80, progress 35%

Many focused suites and TypeDB-free checks pass, with useful TCP schedules.
Final aggregate acceptance, obsolete-path removal, and documentation
completion are still substantial work. Its mass is high because final
verification must cover both clients and removal must preserve the settled
contract.

** Weighted total

percent. Therefore the total is 70025 / 1060 = 66.0613207547%, reported as 66%
