* Estimated progress through 11_plan_simplify-cohere.org

As of 2026-09-09, using the plan and the latest progress checkpoint (including
the reported revisions bdfb976e, 8b2a7401, and 7fc04ca3), implementation plus
required acceptance verification is estimated at 56% overall. Each of the 15
implementation-sequence sections below has equal weight (1/15); the point
estimates sum to 845, and 845 / 15 = 56.3%, rounded to 56%.

** Execution checkpoints and starting points: 80%

Baseline tracing and the working-path checkpoints are established; final
acceptance evidence is still pending.

** Establish identities and build the executable state model: 65%

Identity and incident models are exercised, but the complete obsolete-worker,
crash, and restart matrix is unfinished.

** Make state ownership and journal publication exclusive: 60%

Owner and journal paths are substantially migrated and tested; the full owner
migration and normalized durable writer path remain incomplete.

** Build the immutable publication boundary on every read path: 60%

Selected graph/manifest publication and several read guards work; remaining
ordinary-read dependency guards and integration are still gated.

** Remove TypeDB and adapt its behavioral tests: 85%

Active fixtures and setup are TypeDB-free with broad passing suites; final
product and aggregate acceptance checks remain.

** Bound observation and preserve targeted reload semantics: 55%

Maximum-age debounce is implemented and reload brackets are tested, while
targeted/bounded cancellable ordinary observation remains incomplete.

** Make ordinary saves durable before enabling early release: 50%

Crash slices and completion markers pass, but retained save bases and the pure
dependency validator are uncommitted and integration remains gated.

** Preserve archive, raw-file, privacy, and undo gates: 65%

Archive, dirty-view, privacy, and undo protections have focused coverage; the
repeated archive ACK/proposal issue still needs resolution.

** Close admission, drain constructors, and publish the fixed census: 65%

Fixed-census and read-only admission behavior is proven in focused and TCP
checks; both-editor lifecycle acceptance is outstanding.

** Implement refusal, status, and durable query waits in both clients: 40%

Refusal/status adapters exist in both clients, but durable query waits and
client multiple-incident/per-buffer restriction maps are not complete.

** Settle buffers after publication with exact base checks: 45%

Exact-base and scalar-gated settlement pieces work; cross-generation proposal
validation and the full restriction lifecycle remain.

** Route every maintenance entry through the same transition: 50%

Several entry paths use the owner and publication contract, but integration of
all source-changing commands and the common final boundary is unfinished.

** Restore authority before exposing startup and reconnect: 55%

Restart report reconstruction and fresh-session authority have focused checks;
the complete editor/process crash acceptance matrix is not run.

** Finish evidence and reports independently of new graph work: 40%

Independent report/evidence records have meaningful coverage, while durable
query waits, remaining ACK behavior, and final delivery recovery continue.

** Verify behavior, then remove superseded paths and update documentation: 30%

Many focused suites pass, but final all-tests acceptance for both editors and
superseded-path removal have not completed.
