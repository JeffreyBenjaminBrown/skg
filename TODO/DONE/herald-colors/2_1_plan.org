* Herald colors and names: the definitive plan
This plan supplants [[file:2_plan.org][2_plan.org]]. That file and [[file:1_discussion.org][1_discussion.org]] record how
these decisions were reached. Nothing here is implemented yet.

The plan has two parts. [[*The rules]] is the specification, written in
the names that phase 1 introduces. [[*The phases]] says how to get there.
* The rules
** Styles
A herald's color states its importance tier, or for a few heralds a
polarity or kind, never its hue. There are ten styles. Each has one
name everywhere: in the shared style file, on the wire, in the Emacs
faces ('heralds-STYLE-face') and in the Neovim highlight groups
('SkgHeraldSTYLE').

| Style       | Look                                     | Meaning                         |
|-------------+------------------------------------------+---------------------------------|
| crucial     | black on cyan '#00ffff'                  | the reader must understand this |
| high        | black on yellow                          | high interest                   |
| medium      | white on orange '#d2691e'                | medium interest                 |
| low         | white on olive '#5e5e20'                 | low interest                    |
| normal      | white on blue                            | ordinary                        |
| message     | black on white                           | prose meant to be read          |
| stop        | white on red                             | polarity: bad, or not yet       |
| go          | black on green '#00bb00'                 | polarity: good, done            |
| nonstandard | white on purple '#8b00ff'                | requests and diff facts         |
| yucky       | pink '#c84286' foreground, no background | error-flavored, but not too bad |

Foreground/background inversions are an unused reserve. A comment beside
the style definitions says so.

Skg dictates every face in a Skg view, in both clients: a black
background, a white default foreground, and Skg's own faces for the Org
elements a view contains. These are colors and weights, not the
typeface. Nothing from the user's theme shows, so no style needs to work
on a light background.
** Relationship heralds
*** Letters
The five relation letters are C (contains), L (links to), S (subscribes
to), O (overrides the view of) and H (hides from its subscriptions).
They are normal. A letter whose relation explains this occurrence's
presence, its /birth/ relation, is high instead. Only the letter
changes; its counts keep their own styles.
*** Tier order
normal < low < medium < high < crucial.
*** Side tiers
Each relation has an in-side ("N nodes R it") and an out-side ("it Rs N
nodes").

| Relation              | in-side                         | out-side                                                                                     |
|-----------------------+---------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| contains              | medium                          | normal; low on a write-protected occurrence (budget-truncated ones included); unintegrated numerator medium |
| links to              | medium; substantive subset high | normal                                                                                       |
| subscribes to         | high                            | medium                                                                                       |
| overrides the view of | low                             | medium                                                                                       |
| hides                 | medium                          | normal                                                                                       |

A mentioner is /substantive/ if it has nonempty body text, visible
direct content, or links to more than one distinct visible target.

Most normal sides are normal because their members are usually visible
elsewhere: as view-children, in the text, or in the
hidden-in/outside-subscribee folders. This is a rationale for the
table, not a mechanism.
*** Ancestor flags
Ancestor flags are the lowercase letters (a = parent, b = grandparent,
…; folders count as generations) that replace a count when ancestors
account for it.
*** Floors
Ancestor 'a' is at least low. 'b' and higher are at least medium. Every
other glyph has floor normal.
*** Carriers
A glyph /shows/ a tier by being drawn in that tier's style. A side's
tier is shown by one designated part of the side, its /carrier/; to
/carry/ the side's tier is to be that part. The carrier is the side's
numeral, if the side shows one; otherwise it is the side's ancestor
flags, all of them together.

A carrier shows the greater of its floor and the side's tier. Every
other glyph on the side shows just its floor, except the slash. For
example, on the subscribes-to in-side, whose tier is high, =2aS= draws
the 2 high and the 'a' low. =aS= has no numeral, so the 'a' is the
carrier and is drawn high.

A fraction's numerator is a subset with a tier of its own, such as the
substantive subset of mentioners, which is high. It gets its own
carrier, chosen the same way from the numerator's glyphs. The side's
carrier is chosen from the denominator's glyphs. On the links-to
in-side, =1/5aL= draws the 1 high, the 5 medium and the 'a' low. =a/5L=
draws the 'a' high, because it carries the subset's tier, and the 5
medium. When the denominator shows nothing, as in =5/L=, only the slash
shows the side's tier.
*** Birth-explained sides
A side whose only members are ancestors that the birth relation already
accounts for has tier low. For example, 'aC' (the parent is the sole
container) is low. A subscribee's 'bS' (its subscriber is the
grandparent) is medium, because of b's floor.
*** The slash
A fraction's slash takes its side's tier, the same as its denominator.
In =2/5L=, the 2 is high, and the slash and 5 are medium.
*** Property counts
A (aliases), I (extra IDs) and F (flags) are low.
*** Markers
ĥ (overrides here) is crucial.
** Other heralds
| Herald                                               | Style       |
|------------------------------------------------------+-------------|
| ☮ write-protected, on nodes, folders and flag rows   | crucial     |
| B (hidden body, abutting ☮)                          | crucial     |
| ⊥ affects not its parent                             | crucial     |
| ⟳ cycle                                              | medium      |
| ⌂ home-repo herald                                   | normal      |
| folder labels and property-row labels                | message     |
| =Reference to unknown node.=                         | message     |
| =node from inactive repo=                            | message     |
| =~NAME=, a relRepo more private than the default     | yucky       |
| save requests and view requests                      | nonstandard |
| =DELETED=, on a 'Phantom::Deleted' or a dead viewnode | message    |

=DELETED= never appears on a 'Phantom::Diff', which renders diff axes
instead. The dead-viewnode herald reads plain =DELETED=, without
echoing its internal kind.
** Diff heralds
A go-styled ✓ marks staged changes, and a stop-styled ✗ marks unstaged
ones, replacing the words "staged:" and "unstaged:". The axes are N
(node existence) and R (relationship membership), with signed forms N,
-N, R and -R. They and the other diff facts are nonstandard. The
metadata atoms are 'addedN', 'deletedN', 'addedR' and 'removedR'.

A text change reads =text changed ✓ ✗=, showing whichever marks apply:
the words nonstandard, the ✓ go, the ✗ stop.
** Link annotations
The optional suffix after a body-text link shows the target's home repo.

| Target state            | Suffix              | Style  |
|-------------------------+---------------------+--------|
| resolved                | =[⌂:pub]= etc.      | normal |
| inactive repo           | =[⌂:inactive]=      | yucky  |
| lookup pending          | =[⌂:…]=             | yucky  |
| lookup failed           | =[⌂:lookup failed]= | yucky  |
| missing (a broken link) | none                |        |

A broken link's label is yucky and underlined. Underlining marks
linkness and is independent of the style.
** Names
*** The principle
One concept has one name. Sweeping every term is out of scope, but any
multiply-named term found while executing this plan is in scope.

Prose and code may spell a name differently. Code is compact enough to
invite misreadings that prose doesn't, so it may close up a compound
word: 'home_skg_repo' could be read as ((home skg) repo), so code writes
'home_skgrepo'. Prose avoids unfamiliar compound words and writes "home
Skg repo".
*** The relations
Each relation has one name in code, on the wire, in herald keys, in .skg
fields and in the shared relations schema. Its Rust enum variant is the
same name in CamelCase.

| In prose                     | In code                      | Roles                  | Letter | Formerly                                               |
|------------------------------+------------------------------+------------------------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------|
| contains                     | contains                     | container, contained   | C      | (unchanged)                                            |
| links to                     | links_to                     | mentioner, mentioned   | L      | textlinks_to, textlinksTo, TextlinksTo; source, dest   |
| subscribes to                | subscribes_to                | subscriber, subscribee | S      | subscribes (wire and herald key), Subscribes           |
| hides from its subscriptions | hides_from_its_subscriptions | hider, hidden          | H      | hides (herald key)                                     |
| overrides the view of        | overrides_view_of            | overrider, overridden  | O      | overrides (herald key)                                 |

Every name in the code column is already the .skg field name or is not
stored, so no .skg data changes.
*** Other terms
| Concept                                    | Name                                                                    |
|--------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| a directory of .skg files ("source")       | prose: Skg repo. Code: 'repo', or 'skgrepo' where Git repos share the scope |
| a Git repository                           | prose: Git repo. Code: 'gitrepo'                                        |
| where a node lives                         | prose: home repo, or home Skg repo. Code: 'home_repo', or 'home_skgrepo' |
| boolean file properties                    | flags: herald F, folder "flags", =req:flags=; code 'Flag'               |
| aliases, extra IDs and flags ("quals")     | properties; code 'Property', 'PropertyFolder'                           |
| A, I and F together                        | property counts                                                         |
| a substantive mentioner                    | substantive (formerly "interesting")                                    |
| cannot be edited here                      | write-protected (not "read-only")                                       |
| why this occurrence is here                | birth (not "reason-for-being")                                          |
| ĥ                                          | overrides-here                                                          |
| diff axes                                  | N (node), R (relationship)                                              |
| a lookup that did not succeed              | lookup failed (not "unavailable")                                       |
| deletion                                   | 'DELETED' (fact), 'delete' (request), 'deletedN' (diff fact)            |
| a non-node view position ("scaffold")      | no general name: name the kind, or say "non-vognode"                    |
*** The viewnode types
#+begin_example
viewnode = vognode | propertyFolder | property | partnerFolder
         | bufferRoot | deadViewnode
vognode  = active | inactive | phantom
#+end_example
A vognode represents a graphnode, which need not exist. The Rust types
are 'Viewnode', 'ViewnodeKind', 'ViewnodeStats' and so on, with a
lowercase n, so that 'DeadViewnode' cannot be read as ((dead view)
node). The vognode variants are 'ActiveVognode', 'InactiveVognode' and
'Vognode::Phantom'.
*** The graphnode types
| Type                 | What it is                               | Formerly       |
|----------------------+------------------------------------------+----------------|
| 'Graphnode'          | the full node, the canonical form        | 'NodeComplete' |
| 'GraphnodeOnDisk'    | the shape of a .skg file                 | 'NodeFS'       |
| 'GraphnodeInRust'    | the projection held in the in-Rust graph | 'NodeRust'     |
| 'GraphnodeInTantivy' | what the search index stores             | 'NodeTantivy'  |
| 'GraphnodeStats'     | a graphnode's relation counts            | 'GraphNodeStats' |

The action types ('DefineNode', 'SaveNode', 'DeleteNode', 'NodeMerge',
'NodeEditRequest', 'NodeChanges') keep their names. ego_tree's 'NodeId',
'NodeRef' and 'NodeMut' name nodes of the view tree, come from an
external crate, and also stay.
** Shared files
'shared/' holds data that the server and both clients read at runtime:
'herald-styles.json' (the palette, the side-tier table and the floors)
and 'relations.json' (the relations schema). 'tests/shared/' holds data
that only tests read.
* The phases
Renames come first, then the style vocabulary, then behavior. Each step
lands as one or more granular commits; related renames may share a
commit. Every commit leaves all test suites passing.
** Phase 1: renames
No behavior change. Tests change only in names, and no .skg data
changes. The order of 1a and 1b matters.
*** 1a. The relations
This step makes each relation's name match the relations table
throughout: the Rust enum ('TextlinksTo' → 'LinksTo', 'Subscribes' →
'SubscribesTo'), 'NodeRelation::relation_name' and
'OUTBOUND_RELATIONSHIP_TYPES', the herald keys in the wire's
'(rels …)' form, both clients' renderers and parsers, the Emacs relSource
menu ('skg--relationship-kind-menu-tree'), tests and docs.

The link relation's roles become mentioner and mentioned, replacing
source and dest. "Textlink" becomes "link" in identifiers and prose
(e.g. 'server/types/textlinks/' → 'server/types/links/'), and
interesting link sources become substantive mentioners
('link_source_is_interesting', 'links_interesting_in', the wire's
'(interesting …)' sub-form).

This step comes first because it removes the link-source sense of
"source", which leaves 1b nearly mechanical.
*** 1b. Source → Skg repo
This is the largest rename: thousands of occurrences in 'server/',
'elisp/', 'nvim/', 'tests/' and 'docs/'. After 1a, "source" in the code
means the repo almost everywhere. The few remaining English uses
("source code", an import's source files) are reviewed by hand. It is
split into commits along these seams.
**** Rust types and functions
'SourceName' → 'RepoName', 'SourceDiff' → 'RepoDiff', 'ActiveSourceSet' →
'ActiveRepoSet', 'source_sets' → 'repo_sets', and so on. In
'server/git_ops/' and other Git-adjacent code, a Skg repo is 'skgrepo'
and a Git repo is 'gitrepo' (e.g. 'is_git_repo' → 'is_gitrepo').
**** Config
'[[sources]]' → '[[repos]]' and source-sets → repo-sets, in the
'skgconfig.toml' parser, the roughly 31 test config files,
'example-data/' and 'data/skgconfig.toml'. There is no compatibility
shim. The .skg files store no source field, so node data needs no
migration.

Each Skg repo's 'DEPENDENCIES.toml' is regenerated at every server start,
and its header comment says "source". The first server start after
this commit rewrites that comment in every Skg repo under 'data/', which
leaves a diff in each of those Git repos for Jeff to commit. This plan
commits nothing inside them.
**** Wire and metadata atoms
'source' → 'repo', 'relSource' → 'relRepo', 'sourceHerald' →
'homeRepoHerald', and the request names ('relSource info', 'source
sets'), on the server and in both clients.
**** Client files and commands
For example 'skg-request-source-sets.el' and
'skg-request-relSource-info.el', plus user-facing command names and
messages.
**** Docs
'docs/' and 'coding-advice/', including 'docs/telescopes.org' and the
glossary.
*** 1c. Boolean properties → flags
'FileProperty' → 'Flag', 'QualFolder::BoolProps' and 'Qual::BoolProp' →
flag forms, 'boolprops' → 'flags', the 'property' metadata and
editRequest atom → 'flag', =req:properties= → =req:flags=,
'skg-request-boolprop-state.el' → a flags name, and the herald letter P
→ F. On disk, flags sit in a .skg file's 'misc' list as the enum's
variant names. Those names stay, so nothing on disk changes.
*** 1d. Diff axes
'newX'/'removedX'/'newM'/'removedM' → 'addedN'/'deletedN'/'addedR'/'removedR';
'ExistenceAxes' and 'MembershipAxes' → node and relationship axes; and
the herald letters X/M → N/R. This touches 'server/types/git.rs', the
rule table, both clients, and roughly 325 test occurrences.
*** 1e. Smaller terms
Read-only → write-protected, wherever it means write-protected.
Reason-for-being → birth. "Action counts" → property counts. The
link-annotation state 'unavailable' → 'lookup failed'.
*** 1f. The viewnode types
**** Renames
'ViewNode' → 'Viewnode' everywhere ('ViewNodeKind', 'ViewNodeStats', …;
about 900 occurrences in 'server/'). 'Qual' → 'Property' and
'QualFolder' → 'PropertyFolder'. 'DeadScaffold' → 'DeadViewnode', and
its metadata atom 'deletedScaffold' (whose value is 'deadScaffold') →
'deadViewnode'. "Scaffold" retires. The generic helpers
('insert_scaffold_as_child', 'unique_scaffold_child_of_viewnode',
'detach_scaffold_transferring_focus', the 'Body_of_Scaffold' error, …)
and roughly 400 identifier and comment uses get names that say which
kinds they handle, or "non-vognode" where they handle every non-vognode
kind.
**** Phantom moves under Vognode
This is the one step in phase 1 that is more than a rename, so it comes
last. 'ViewNodeKind::Phantom' becomes 'Vognode::Phantom'. Patterns
that name an inner variant (about 215 references to 'Vognode::Active'
and its kin) are unaffected. Exhaustive matches on 'Vognode' fail to
compile until they handle the phantom.

Seven sites are audited by hand, because they match a whole vognode and
may assume it is a current graph member:
- 'id_if_vognode' in 'server/types/viewnode.rs'
- 'server/to_org/util.rs'
- 'server/types/tree/generic.rs'
- 'server/update_buffer/ancestry.rs', which also holds the one
  'Vognode (_) | Phantom (_)' pattern
- 'server/types/tree/viewnode_nodecomplete.rs'
So are the methods on 'Vognode' ('id', 'pid_and_source'), which define
what a phantom returns. Doc comments that call vognodes graph-backed
change to the new definition. The wire is unaffected: the metadata
forms ('node', 'diffPhantom', 'deleted', 'unknown', 'inactiveNode')
stay.
*** 1g. The graphnode types
The renames in [[*The graphnode types]], including the roughly 770
'nodecomplete…' function names, plus 'ActiveNode' → 'ActiveVognode',
'InactiveNode' → 'InactiveVognode' and 'MpActiveNode' alike.
** Phase 2: shared files and the style vocabulary, with no visual change
*** The shared style file
'shared/herald-styles.json' holds the palette (style → foreground,
background, underline), including the non-herald faces of phase 6, the
side-tier table and the floors. Both clients read it at load time, by a
path relative to their own source. In this phase it maps today's colors
onto the new style names, so the clients render exactly as before.
*** The shared relations schema
'shared/relations.json' holds, per relation: its name, display letter
and position in display order, its two roles, which role's .skg file
stores the edge, and the folder kind for each role. Rust keeps its enum,
for exhaustive matching. A conformance test checks the enum and
'OUTBOUND_RELATIONSHIP_TYPES' against the file. Both herald renderers
take letters and order from it. The Emacs relSource menu takes relation
and role names from it, and keeps its menu prose. When Neovim gains the
folder policies, it reads the same file.
*** Server
'HeraldColor' → 'HeraldStyle', with ten variants serialized as the style
names. The rule table keeps its current visual assignments; today's
'Orange' becomes high, which is what it already renders as. A
conformance test checks that every style the rule table names is
defined in the style file. 'tests/elisp/herald-rules.sexp' is
regenerated.
*** Clients
The lens engines' color-keyword lists ('skg--color-keyword-p' in
'elisp/skg-sexpr/skg-lens.el', and the keyword table in
'nvim/lua/skg/sexpr/lens.lua') accept the style names. Faces and
highlight groups are defined from the shared file, with the new names.
This removes the swapped-hue keywords logged in 'TODO/problems.org'.
** Phase 3: shared rendering fixtures
A shared case file under 'tests/shared/' (metadata → expected text and
style spans) is read by both 'tests/elisp/test-heralds-minor-mode.el'
and 'tests/nvim/heralds_spec.lua'. Today's hand-mirrored cases move into
it, as does the pinned rule table 'tests/elisp/herald-rules.sexp'. From
here on, each rendering change is specified once.
'tests/shared/literal-link-cases.txt' is the precedent.
** Phase 4: birth facts name the explaining ancestor
The birth-explained rule needs to know which ancestor, on which side,
the birth relation accounts for. Each birth entry on the wire gains its
side and generation, e.g. '(birth (contains in 1))' instead of '(birth
contains)'. 'birth_relations' in 'server/update_buffer/viewnodestats.rs'
supplies them:

| Birth                             | Relation, side, generation                     |
|-----------------------------------+------------------------------------------------|
| ordinary content                  | contains, in, 1                                |
| subscribee-folder member          | subscribes_to, in, 2                           |
| subscriber-folder member          | subscribes_to, out, 2                          |
| overridden-folder member          | overrides_view_of, in, 2                       |
| overrider-folder member           | overrides_view_of, out, 2                      |
| hidden-folder member              | hides_from_its_subscriptions, in, 2            |
| hider-folder member               | hides_from_its_subscriptions, out, 2           |
| hidden-in-subscribee member       | hides_from_its_subscriptions, in, 4; contains, in, 2 |
| hidden-outside-subscribee member  | hides_from_its_subscriptions, in, 3            |
| drawn overrider (overrides-here)  | overrides_view_of, out, no ancestor            |
| backpath graft                    | its role's relation and side, at the graft's generation |

The implementation verifies these generations against the code. The
change covers 'server/herald_tokens.rs' and its wire doc, both clients'
parsers, and the tests.
** Phase 5: new style assignments
Each item is a commit, with its fixture cases updated.
*** Relationship renderer, in both clients
All letters normal and the birth letter high; the side tiers, floors,
carriers and birth-explained rule, read from the shared files; the slash;
A, I and F low; ĥ crucial. The multiple-containers special case goes.
*** Rule table
☮ becomes its own crucial token: each write-protected label gets two
rules matching the same atom, one emitting ☮ and one the message, and
the renderer's ordinary space separates them. 'leaf_ro!' and the ☮
prefix in 'Flag' herald text disappear. The remaining assignments follow
[[*Other heralds]]: B and ⊥ crucial, ⟳ medium, ⌂ normal, labels message,
unknown and inactive placeholders message, =~NAME= yucky, requests
nonstandard, and =DELETED= message, without the echoed kind.
*** Diff heralds
The ✓/✗ prefixes and nonstandard N/R facts, in every form of the rule
table that carries diff axes.
*** Link annotations
The suffix table in [[*Link annotations]], in
'elisp/skg-link-annotations.el' and 'nvim/lua/skg/link_annotations.lua'.
Broken-link labels use yucky plus an underline in both clients.
*** Final palette values
Go becomes '#00bb00' with black text, and yucky becomes a pure
foreground. Every other value is unchanged from today's nearest face.
** Phase 6: Skg dictates the faces in its views
In Emacs, 'skg-content-view-mode' remaps buffer-locally the 'default'
face to white on black, and the Org faces a view uses (headline levels,
links, and the like) to Skg's own definitions. In Neovim, Skg view
windows get window-local highlights ('winhighlight') doing the same,
applied by the autocmd that sets their conceal options. The palette for
these faces lives in 'shared/herald-styles.json'.
** Phase 7: docs
'docs/heralds.org' is rewritten around the styles and the herald kinds.
One table of kind → style → glyphs covers relationship heralds, property
counts, position markers, home-repo heralds, labels, requests, diff
facts and placeholders, replacing "Other heralds". The docs state that
Neovim assumes 'termguicolors'. The drift logged in 'TODO/problems.org'
on 2026-10-04 is fixed, and that entry moves to 'TODO/DONE/problems.org'.
** Verification
After each commit, run the affected suites, one at a time:
'bash/nextest.sh' (Rust), 'bash/emacs-tests.sh' and 'bash/nvim-tests.sh'
(the clients), and 'bash/integration-tests.sh' for the wire, config and
rename steps.

Tests can check style names but not colors, so the work ends with Jeff
looking at a view in each client.
