* (chosen, switchable) : '--yes' alone cancels the delete-from question
Emacs asks "delete from the visible containers, or all?"; the tool's
'--yes' takes the answer that writes nothing more, so a deletion that
raises the question is cancelled unless '--delete-from visible|all' is
given. Switch: let '--yes' mean "visible", the client's first choice.
** DONE jeff: When does that happen? i.e. under what condiitions?
*** TODO Jeff : Ed's answer
Only under a skgrepo restriction, and only when you delete a node that
is also named by relationships recorded in repos the restriction keeps
out (say, one of your private sections contains it). The server then
writes nothing and asks "Delete from: cancel, visible records only, all
owned records", with the count ('docs/COMMANDS.org', C-c <backspace>).
With no restriction, or when nothing hidden names the node, the
question never comes and the deletion just happens. So the switch
matters only to a restricted session deleting a node with hidden
containers; my servers run unrestricted, so I expect to meet it rarely,
which is why I let '--yes' pick the cautious answer.
* (chosen, switchable) : 'relRepo:NAME subtree' infers the kind from its headline
Emacs offers a menu (node, subscribee folder, overridden folder); the
tool reads the kind from the headline the word is written on. Switch:
an explicit kind word.
** DONE jeff : When, where does emacs offer that menu?
*** TODO Jeff : Ed's answer
In 'skg-set-relRepo-recursive' (C-c s R, or C-u C-c s r), before it
asks for the repo: an org-menu of the five node-node relations, whose
level-2 headlines are the roles a view-child can play toward its
viewparent; the three settable ones are content, subscribee and
overridden ('docs/COMMANDS.org', C-c s R). It selects which
relationships in the subtree the new relRepo applies to. The plain
C-c s r has no menu; it acts on the one relationship at point. The tool
reads the kind from the headline the word sits on, so 'relRepo:NAME
subtree' on a node applies to content relationships, on a subscribee
folder to subscriptions, on an overridden folder to overrides. What it
cannot express is "content AND subscribee at once" in one word; that
would be two saves, or a kind word. I expect one kind at a time to be
the common case.
