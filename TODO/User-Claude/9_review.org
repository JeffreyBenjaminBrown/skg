#+title: User Claude: review of plan 8, and choices Jeff may overturn

* TODO (chosen, switchable) : a Unix socket in the output directory
The daemon listens on 'DIR/skg-for-ai.sock' and one-shot commands with
that '--out-dir' route through it. Alternatives: a second TCP port (works
across machines; nothing here needs that), or a request file the daemon
watches (no sockets; slower and racy). The socket is the plain Linux way
and is easy to swap later, since only the transport layer of the tool
would change.

* TODO (chosen, switchable) : the ratio signal needs three content words
The fuzzy retype test asks when Dice >= 0.6 or the difflib ratio >=
0.75. On short titles the ratio fires too easily. Chosen: the ratio alone
counts only when both normalized titles have at least three content
words; Dice is unchanged. Switch: drop the guard, or lower it to two
words, by changing one constant and its unit cases.

* TODO (chosen, switchable) : the daemon log is the wake signal
No new protocol for "something changed": the daemon appends a line to
'DIR/skg-for-ai.log' for every re-render and warning. Anything that wants
to wake me (Facilitator's later machinery) tails that file. Switch: a
proper event channel, when there is a consumer for it.
