* Vocabulary
Check each word against 'docs/glossary.org' (as plan 1 leaves it) and
the code before the run ('coding-advice/names.org').
** forks, forker, forkee, fork partner
Plan 1's relation and roles: the forker forks the forkee. A fork
partner of N is a note N forks or that forks N (plan 1's coinage).
"Fork" also names the fork operation, the gesture that clones a
foreign node ('docs/glossary.org', "clone, and the fork operation");
plan 1 keeps that name as "the fork operation". Here "fork" alone is
always the relation.
** forks folder
Plan 1's one folder of the relation, 'Folder::Forks' (atom
'forksFolder', Jeff's "its forks").
** member as such: forkee as such, forker as such
A forkee as such is an occurrence standing, warranted and claiming, in
the forks folder of my node N (N forks it), as a subscribee as such
stands today ('subscriber_of_subscribee_as_such', 'server/refresh/goal.rs');
a mutual is one. A forker as such (coined) is an occurrence standing,
warranted and claiming nothing, in N's forks folder: a forker-only
member. "Member as such" means either.
** what a member shows
The content goal of a member as such M under N: M's content that N
neither contains nor hides, by the records the skgrepo restriction
shows, in M's order. For a forkee as such this is today's
'less_what_the_subscriber_integrates' ('server/refresh/goal.rs'); a
forker as such gets the same, by the same function.
** mutual
A mutual of N is a note N forks that also forks N. It stands once in
N's forks folder, as a forkee as such (it claims), heralds =bKb=.
** repeat
An occurrence of a node whose editability another occurrence in the
buffer holds ('Walk::editability_is_held', keyed by
'Walk::editability_key'). The glossary's "editability rule" uses the
word without defining it; slice 1 adds the definition.
** cut
To delete a child's headline from under a member as such and save,
whether or not it is pasted elsewhere. A cut child is one the member's
list names that the buffer no longer shows claiming under it.
** drawn folded
A folder is drawn folded when each of its members carries 'folded':
a folder's collapse lives on its members ('Walk::added_compound_folders',
'server/refresh/mod.rs').

* How the editability rule and the node budget bound the recursion
Every editable member as such is an editable occurrence carrying an
editable-view request, so it gets its own forks folder, whose members
are drawn editable in turn: the recursion now runs through forkers as
well as forkees. Two things stop it:
- Each node is editable at most once per buffer. A member whose node's
  editability is held is drawn write-protected
  ('drawn_occurrence_editability' asks 'Walk::editability_is_held'),
  and 'hold_editability' refuses a second holder at a visit; a
  write-protected occurrence gets no default folders
  ('add_default_folders' returns unless 'editable_gnid' is some). So
  the recursion meets each node of the fork graph at most once, and N
  reappears under its own members only as a repeat (every member of
  N's forks folder has N in its own forks folder).
- Each editable occurrence charges the budget once, at its visit
  ('consume_editableView_request'); once spent, members are drawn
  write-protected and draw nothing more.
A popular note with many forkers now spends its budget across them
level by level; content deeper in the view is then drawn
write-protected. That is the budget's purpose ("need not count
exactly, only stop an explosion", 'editability.rs' module comment).
