* Open questions for Jeff
Each question names the slice it affects and the answer the slice
follows ("provisional") if Jeff has not answered before that slice

** Q1. Which occurrence holds a node's editability when it is both a subscribee as such and ordinary content?
Slice 2. Today the walk decides by level order: the first occurrence
of a node whose visit consumes an editable-view request holds the
node's editability ('consume_editableView_request',
'Walk::hold_editability', 'server/refresh/editability.rs'), and every
later one is a write-protected repeat. Once subscribees are drawn
editable, a subscribee as such that is visited first will hold the
editability, and the node's ordinary occurrence deeper in the view
will be write-protected. A subscribee as such refuses title and body
edits: the save refuses the whole buffer if one differs from disk
('validate_text_claims',
'server/from_text/local_fieldintent_collection/validate_text_claims.rs').
So the user could no longer edit that node's title where it stands as
content without a C-c s e first.
Provisional: no new rule; level order decides, as for every other
occurrence, and C-c s e moves editability.
** Q2. Does a repeat member of the subscribee folder show a containers folder?
Slice 1. Agreed behavior 2 says a subscriber that is a repeat shows
nothing under it; agreed behavior 1 says a repeat subscribee "stays a
write-protected repeat", which today carries its containerward chain
('after_drawing', 'server/refresh/visit.rs'); agreed behavior 3 moves
every member's chain into a containers folder.
Provisional: a repeat member of either subscription folder shows
nothing under it, no containers folder included.
** Q3. Where does the containers folder under a member stand?
Slice 1. "Drawn folded so the member's content reads first" can mean
first among the member's children, collapsed to one line, or after
the content.
Provisional: it stands among the member's leading folders, after any
compound partner folder, before the member's content; its members
carry 'folded', so only its headline shows.
** Q4. What happens to a line under a subscriber once the subscribee integrates or hides it?
Slice 3. When N comes to contain, or hide, something its subscriber S
adds, that node is no longer something S adds, and the goal of S
drops it. Under a write-protected occurrence the refresh keeps an
unwarranted child, marked ⊥ ('mark_claims', 'server/refresh/visit.rs').
Provisional: it vanishes, when S still contains it by some record (it
is now shown in N's content, or in the folder of what N hides of S's
content); a child S no longer contains, or one the user typed there,
stays, marked ⊥, as in any write-protected set.
** Q5. Does what a subscriber adds charge the node budget?
Slice 3. Today a folder fills its members without spending any budget
('docs/glossary.org', "extent, extension, node budget"), and only an
editable occurrence charges it ('consume_editableView_request').
Provisional: no. A subscriber folder is unbounded in breadth, as every
folder is; what each member adds is one level deep.
** Q6. May C-c s e still make a member of the subscriber folder editable?
Slice 3. Today any partner-folder member can be made editable by
request ('fulfill_editableView_requests'), and then shows its whole
content, which the save writes as its contains. Jeff's words say a
subscriber member is "never editable".
Provisional: the refresh never draws one editable, and an explicit
request still makes it an ordinary editable member, with its whole
content, as today; it is then no subscriber as such.
** Q7. Members already standing in a subscribee folder
Slice 2. The agreed behavior says a member "that the folder holds"
should be drawn editable. The refresh keeps the editability an
occurrence has in the buffer ('settle_before_the_walk'); it makes a
held write-protected occurrence editable only through an editable-view
request ('consume_editableView_request').
Provisional: an editable-view request that extends the subscriber
(its first extension, or C-c s e on it) passes through its subscribee
folder to the write-protected members standing there, as it passes to
write-protected content ('extend_requested_content'); a plain rerender
leaves them as they are.

* Vocabulary
Check each word against 'docs/glossary.org' and the code before the
run ('coding-advice/names.org', "Check a plan's words before the run").
** Existing words this plan uses as the glossary defines them
"subscribee as such", "the subscribee-visibility step", "role tree",
"foldering", "the containerward chain", "chain node", "override
substitution", "perspective", "partner folder", "compound partner
folder", "write-protected set", "editability rule", "extent", "node
budget", "default folders" (the glossary's "Default presentation",
'add_default_folders'), "warranted", "claims membership", "described
node" ('described_node', 'server/refresh/ancestry.rs').
** subscription folders
Both folders of the 'subscribesTo' relation: the subscribee folder
('Folder::Subscribee', 'subscribeeFolder') and the subscriber folder
('Folder::Subscriber', 'subscriberFolder'). The plan's shorthand; the
glossary gains it in slice 1.
** repeat
An occurrence of a node whose editability another occurrence in the
buffer holds ('Walk::editability_is_held',
'server/refresh/editability.rs', which keys by
'Walk::editability_key', so an extra ID counts as its node). The
glossary's "editability rule" uses the word ("a repeat is
write-protected") without defining it; slice 1 adds the definition.
Whether an occurrence is a repeat is asked where the refresh draws it
and at its visit, so it depends on level order (see "Pitfalls").
** subscriber as such (coined here)
A write-protected occurrence standing in a subscriber folder that the
folder's relation warrants: its node S subscribes to the folder's
viewparent N. It mirrors the subscribee as such
('subscriber_of_subscribee_as_such', 'server/refresh/goal.rs'). An
editable member of a subscriber folder (made so by a request, Q6) is
no subscriber as such.
** what a subscriber adds (coined here)
The goal of a subscriber as such S under N: S's content that N
neither contains nor hides, by the records the skgrepo restriction
shows, in S's order. It mirrors what a subscribee as such shows of its
content ('less_what_the_subscriber_integrates', 'server/refresh/goal.rs'),
with the roles reversed.
** the two new compound partner folders (coined here)
Under a subscriber as such S of N:
- 'Folder::SubscribeeContentHiddenBySubscriber', atom
  'subscribeeContentHiddenBySubscriberFolder': what S hides of N's
  content, N.contains ∩ S.hidesFromSubs, in the order of S's hides
  list. Label: "☮ c contains these, but a hides them."
- 'Folder::SubscriberContentHiddenBySubscribee', atom
  'subscriberContentHiddenBySubscribeeFolder': what N hides of S's
  content, S.contains ∩ N.hidesFromSubs, in the order of N's hides
  list. Label: "☮ a contains these, but c hides them." (Jeff's example:
  "<a> contains these but <c> hides them"; the comma follows the
  existing compound labels.)
In prose: "the folder of what the subscriber hides of the subscribee"
and "the folder of what the subscribee hides of the subscriber".
** drawn folded
A folder is drawn folded when each of its members carries 'folded':
in both clients a folder's collapse lives on its members, since
'folded' on a headline hides it in its parent (the comment on
'Walk::added_compound_folders', 'server/refresh/mod.rs').

* Agreed behavior
** 1. Subscribees editable by default
*** What decides it today
A member of a subscribee folder is always drawn write-protected:
'drawn_occurrence_editability' ('server/refresh/editability.rs') lets
a drawn occurrence be editable only where
'children_are_editable_content' ('server/refresh/extent.rs') holds of
its parent, which is false for every folder, or at the buffer root.
It shows its content only after C-c s e
('fulfill_editableView_requests', 'consume_editableView_request').
'tests/hidden_from_subscriptions.rs' asserts the bare leaf
('test_hidden_within_but_none_without', "Initial view from R:
write-protected subscribee is bare leaf"; also
'test_subscribee_and_compound_partner_folders' and
'test_overlapping_hidden_within').
*** What it should be
In a content view, an occurrence the refresh draws in a subscribee
folder whose described node is an editable occurrence (the folder
reads claims: 'parent_reads_claims', 'server/refresh/ancestry.rs')
should be drawn as content is: write-protected carrying an
editable-view request, which its visit consumes, so that it becomes
editable when it is no repeat and the budget allows. Its content is
then the content a subscribee as such shows, which 'content_goal'
('server/refresh/goal.rs') already computes: what the subscriber
neither contains nor hides. Its default folders follow
('add_default_folders'): the hidden-in compound folder it already
gets, and, being an occurrence an editable-view request extends, its
own subscribee folder and (behavior 2) its subscriber folder. A member
whose node's editability another occurrence holds stays a
write-protected repeat. The editable-view request that extends the
subscriber passes through its subscribee folder to the write-protected
members standing there (Q7). A subscribee folder under a
write-protected occurrence (a C-c l s on it) keeps its members
write-protected: the folder reads no claims, and the save writes no
hides from it.
*** The save handles an editable subscribee as such: verified
Read for this plan: 'visit_occurrence' and 'visit_subscribee_folder'
('server/from_text/local_fieldintent_collection/traverse.rs') emit,
for an editable occurrence in a subscribee folder that claims, a
'SubscribeeTextClaim' (title and body must match disk,
'validate_text_claims') and, when the folder's recorder is editable,
a 'SubscribeeVisibility' signal naming the content it shows
('visible_content_members'); it is not save-eligible, so its own
title, body and contains are never written. 'resolve_visibility'
('resolve_visibility.rs') then unhides content shown claiming and
hides content not shown that the subscriber does not contain, by
visible records, for an owned subscriber only. An unchanged buffer
writes nothing: the hides it infers are those the subscriber has, and
'filter_wouldbe_noop_nodeInstructions'
('server/from_text/local_fieldintent_collection/mod.rs') drops a save
identical to the graph, so 'file_subscribee_members_written_in'
('server/from_text/file_what_is_written_in.rs') files nothing. Slice 2
asserts this with a test rather than relying on this reading.

Two consequences become reachable by default, and stay as the save
has them:
- Editing a subscribee as such's title or body refuses the whole save
  (Q1).
- An editable subscribee as such E gets its own subscribee folder,
  whose members F are subscribees as such of E; 'visit_subscribee_folder'
  passes 'subscriber_is_editable : recorder . is_editable', so F's
  visibility signal rewrites E's hides when E is owned. E itself is
  not save-eligible, so its subscribee folder's membership is never
  read (a removed F comes back at the save's refresh).
** 2. Subscribers drawn by default
On every occurrence that today gets its subscribee folder unasked (an
editable occurrence carrying an editable-view request, in a content
view: 'add_default_folders'), the subscriber folder should be drawn
too when the node has subscribers the skgrepo restriction shows
('partnerFolder_members_in_graph' with 'Folder::Subscriber'), placed
after the subscribee folder, or after the leading folders when there
is none. The folder stays write-protected ('Folder::is_editable').
Its members are drawn write-protected, as today
('drawn_occurrence_editability' never allows a member of it), and are
never drawn editable: a list shown in part cannot be saved.

A member that is no repeat is a subscriber as such S, and shows:
- what it adds, write-protected, claiming nothing, in S's order, shown
  raw (no override substitution happens under a write-protected
  occurrence: 'substitute_overriders');
- the folder of what the subscriber hides of the subscribee, when
  nonempty;
- the folder of what the subscribee hides of the subscriber, when
  nonempty;
- its containers folder, drawn folded (behavior 3).
The two compound folders are drawn folded, as the existing ones are
when the refresh adds them ('Walk::added_compound_folders'). A member
that is a repeat shows nothing under it.

Why two new folder kinds, and not the existing compound ones: a
folder's label is a fixed text per atom in the herald table
('herald_rule_table', 'server/heralds.rs'), whose ancestor letters are
fixed too ("It contains these, but c hides them." for
'hiddenInSubscribeeFolder', where the subscribee is the viewparent and
the subscriber three generations up, 'compound_folder_parties'). Under
a subscriber as such the roles stand the other way round: the
subscriber is the viewparent and the subscribee three generations up.
The hidden-in kind would print a false label there, its ancestry row
('ANC_HIDDEN_IN', 'required_ancestry', 'server/refresh/ancestry.rs')
requires a subscribee folder above it, and the save's validation
('validate_hiddenInSubscribee_folder', 'cannot_be_child_of_vognode',
'server/from_text/buffer_to_viewnodes/local.rs') allows it only under
a subscribee as such. The hidden-outside kind is editable and stands
in a subscribee folder ('Folder::is_editable', 'ANC_HIDDEN_OUTSIDE').
The goals, though, are the hidden-in kind's with the roles reversed,
and both reuse 'hidden_in_subscribee' ('server/refresh/goal.rs'): the
folder of what the subscriber hides of the subscribee is
'hidden_in_subscribee (N, S)', and the folder of what the subscribee
hides of the subscriber is 'hidden_in_subscribee (S, N)'.
** 3. Containerward chains foldered under subscription-folder members
Today every partner-folder member but a property, an acquisition or an
illegal-edit member carries its containerward chain hung directly
under it ('Folder::members_carry_containerward_chain',
'server/types/viewnode.rs'; 'after_drawing', 'server/refresh/visit.rs';
'draw_containerward_chain', 'server/refresh/chain.rs'), and a
write-protected member's vognode children are known as chain nodes by
where they stand ('holds_containerward_chain', 'stands_in_containerward_chain',
'server/types/tree/forest.rs'). Jeff's complaint: under a subscribee,
its containers looked like its children.

In a content view, a member of either subscription folder should
carry no chain directly. Where the refresh draws such a member, it
should draw instead a containers folder under it ('Folder::Container',
which omits view-ancestors, 'Folder::omits_view_ancestors'), drawn
folded, placed as Q3 says; the folder's own visit fills it, and each
of its members carries its chain as a containers folder's members
always have. A member that is a repeat where it is drawn gets none
(Q2). The vognode children of such a member are its content (for an
editable subscribee), what it adds (for a subscriber as such), or
nothing. Every other partner folder keeps today's unfoldered chain,
the four compound ones included; the diff view keeps it for every
folder.
** 4. Forks of one note meet
Fixture: N contains X1, X2, X3, X4. R and T are forks of N: each
subscribes to N and overrides N. R contains X1, X2, X3. T contains
X1, X3, Y, Z and hides X2. N hides Z. (X4 is what N gained after the
forks; Y is what T added; X2 is what T deleted; Z is something N
hides that T contains.)
*** R's view
R, the root, is editable. Its subscribee folder holds N, editable,
whose content is X4 alone (N's content less what R contains or
hides). N's subscriber folder holds R, a repeat (R's root holds R's
editability), with nothing under it, and T, a subscriber as such,
showing Y; the folder of what T hides of N, holding X2; and the folder
of what N hides of T, holding Z.
*** N's view
N, the root, is editable, with X1 to X4. Its subscriber folder holds
R, which adds nothing and hides nothing of N's (so nothing stands
under it but its containers folder, where R has containers), and T,
as above.
*** Saves
Saving R's view unchanged writes nothing and files nothing. Deleting
X4 under N in R's view and saving makes R hide X4 (the
subscribee-visibility step), and the redrawn view shows X4 in N's
hidden-in folder.
** 5. Out of scope
As "Scope" says. In particular nothing changes in a buffer of the
diff view, and no folder is drawn unasked in the search buffer.
** 6. What stays as it is, and what changes, among the defaults a user sees
*** Kept
- Override substitution ('substitute_overriders',
  'is_overridden_and_shown_raw', 'server/refresh/visit.rs'): a
  member of a partner folder is shown raw; the content of a subscribee
  as such is substituted, as today; a view root is raw, and its
  children too where it is overridden.
- The hidden-outside folder: drawn in a subscribee folder when its
  subscriber hides something outside every subscribee, last there,
  its members folded ('add_hidden_outside_folder',
  'keep_hidden_outside_folder_last').
- The hidden-in folder under an editable subscribee as such, its
  members folded.
- A fresh content view's containers folder on each root, unfolded,
  first ('stub_of_fresh_content_view', 'server/update_buffer.rs').
- The fold state of everything already in a buffer: the refresh keeps
  it, and only what it draws is drawn folded.
- The diff view: no default folders; every folder's members carry
  their chain directly; editability by the pruned change view's U.
- The search buffer: nothing drawn unasked.
- Other partner folders (containers, mentioners, override, hider,
  hidden, the four compound ones): their members carry the chain
  directly.
- The editability rule: one editable occurrence per node per buffer;
  the node budget ('initial_node_limit').
- C-c s e, C-c s w, C-c s W, and every C-c l command.
- What the save does with each folder: the subscribee folder's
  membership read for an editable, save-eligible subscriber; every
  other new folder write-protected and save-inert.
*** Changed
- A member of a subscribee folder under an editable subscriber is
  editable by default (behavior 1); it charges the node budget, so a
  view with many subscriptions spends the budget sooner.
- Every editable occurrence carrying an editable-view request shows
  its nonempty subscriber folder by default (behavior 2).
- Members of both subscription folders carry their chain in a folded
  containers folder (behavior 3), in content views.
- Two new compound partner folders with their labels.

* How the editability rule and the node budget bound the recursion
An editable subscribee as such is an editable occurrence carrying an
editable-view request, so it gets its own default folders: its
subscribee folder, whose members are drawn editable in turn, and its
subscriber folder. Three things stop the recursion:
- Each node is editable at most once per buffer: a member whose
  node's editability is held is drawn write-protected
  ('drawn_occurrence_editability' asks 'Walk::editability_is_held'),
  and at its visit 'hold_editability' refuses a second holder; a
  write-protected occurrence gets no default folders
  ('add_default_folders' returns unless 'editable_gnid' is some). So
  the subscription recursion visits each node reachable by
  subscription at most once, and a cycle of subscriptions ends at its
  first repeat.
- Each editable occurrence charges the node budget once, at its visit
  ('consume_editableView_request'); once it is spent, drawn members
  stay write-protected and draw nothing further.
- A subscriber as such is write-protected: it gets no default
  folders, and what it adds is drawn write-protected, so nothing
  recurses through a subscriber folder. Its breadth is bounded by the
  graph alone, as any folder's is (Q5).

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
** Live servers and data
Ports 1730 and 1761 are Jeff's live servers, running
'target/release/skg' with 'data/jeff/skgconfig.toml' and
'data/claude/skgconfig.toml', without hot reload. Never start, stop
or connect to them, never pass those ports or configs to anything, and
never build the release profile. Tests use 'target/debug' and pick
their own ports; isolated instances only ('.claude/skills/verify/SKILL.md').
Every commit must leave the tree green: a restart rebuilds from it.
** The diff view shares every function this plan touches
'drawn_occurrence_editability' has a diff-view arm that makes a node
of U editable wherever its parent may hold editable members
('in_the_diffViews_u'). Widening "may be editable" to subscribee
folders without gating it on 'RefreshMode::ContentView' would make
diff-view subscribee folders draw editable members. Every rule of
this plan asks 'walk . is_diff_view ()' (or the mode) and does nothing
there. Diff-view subscribee and subscriber folders appear in
'tests/refresh/diff_view.rs' and 'tests/pruned_change_view/'.
** Every reader of the chain predicates
'Folder::members_carry_containerward_chain' is read by
'holds_containerward_chain' ('server/types/tree/forest.rs') and by
'after_drawing' ('server/refresh/visit.rs'), and named in comments
only in 'server/refresh/chain.rs'. 'holds_containerward_chain' is read
by 'stands_in_containerward_chain' (same file) and by 'is_chain_parent'
('server/refresh/extent.rs'), whose readers are 'goal_of_parent'
('server/refresh/goal.rs', which picks 'chain_goal' or 'content_goal')
and 'tag_children' ('server/refresh/diff_view.rs', diff view only).
'stands_in_containerward_chain' is read by 'leaves_named_at' and the
check near line 685 of 'server/diff_view/processed_requests.rs' (diff
view only), by 'restoreRel_stands_where_it_may'
('server/from_text/buffer_to_viewnodes/local.rs'; 'restoreRel' is a
diff-view request, so it takes the diff view's answer), and by
'tests/refresh/chain.rs'. A content-view-only change to which members
carry the chain must reach every one of these, by a parameter saying
whether the view is the diff view; none of them may be left asking a
mode-blind version.
** A member's children change meaning with the chain
Today the vognode children of a write-protected member of a
subscription folder are chain nodes, and its goal is 'chain_goal'.
Once those members carry no chain in a content view, the same
children are content: 'goal_of_parent' gives 'content_goal', the
extent of a write-protected occurrence is none ('extent_of_parent'),
and the claims rung keeps an unwarranted child of a write-protected
occurrence ('mark_claims'). A buffer drawn before this plan therefore
keeps its old chain nodes under such members, marked ⊥; under a
member that is (or is made) editable they claim nothing and so show
the "detach" herald as pending detachments, which the subscribee as
such's save ignores ('visit_occurrence' does not emit 'SetContains'
for it). No migration ('coding-advice/no-legacy-support.org'):
reopening a view redraws it. Say so in the docs' "what changes" note
of slice 1, and in progress.org.
** The refresh never makes a held write-protected occurrence editable
'settle_before_the_walk' records the editable occurrences the buffer
holds; a write-protected one becomes editable only through an
editable-view request ('consume_editableView_request'). So a view
drawn before slice 2 keeps its write-protected subscribees until the
subscriber is extended (Q7). Tests that rerender an old buffer text
expect that.
** Repeats depend on level order
The walk visits parents in level order ('walk_in_level_order',
'server/refresh/mod.rs'), and folders stand before content among a
node's children. Whether a member is a repeat is asked when it is
drawn and at its visit, so an occurrence drawn editable later in the
walk does not make an earlier-visited member a repeat in the same
refresh. A subscriber as such that drew what it adds before its node
became editable elsewhere keeps those children at the next refresh,
where it is a repeat: the extent draws nothing new, and the claims
rung removes nothing warranted. This is stable (a fixpoint), not a
defect; do not add a rule removing them.
** The editable-view request is consumed per visit
'consume_editableView_request' and 'drop_editableView_request' bracket
a visit; 'extend_requested_content' runs at the visit of the parent
carrying the request, before the parent's default folders are added
('visit_parent' order). A subscribee folder that 'add_default_folders'
adds at that visit is empty then; its members are drawn at the
folder's own visit, by 'reinstate' through 'draw_item' and
'drawn_occurrence_editability', which is where a drawn member gets its
request. Passing the request through a subscribee folder that already
stands (Q7) belongs in 'extend_requested_content'.
** 'add_default_folders' returns early for write-protected occurrences
It reads 'editable_gnid' first. The folders under a subscriber as
