* Question 1: terms coined or reused
** 1. "fork" and "its forks" mean three different things, and the glossary contradicts itself
The glossary defines "a *fork* of a note N" as "a note that forks N",
which is a forker ('docs/glossary.org:943'; also
'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/mirrors.rs:4', 'docs/mirrors.org:86'). By that
definition N's forks are N's forkers. But "hides from its forks" uses
"its forks" for the hider's forkees:
- 'docs/glossary.org:238-240' ('=hides= means "hides from its forks":
  the hider keeps the hidden node out of what the hider shows of its
  forkees')
- 'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/relation_accessors.rs:35'
- the folder labels 'server/heralds.rs:470' ("It hides these from its
  forks.") and 'server/heralds.rs:472' ("These hide it from their forks.")
- 'docs/heralds.org:83-84', 'docs/data-model_friendly.org:58',
  'tools/skg-for-ai/README.org' ("'H' hides (from its forks)")
- 'server/nodeMerge/nodeMergeInstructionTriple.rs:232'
  ('gnids_shown_through_forks', meaning through forkees)
- 'tests/hidden_nodes.rs:107,179,348,595,703', 'tests/merge/merge_nodes.rs:159'

A third reading takes "its forks" to mean fork partners: the diff
view's changed-folder label "its forks changed"
('shared/herald-styles.json:111', 'docs/heralds.org:306',
'docs/glossary.org:1600'), and Jeff's own name for the folder
('TODO/every-mirror/1_plan.org', Q8). A fourth uses "a fork" for the
fork operation ('docs/forks.org:3' bolds "*fork*" as the gesture;
'docs/forks.org:28' "two levels per fork"; see finding 12). The
labels a user reads are where this does the most harm: under the
glossary's definition, "It hides these from its forks" says the
opposite of what the code does in plan 1.
- Fix: add a glossary entry for the bare word "fork", keep "a fork of
  N = a note that forks N", never write a bare "its forks", and
  relabel the two hide folders "It hides these from what it forks." /
  "These hide it from what they fork." (or "from its fork partners"
  once plan 2 widens hides to both directions).

** 2. User docs say "a fork" stands in a node's place; the code can also show the node's forkee or a sibling
The mirror shown can be a forkee of N ('Side::In' in
'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/mirrors.rs:239-251'). It can also be a
sibling: another fork of what N forks (rule 4 of "Which mirror
stands"). The foreign owner's half of 'jeffs_example_from_both_perspectives'
('tests/dbs/in_rust_graph/mirrors.rs:96') shows n in f's place, where f
forks n. Yet these places say a fork stands in:
- 'docs/COMMANDS.org:1323-1324' and 'docs/COMMANDS-nvim.org:1296-1297'
  ("someone has forked it, a view may show one fork in its place")
- 'tools/skg-for-ai/README.org:195-198' ("the view may show the fork in
  its place", "Whose forks count is the perspective")
- 'docs/sharing-model.org:48' ("It does not show a fork in the root's
  place")
- Fix: say "one of its mirrors" (and "whose skgrepo list ranks the
  mirrors"), as 'docs/heralds.org:169' already does.

** 3. The compound folders' names, label and parties lie in plan 1
- 'hiddenOutsideOfForkPartnersFolder' says, and its label says, "b hides
  these, but nothing it forks or that forks it contains them."
  ('server/heralds.rs:466'). Its membership reads only what the forkees
  contain: 'hidden_outside_of_forkees' at 'server/refresh/goal.rs:580',
  and the glossary admits it at 'docs/glossary.org:516-520'. So a hide
  that a forker contains is listed there, and the label is false for
  it until plan 2.
- 'hiddenInForkPartnerFolder' is drawn only under a forkee as such
  ('server/refresh/goal.rs:543-546'). "Fork partner" in its name
  promises plan 2's behaviour.
- 'CompoundFolderParties::forker' ('server/refresh/ancestry.rs:148', doc
  at ':137-145') and the style keys 'hiddenInForkPartnerFolder_forker' and
  'hiddenOutsideOfForkPartnersFolder_forker' ('shared/herald-styles.json:80-81',
  'docs/heralds.org:194-195') name the note whose hides the folder
  shows. That note is a hider. In the hidden-outside folder it need not
  fork anything: a note with only forkers, hiding one of them, has the
  folder too ('docs/glossary.org:514-516').
- Fix: rename the field and the two style keys 'hider'. Until plan 2
  lands, either word the hidden-outside label by what it reads
  ("nothing it forks contains them") or record the lie as a
  'TODO/problems.org' entry.

** 4. 'side_of_mirror_shown' gives one direction two opposite names
'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/mirrors.rs:233-238' says 'Side::Out' is
when 'shown' reaches 'original' "by forker steps only (it forks a note
that ... forks 'original')". The code decides that with
'reaches_by_forkee_steps (shown, original)'
('server/dbs/in_rust_graph/mirrors.rs:247, 253-255'), whose doc calls
the same forker-to-forkee walk "forkee steps". "Forker steps" and
"forkee steps" are coined in the same module for one direction. The
glossary already has the word: "forkeeward" ('docs/glossary.org:618-620').
- Fix: rename the function 'reaches_forkeeward' and make the docstring
  say "'Side::Out' when 'shown' reaches 'original' forkeeward, 'Side::In'
  when 'original' reaches 'shown' forkeeward".

** 5. "fork rules" is defined in no glossary entry, and covers rules that are not about forks
- The term is used as a term in 'docs/api-and-formats.org:835' ("one per
  violation of the fork rules"), in the startup warnings
  ('fork_rule_warnings', 'server/main.rs:157-183', 'server/dbs/init.rs:44-79')
  and across 'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/fork_rules.rs'. No glossary entry
  defines it.
- 'ForkRuleViolation' ('server/dbs/in_rust_graph/fork_rules.rs:37-55')
  includes 'UnknownSkgrepo', 'SelfContains' and 'SelfHides'. None of
  these is a rule about forks: they are rules 4 and 5 of "Hard
  validation constraints" ('docs/data-model_technical.org:70-72').
- 'server/dbs/in_rust_graph/fork_rules.rs:7-9' says "a note it forks is
  the same note as it". Skg's word for that is mirror, not "same note".
  Mirrors are distinct notes that may stand in each other's places.
- Fix: add a glossary entry ("the fork rules: rules 4 to 6 of the hard
  validation constraints") or rename the module 'owned_note_rules'.
  Reword the sentence as "a note it forks is its mirror, which may
  stand in its place".

** 7. "honest carrier" reuses the glossary's "carrier" in another sense

or an extra ID ('docs/glossary.org:1809-1810'). This plan carried
forward and widened a second sense: the headline that bears an
'(inPlaceOf N)' part.
- 'docs/api-and-formats.org:2066' ("Any mirror is an honest carrier")
- 'server/types/viewnode.rs:390' ("the carrier must be a mirror of N")
- 'server/from_text/buffer_to_viewnodes/validate_tree.rs:180,209'
- 'server/types/errors.rs:144,357'
- the "honest carrier" lines in 'tests/mirror_substitution.rs'
- Fix: say "the headline bearing the part" (variable 'bearer') and
  keep "carrier" for IDs.
