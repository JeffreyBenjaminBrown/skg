* How this was run
On 2026-11-16, after slice 6 (HEAD '1b6a4b89'), with 'target/debug/skg'
('cargo build --bin skg', already current) and isolated servers on
ports 24901 to 24904, each on scratch data under

running. The live servers on 1730 and 1761 were never touched. Every
scratch server was stopped by its PID and its port confirmed free
afterwards; the scratch data has been deleted. Each server was driven

  ('view_ID.full.org', 'view_ID.compact.org'), the buffer sent
  ('sent.full.org') and the view the save drew ('saved.full.org',

- Not matched, a real bug: a forkee as such nested under an editable,
  owned member as such (V forks W, W forks U) makes the save read U's

  rendered, and any edit of W there (its title, a cut from U) is

** Received
- The preview with no baseline: "Hides 'u1' from 'W', as content of
  'U'." (R's view: "Hides 'X1' / 'X3' / 'Y' from 'N', as content of
  'T' ...").
- Unchanged, with its baseline: no file written, but "Left as the
  graph has it: W (W), which changed after this buffer was rendered and

- W retitled: "NOTHING WAS SAVED ... A node this save would write

** Verdict
NOT MATCHED, a bug. W is an owned member as such, so the traversal
emits its title and body (behavior 2, "Title and body"), which gives W
a Save intent whose 'contains' is unspecified.
'forker_contains_after_save' ('lower.rs') returns 'intent . contains .
or_default ()' for any Save intent, so W's contains "after the save" is
empty, and the forkee-visibility step ('resolve_visibility.rs', its
'forker_contains' and 'inferred_hides') reads every child of U that W
contains, and so does not show under U, as cut from U. Its neighbor
'forker_forks_after_save' already guards with 'is_unspecified'. Today
the stale check masks it: the baseline parse makes the same mistake, so
W is "stale" and left out. Effects: a false warning on every save of
such a view, no edit of W possible there (title, body, a cut from its
forkee, a typed child), and the wrong hides written by any save whose
baseline does not show W (a buffer with no copy of W in its baseline).
