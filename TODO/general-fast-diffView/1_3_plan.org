* Vocabulary
"Headline record": a headline line of a view's text together with
the body lines after it, up to the next headline line or the end.
A headline line is a line matching '^\*+ ' (org's rule; a body line
that would match is escaped by 'encode_body_for_view',
'server/body_view_escape.rs'). The text before the first headline
line is the preamble.

"The vnid of a headline line": the X of a line beginning
'^\*+ (skg (vnid X)', the renderer's form ('viewnode_to_string_where_it_stands',
'server/org_to_text.rs:171'). A headline line not of that form
has no vnid.

"Fold and focus atoms": the bare atoms 'focused', 'folded' and
'bodyFolded' at depth 1 of a headline's '(skg ...)' form (the
server parses them anywhere there, 'server/serve/parse_metadata_sexp.rs:626-631';
it renders them right after '(vnid X)', 'foldAndFocus_atoms',
'server/org_to_text.rs:214').

"The stripped form" of a headline line: the line with each fold and
focus atom at depth 1 of its '(skg ...)' form removed together with
the one space before it. Depth counts parentheses outside string
literals ('"..."' with backslash escapes, as 'metadata_value_atom'
writes them, 'server/org_to_text.rs:18-24'). A line without a
'(skg ...)' form is its own stripped form. The stripped form of a
text strips every headline line and leaves body lines and the
preamble alone. This is one rule implemented three times (Rust,
elisp, Lua); slice 1 writes the shared cases file all three test
against.

"The delta": the envelope entry '(changed-headlines (V1 V2 ...))',
the vnids of the headline records whose stripped headline lines
differ, in the new text's order. Its absence means "whole": the
client redraws as today. Present and empty, '(changed-headlines
())', it means no headline changed.

"The reference text": what the server compares the new text with.
For the save-result, the saved buffer text of the request
('envelope . saved_buffer'). For a streamed view, the rendering of
the forest the connection holds for that view before the refresh,
which reproduces the text last sent for it ('report_envelope.org',
section 2).

"Patch": the client's way of applying a delta: replace each listed
headline line by the corresponding line of the new text's stripped
form, re-herald what those lines' heralds depend on, then check the
buffer against the new text's stripped form, falling back to the
whole redraw on any mismatch.

* Agreed behavior
** The server computes the delta
For a save-result and for each streamed view, the server will
compute the delta from the reference text and the new text:
- Split both into a preamble and headline records.
- The delta is "whole" (the entry absent) when the preambles
  differ, when the record counts differ, when some headline line
  of either text has no vnid, when the vnid sequences differ, or
  when some record's body lines differ.
- Otherwise it lists, in the new text's order, each vnid whose
  stripped headline lines differ. It may be empty.
It never changes the text sent: the 'content' entry stays the whole
new text, fold and focus atoms included.

** The envelope
The save-result gains '(changed-headlines (V...))' after its
existing entries, when the delta is not "whole". A collateral-view
or rerender-view gains it after its 'content' entry (an entry
between 'view-id' and 'content' would break
'tests/pruned_change_view/stage_and_commit.rs:252'). vnids print as
bare symbols; none reads as a number ('server/types/views_state.rs:384-398').
The confirmations, error replies and withheld saves carry none.

** The clients patch, check, and fall back
Given a delta, a client will:
1. decline to patch (whole redraw) if a precondition fails: in
   Emacs, 'org-fold-core-style' must be 'overlays' (with
   'text-properties' a replaced headline under a folded body hides
   itself, 'report_envelope.org' section 3); in either client, a
   streamed view's buffer with unsaved edits keeps today's
   treatment;
2. compute the stripped form of the new text;
3. replace each listed headline line, located by its vnid, by that
   vnid's line in the stripped form; a vnid it cannot locate makes
   it fall back;
4. re-herald each replaced line's folder ancestors (the existing
   hooks do) and its children and grandchildren (no hook does; a
   pending-detachment herald reads its parent and grandparent,
   'skg--pending-detachment-at-point-p', 'elisp/skg-metadata.el:1028');
5. compare the whole buffer text with the stripped form; on any
   difference, redraw the whole buffer from the new text as today.
On success the client leaves folds and point as they are, skips the
fold restore and the atom removal, marks the buffer unmodified,
captures the baseline (it equals the stripped form) and refreshes
the link annotations as today. Correctness never rests on the
delta: whatever the client shows after a patch equals what a whole
redraw would show, folds and point aside.

** The pre-send snapshot edits nothing (Emacs)
'skg--buffer-snapshot-with-save-metadata' ('elisp/skg-request-save.el:214')
will return the same string it returns today, byte for byte, but
build it without editing the buffer: it will read the fold and
focus state from the live buffer, copy the text into a temporary
buffer with no change hooks, add the atoms there with the existing
editing functions, and return that text. The live buffer then sees
no edit, no unfold and no refold before a save.

** A readiness save redraws the other buffers of the diff view
A save whose buffer carries a readiness request will add to its
collateral views every other open view of the connection that is a
buffer of the diff view ('diffView_buffer_kind' is Some) and that
the save envelope does not mark dirty. Their refresh recomputes
their '(ready ...)' facts; with the delta, each client patches the
few lines that changed.

** What stays as it is
- The text the server renders, and what a whole redraw does, fold
  restore and point placement included.
- Every response without a delta.
- What a dirty view gets: never redrawn by a save.
- Neovim's pre-send snapshot.
- The save lock, the readiness summary, the link annotations.

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
** Jeff's live server and client run from this tree
His server is 'target/release/skg', started by
'bash/start-servers.sh' without '--hot-reload', so edits under
'server/' do not reach it until he restarts it. A restart rebuilds
from whatever the tree holds, so every slice must leave the tree
green; the delta is additive (absent means "whole"), so any mix of
old and new client and server works. Never build the release
profile; tests use 'target/debug'. Never start or stop anything
with 'data/skgconfig.toml'; isolated instances only
('.claude/skills/verify/SKILL.md').
** Emacs reads '()' as nil
'(cadr (assoc 'changed-headlines response))' is nil both for an
empty delta and for none. Test presence with 'assq', as the
readiness summary does ('skg-handle-save-sexp',
'elisp/skg-request-save.el:1088').
** Unknown envelope keys
Every client parser ignores unknown keys (Emacs, Neovim
'payload.field', the AI tool's 'response_field'), but three Rust
tests pin whole envelopes: 'tests/unit/serve_util.rs:47-59'
(spared if the field is pushed in 'SaveResponse::to_sexp_string',
'server/serve/handlers/save_buffer.rs:220', not in
'buffer_response_entries'), 'tests/unit/serve_util.rs:73-82'
('format_single_view_sexp' gains a parameter) and the entry order
of 'tests/pruned_change_view/stage_and_commit.rs:252'.
** The saved buffer's text is not passed to the rerender today
'update_views_after_save' ('server/rerender_pass.rs:131') does not
receive the request's buffer text; the caller
('update_from_and_rerender_buffer_with_approvals',
'server/serve/handlers/save_buffer.rs', the call near :1131) has
it as 'org_buffer_text'. The held forest and the parsed forest are
not the reference: the first predates the user's edits, the second
lacks derived facts and new vnids.
** A withheld save registers a forest that matches no sent text
'withhold_save_rerender' ('server/rerender_pass.rs:238') registers
the parsed saved view without sending it. A later rerender-view's
delta is then computed against the wrong reference; the client's
check falls back. Acceptable; do not special-case it.
** The client reprints lines it edits
'skg-edit-metadata-at-point' and Neovim's 'edit_metadata_at_line'
reprint the whole line ('report_envelope.org' section 5); a reprint
can differ from the server's form (an empty title gains a trailing
space). The patch must insert the stripped line as text, never
through those functions, and the check catches the rest by falling
back.
** Folds
Emacs: with 'overlays', a one-line replacement keeps every fold
around and below it ('report_envelope.org' section 3, measured).
Neovim: whether a closed expr fold survives a one-line
'nvim_buf_set_lines' is unverified; slice 3 finds out before
relying on it.
** The pre-send snapshot runs with the save lock suspended
It unlocks and relocks around its edits
('elisp/skg-request-save.el:214-242'). Once it edits nothing, it
need not unlock; keep 'skg--inhibit-dirty-view-confirmation' only
where an edit remains.
