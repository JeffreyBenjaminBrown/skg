* One name, several things
For each of these, either qualify every use, as the "merge" rule does,
or rename the minority sense.
** fold
Org folding ('Viewnode.folded', 'body_folded', 'skg-add-folded-markers')
vs the telescope fold (combining sections into a node) and unfold
(splitting it on save). Both are central, and both appear in
'viewnode.rs'-adjacent code. Org owns "fold"; it is the editor's word.
Recommendation: rename the telescope sense. Candidates: *compose* /
*decompose*, or *stack* / *unstack* (sections stack in privacy order).
"visible fold" / "full fold" become "visible composition" / "full
composition". Identifier tier ('telescope/fold.rs', 'FoldWarning') plus
docs.

** hide

rendering omits); "hidden in its parent" (the comment on

'hidden_body' -> 'omitted_body' (the herald B is display-only, so the

or "withheld" for repo-set restriction.
