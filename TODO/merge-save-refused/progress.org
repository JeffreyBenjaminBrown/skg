*** Deviations from the plan's how
**** The merge pairs carry two pids
'from_text.rs' resolves each pair's acquiree, as written (perhaps
an extra ID), to its pid through 'pid_else_raw_id' before the
pair enters 'SavePlan', so every consumer compares pids (the
plan's "IDs are compared as pids"), and 'candidates_from_disk',
which has no graph, gets a pid it can find a telescope for. For
the same reason the triple's acquiree delete now carries the
acquiree's pid ('nodeMerge_from_acquirer_and_acquiree').
**** The older as-written deletion check is gone
'validate_nodeMerge_pair' ('server/nodeMerge/validate_nodeMerge.rs')
refused "Acquirer/Acquiree ... cannot be marked for deletion" as an
'Other' string, comparing IDs as written, and ran first, so the
plan's test of the new error could not see it.
'validate_no_simultaneous_delete_and_nodeMerge'
('server/from_text/validate.rs') replaces it, by pid.
**** The new variant also carries the title
'CannotDeleteAndMergeSimultaneously { pid, title, role }', the role a
new 'NodeMergeRole' ('server/types/errors.rs'), because the org form
shows the title and the formatter has no graph. It names no
headlines.
**** The coherence assertion counts only the last instruction per ID
A save that edits the acquiree and merges it saves it in phase one
and deletes it in phase two; the 'debug_assert!' after the swap-in
fired. The same was already reachable through a Hoist repair of an
acquiree. Fixed in its own commit.
**** The foreign merge refusal, on pairs
Each foreign member of a pair yields 'ModifiedForeignNode (pid,
home)', which is what the triples' foreign acquirer and acquiree
delete yielded. A foreign acquiree no longer also yields a
'CreatedForeignNode' for the text preserver, whose fresh pid meant
nothing to the user. 'tests/multi_repo_errors.rs' counts only the
former, and passes unchanged.
**** Other entry points take pairs too
'update_graph_including_nodeMerges' and its under-the-gate form take
pairs and build the triples through 'prepare_save_under_mutation_gate'.
'merge_nodes', which applies triples it is given, keeps them; its
Hoist gate derives the pairs from them. 'prepare_save_under_mutation_gate',
'PreparedSave' and its new 'nodeMerges ()' are public, so the
adapted triple test in 'tests/merge/' can reach them.
**** One more doc paragraph
The stale-buffer paragraph of 'docs/api-and-formats.org' said the
check covers "the two nodes of each nodeMerge"; it no longer does,
so nodeMerges joined its list of derived writes that are not
checked. Slice 3 rewrites that paragraph anyway.
