* Refactoring for dev: 'server/serve.rs' and 'server/serve/'
  The territory: 'server/serve.rs' and everything under 'server/serve/'
  (the connection loop, 'protocol.rs', 'util.rs', 'rerender_stream.rs',
  'parse_metadata_sexp.rs' and the handlers). The wire protocol must not
  change by a byte. Every refactor below was checked by 'bash/nextest.sh'
  (the whole Rust suite, including 'tests/unit/serve_util.rs') and the
  whole suite before merge.
* Done
** One function dispatches a request to its handler
   'dispatch_request' ('server/serve.rs') holds the match on request
   type; 'handle_emacs' only reads requests and, between them, tells the
   client what the repo watcher reloaded. Its stale doc comment (naming
   two handlers that do not exist) is gone.
** The shutdown handler exits the process itself
   'cleanup_and_shutdown' cleaned nothing up and ignored its env; its two
   lines joined 'handle_shutdown_request'.
** The save handler sends its answer and its refusals through one function each
   'send_save_response' ('handlers/save_buffer.rs') holds the order in
   which a finished save's answer is chosen; 'send_save_refusal' replaced
   four copies of the refused save-result. A doc comment that sat above
   the wrong function moved to 'fork_approved_from_request'.
** A response's (KEY "VALUE") entry is built by one function
   'string_atom_sexp' and 'string_entry_sexp' ('server/serve/util.rs')
   replaced three private 'atom'/'pair' pairs (text_release, telescope_hoist,
   receipt), two closures in save_buffer and util's own hand-built entries.
** A save request's fields are read in 'save_buffer/request.rs'
   The envelope parser and the request-line readers moved unchanged
   out of 'handlers/save_buffer.rs' (now 1145 lines). The request
   types ('SaveRequestEnvelope' and kin) stayed in 'save_buffer.rs',
   where 'docs/glossary.org' cites them. 'tests/save_request_envelope.rs'
   imports the parser from 'save_buffer::request'.
** A save request's '(KEY . "true")' flags are read by one function
   'request_field_is_true' ('save_buffer/request.rs').
** A diff of every skgrepo is computed in 'server/git_ops/diff.rs'
   'compute_diff_for_every_skgrepo' left the save handler; only
   'titles_by_gnids.rs' calls it.
* Candidates left, best first
** Done: split 'update_from_and_rerender_buffer_with_approvals_or_preview'
   Six of its steps are functions of their own, in place and in order;
   'server-longest-functions.org' says which and what is left of it.
** 'parse_metadata_sexp.rs' (1106 lines)
   Three seams: the metadata types (to line ~245), the builder
   'viewnode_from_metadata' (~245-465), and the parser (the rest).
   'docs/api-and-formats.org' and 'coding-advice/architecture.org' say
   the grammar and the parser live in this file, so the parser should
   stay; moving the types or the builder out touches the imports of
   about eight test files ('rg parse_metadata_sexp:: tests'). Safe,
   mechanical, moderate gain.
** About fifteen 'sexp::parse (request)' walks across the handlers
   Each reads one field its own way ('fork_skgrepos_from_request',
   'strandeds_review_from_request' in 'save_buffer/request.rs' walk the
   list for a key's whole form). A 'form_from_request (key, request)'
   in 'util.rs' could serve them; the refusal texts must stay word for
   word. Small gain.
** 'send_import_response' ('handlers/import_md_and_org.rs', ~160 lines)
   and 'handle_text_search_request' (~145 lines) are the next longest;
   not read.
