* Refactoring for dev: 'server/types/' and 'server/dbs/'
  The territory: everything under 'server/types/' and 'server/dbs/'.
  A change there forces call-site edits across 'server/'. Every
  refactor below was checked by 'bash/nextest.sh' (the whole Rust
  suite, about fifteen seconds warm) and the whole suite before merge.
* Done
** A 'NodeInstruction' says its gnid, saved graphnode and deleted gnid
   'gnid', 'saved_graphnode' and 'deleted_gnid' ('server/types/save.rs')
   replace the Save/Delete matches written out in a dozen files, and
   two private copies of 'gnid' ('definition_pid', 'instruction_gnid').
** An ordinary save without nodeMerges returns no search index
   'update_graph_minus_nodeMerges' and its '_with_hoist_approval' form
   return '()', not an always-'None' 'Option<TantivyIndex>'.
** A graphnode in the in-Rust graph converts to a 'Graphnode' by 'From'
   'impl From<&GraphnodeInRust> for Graphnode' ('server/types/nodes/rust.rs')
   replaces three copies of the projection ('graphnode_from_graphnodeInRust',
   'complete_from_rust', the body of 'graphnode_from_graph').
** A graph lookup that cannot fail returns an 'Option'
   'opt_graphnode_by_gnid' (always 'Ok', config ignored) and
   'graphnode_graphFirst_by_pid_and_skgrepo' (skgrepo and config
   ignored, a copy of 'graphnode_by_gnid') are gone; their callers use
   'graphnode_from_graph' or 'graphnode_by_gnid' ('server/dbs/node_lookup.rs').
** A config names its missing skgrepo through one method
   'SkgConfig::skgrepo_or_err' ('server/types/misc.rs') holds the
   message "Repo '...' not found in config", once written at eight sites.
* Candidates left, best first
** 'graphnode_by_gnid' takes a config it ignores
   'server/dbs/node_lookup.rs'. Dropping '_config' is mechanical and
   safe (compile-checked), but four of its eleven callers are tests
   ('tests/partner_folder_matrix.rs', 'tests/shared/view_and_save_helpers.rs'),
   which then change by more than a rename; it needs the coordinator's

** Split 'server/types/misc.rs' (993 lines) along its seams
   It holds 'ID', 'MSV', 'RelPartner', 'SkgrepoName', 'Skgrepo',
   'SkgConfig' (two impl blocks, at ~354 and ~605) and 'TantivyIndex'.
   An agent looking for the config type would not guess "misc". A
   'config.rs' holding 'SkgConfig', 'Skgrepo' and the foreign-order
   types is the clear seam. Safe (moves only), but about seventy files
   import 'SkgConfig' from 'types::misc', so the diff is wide; a
   re-export would be the legacy shim the rules forbid.

   impls from line ~773 ('Folder' alone ~190 lines of impl). The diff
   facts types at its head ('ChangeFacts', 'DiffChange', 'DiffObject',
   'ProcessedRequest') could be their own file. Not surveyed closely.
