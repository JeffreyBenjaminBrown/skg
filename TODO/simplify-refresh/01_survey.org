** The question
Given the old buffer, parsed back into a viewforest, and the new
state of the graph, how does the server build the new buffer?
This report describes that construction as the code stands, in
present tense. Where it describes a prior state, or proposes
something, it says so.
** What it leaves out
It sidesteps how a view is first drawn and how save instructions
are extracted from a buffer, with one exception that the reader
needs: the first drawing of a view runs the same completion over
a stub forest, so "how a saved view is completed" and "how a view
is drawn" are one mechanism with two entrances. The search buffer
is included: it is the one buffer the server draws without the
walk, and a save of it runs the walk like any other.

** The glossary comes first

* The short version
A completion is one breadth-first walk over the viewforest. Each
viewnode gets one visit, and a visit may change only that
viewnode and its children. Children the visit creates are visited
later in the same walk. At a visit, the viewnode's kind picks a
rule: an ordinary node's visit settles its editability and brings
its content children in line with the graph; a folder's visit
brings its members in line; a property folder's visit refreshes
its entries. Every such rule has the same shape: compute a goal
list from the graph, find the children that stand for goal
entries, keep those, create the missing ones, and convert the
rest. Nothing is ever detached during the walk; a child the graph
no longer warrants becomes a non-member if it holds anything,
else a tombstone, and a prune at the end removes the childless
tombstones and empty write-protected folders. After the prune, a
tail marks roots, corrects memberships, computes statistics,
applies the skgrepo restriction, assigns viewnode IDs and
renders. The diff view runs the same walk in diff-view mode,
which adds a step at the end of each ordinary node's visit and
makes the folders read the union of HEAD and the worktree. The
search buffer is the exception to the first sentence: the server
draws it by hand, in two phases, and the walk first touches it
when the user saves it.
** The rules, as the code states them
Each of these is enforced somewhere in the code. The later
sections say where; the section "Where the boundaries blur" says
where one of them is enforced more than once.
*** One walk, level order, one visit per viewnode
'complete_nodes_in_level_order' ('complete.rs:246'). A visit
reads its ancestors and the graph, never its siblings or
descendants.
*** A visit dispatches on the viewnode's kind
'dispatch_node_update' ('complete.rs:267'). A folder also checks
its ancestry first and dies if an ancestor is of the wrong kind.
*** Goal, managed children, key, keep, create, convert
Every reconciler: the graph gives a goal list; a selector says
which children the reconciler manages; a key matches a managed
child to a goal entry; a matched child is kept (adopted), a
missing one created, and a stale or duplicate one converted.
*** Nothing is detached; convert, then prune
'convert_unwarranted_child' ('server/update_buffer/util.rs:197'):
an unrestricted vognode holding anything is retained as a
non-member; everything else becomes a tombstone. The prune
('prune_self_deletable_when_empty', 'complete.rs:544') removes
childless tombstones, childless deleted phantoms and empty
write-protected folders, never a root.
*** A node has at most one editable occurrence
The editable map, with Final and Tentative claims, decides which
('server/to_org/extend/editable.rs', module comment). The walk
prefers a member to a non-member.
*** A write-protected occurrence holds no member
Whenever an occurrence becomes write-protected, its member
children become non-members ('make_member_children_nonMembers').
*** A kept child never moves
A created one goes after its model predecessor.
'KeptChildOrder::Unmoved', with two exceptions: lists whose order
the save writes follow the graph's order ('FollowsGoal'), and the
diff view moves its removed members to their slots.
*** Folder members carry the containerward chain
Under every member of a partner folder but the two acquisition
folders hang its containers, write-protected non-members, and
theirs, to the roots.
*** The diff view is the same walk in diff-view mode
Over the straddle the folders show the union of the two graphs,
and each editable node's visit ends with a step that adds the
node's change viewnodes.
*** The tail runs for every render
Roots become members, members under dead parents become
non-members, claims a write-protected parent does not support are
flipped, stats are computed, the restriction is applied, viewnode
IDs are assigned, and the text is rendered.
*** Every buffer but one is drawn by the walk
The search buffer is drawn by hand in two phases, with a tail of
its own, and meets the walk at its first save.

* The pipeline, from parsed buffer to text
** What arrives
The save has already updated the graph; completion assumes it
('update_views_after_save', 'server/update_buffer.rs:148'). The
parsed viewforest carries, per viewnode, its kind, its title and
body as typed, its membership and editability, its view requests,
its fold state, its viewnode ID, its change facts as display
facts, and the edit requests the user wrote. Save extraction is
outside this report; what matters here is that the buffer's shape
is the old shape, and the graph is the new graph.
** Before the walk, for the saved view
In order ('update_views_after_save'):
*** unknown viewnode IDs are dropped
'drop_unknown_viewnode_ids_in_parsed_viewforest': an ID the
view's record does not know becomes None, to be reissued at the
end.
*** fork roots are replaced
'replace_saved_view_fork_roots': a root this save forked is
rewritten to the clone.
*** edit requests are consumed
'consume_edit_requests_after_save': readiness, relRepo, restore
and edit requests are cleared; view requests stay for completion.
*** merged IDs are rewritten
'rewriteInPlace_viewnodes_whose_skgid_is_newly_extra': an
occurrence whose ID this save made an extra ID of another node
takes that node's pid and text.
*** the text-release gate
Under a restriction, a check that no protected text can leak;
nothing changes in the tree.
*** ID folders are removed
'remove_id_folders', not in the diff view: an ID folder holding
only ID entries is detached, one holding anything else
entombstoned. This is one of two detachments outside the prune.
*** the diff view brings its straddle up to date
'prepare_diffView_rerender': the straddle in the slot is rebuilt
if HEAD or the worktree moved; with the integrate option, each
node of U gets an editable occurrence.
** The walk
'complete_viewforest' ('complete.rs:206'): a FIFO queue starts
with the buffer root; each dequeued viewnode gets its visit, and
its children as they stand after the visit are enqueued. Then the
prune. The queue is what makes "first wins" and the budget
order-correct: the first occurrence reached in level order wins,
not the first in depth-first order.
** After the walk
*** the diff view's finish
'finish_diffView_rerender': with the integrate option, the
content origins of D the buffer lacks are appended as roots and
completed with the same context; then the readiness facts are
read from the store onto the viewnodes
('set_diffView_facts_in_viewforest').
*** the tail, 'finish_viewforest'
In order: 'mark_view_roots_as_members';
'mark_orphans_under_dead_parents_false' (a member under a deleted
phantom or a tombstone becomes a non-member);
'validate_membership_claims' (a member under a write-protected
unrestricted parent that does not contain it becomes a
non-member; editable parents are trusted); graph stats; viewnode
stats; 'apply_skgrepo_set_to_viewforest' (restricted nodes become
restricted vognodes); 'assign_viewnode_ids';
'viewforest_to_string'.
** The renders and what differs between them
*** the saved view
All of the above; 'RerenderOptions::for_saved_view': default
folders off, override substitution of existing members on, the
warning sink present.
*** a collateral view
A clone of the server's copy; the merged-ID rewrite; then the
rerender with 'RerenderOptions::ordinary': both flags off, no
sink. The parse-side steps do not run, since nothing was parsed.
*** rerender-all
Each open view, as a collateral view, with no save facts (empty
deleted sets). The merged-ID rewrite does not run.
*** the skgrepo-set switch
Each open view, after the prepass: now-hidden deleted and unknown
phantoms become tombstones, now-hidden unrestricted vognodes
become restricted, and a postorder prune removes restricted
leaves, properties under them, tombstone leaves and the folders
this empties. Then the rerender with default folders on, since
the switch may have revealed members.
*** the de novo render
A stub forest; 'CompletionContext::for_content_view': default
folders on, nothing deleted, nothing there before, the sink
present but dropped. The same walk and tail.
*** the diff view's first render
A stub over the straddle; 'CompletionContext::for_diffView': no
default folders, no restriction, no sink.
*** the search buffer, in two phases
Drawn by hand, not by the walk, and completed at its first save;
see "The search buffer" below.

* One visit
** The order of a visit
'dispatch_node_update' ('complete.rs:267'):
*** a folder checks its ancestry first
'folder_is_generalized_orphan': if some required ancestor is of
the wrong kind, 'entombstone_generalized_orphan_folder' disposes
the children and entombstones the folder, and the visit ends.
*** the diff view's hook before the extension
'diffView_visit_before_extension' reads the completion mode. In
a content view it does nothing. In diff-view mode: a changed
property folder's visit is already done; a historical phantom
adds its old content, and is visited as a node only if its node
is back. What else the mode changes (folders over the straddle,
no substitution, no default folders) each visit asks of the mode
itself, through its methods.
*** then by kind
An unrestricted vognode: the ordinary node's visit. The
subscribee folder in a content view: its own reconciler. The two
filter folders: theirs. Any other partner folder with a member
role: the generic folder reconciler, over the live graph or the
straddle. The alias, ID and flags folders: their refreshers. A
historical phantom whose node is back: the ordinary node's visit.
Everything else is a no-op: restricted vognodes, unknown and
deleted phantoms, tombstones, properties, the buffer root.
*** after any partner folder, in a content view
If the folder's members carry the chain, each member's chain is
reconciled
('reconcile_containerward_chains_under_folder_members'). Over the
straddle the folder reconciler did it already.
** An ordinary node's visit, step by step
'visit_normal_node' is 'update_normal_node' then the diff view's
part after it.
*** the deletion preflight
If this save deleted the node,
'mutate_unrestrictedVognode_to_deletednode' makes it a deleted
phantom, subtree attached, and the visit ends.
*** the write-protect request
'apply_writeProtect_request': plain form, this occurrence and its
member children become non-members; subtree form, the whole
content subtree.
*** the budget
If the budget is spent, this extension is charged, and the node
is not a root, the occurrence is write-protected, its members
become non-members, and the rule is marked settled.
*** the editability rule, when a request is present
'apply_editability_rule': yield to a Final claim elsewhere
('ClaimedElsewhere'), or take the claim as Final,
write-protecting any Tentative occurrence with its subtree
('MadeFinal', cascade on).
*** the alignment, in the diff view
'align_content_members_before_extension', described under the
diff view.
*** the content extension
'extend_true_content_at_unrestrictedVognode', described in the
next section: the rule for an occurrence without a request, then
the clobber path or the reconciliation path.
*** default folders
If the node is fresh on a de novo render, or carried an
editable-view request, and default folders are allowed:
'maybe_add_default_partnerFolder_branches'.
*** the other view requests
'execute_unrestrictedVognode_view_requests': folder requests, the
flags request. The editable and write-protect requests are gone
by now.
*** the hidden-in folder
'ensure_hiddenInFolder_under_editable_subscribee', not in the
diff view.
*** the diff view's part
'diffView_visit_after_extension': a deleted phantom whose node
HEAD has becomes historical; a node still unrestricted gets the
per-visit step.

* The content of an ordinary node
'extend_true_content_at_unrestrictedVognode'
('server/update_buffer/reconcile/content.rs:74').
** The rule for an occurrence without a request
Unless settled,
'make_writeProtected_if_repeat_then_extend_editable_map': first
'take_claim_from_tentative_nonMember' (an editable member takes a
Tentative claim from a non-member that is not its ancestor; the
non-member is write-protected with its subtree); then, if the map
holds the node, this occurrence is write-protected; if it is
write-protected, however it got so, its members become
non-members; else it claims Tentative.
** The write-protected path
'clobberWriteProtectedViewnode': title and home skgrepo from the
graph, and return. No budget charge, no children touched: every
effect of write protection on children happened at the moment of
write-protecting.
** The editable path
The budget is charged. 'sync_unrestrictedVognode_from_disk'
resets title, home skgrepo and body. Then
'reconcile_content_children', then the cascade if Final, then
'order_children_as_non_vognodes_then_ignored_then_content':
folders and properties first, then non-member vognodes, then
members and phantoms.
** The goal list
The node's 'contains' in file order, each member resolved to a
pid or kept raw if unresolved, with its relRepo, gated by the
restriction ('outbound_member_keys_for_relation_gated'). For a
member of a subscribee folder, the list is the contains minus
what the subscriber hides and minus what the subscriber contains
('content_goal_list'). Then 'omit_restricted_members'.
** The passes, in order
*** substitution of existing members
'replace_raw_content_children_with_visible_overriders', only in
the saved view and only when the node is not shown raw: a member
whose node is overridden is rewritten in place to the overrider,
with 'overridesHere' set.
*** adoption
'adopt_nonMember_children_that_are_content': for each goal entry
with no member child of that raw ID, the first non-member child
of that raw ID becomes the member.
*** the goal-list reconciler
'complete_content_children' over 'complete_managed_children'.
Managed: member unrestricted vognodes and every unknown phantom.
Key: 'collected_skgid' (the original when a substitute is shown),
or the unknown's raw ID. Order 'FollowsGoal'. Before it,
'normalize_relationship_backed_content_unknowns' turns a member
whose node vanished, but whose ID the contains still names, into
an unknown phantom. A matched child is kept as is; of several
copies the editable one, else the first. A stale or duplicate
child is converted. A missing entry is created: an editable
member ('mk_editable_viewnode'), a restricted vognode, or an
unknown phantom; a substitute when the node is overridden.
*** erroneous members
'mark_erroneous_content_children_as_indep': a member whose key is
not in the goal becomes a non-member. The reconciler converted
these already; this finds nothing in practice.
*** unknown non-members
'convert_nonmember_unknown_children_to_tombstones': an unknown
phantom not in the goal becomes a tombstone.
** The deleted-by-this-save node
Handled before any of this, by the preflight. Its folders die at
their own visits as generalized orphans; its member children
become non-members in the tail; a childless deleted phantom is
pruned.

* The editability walk
'server/to_org/extend/editable.rs', module comment.
** The claim
The map holds, per node, Final or Tentative at one tree position.
Only an editable occurrence claims. The map is rebuilt at every
completion, so editability can move between rerenders.
** Without a request
Described above: first wins, with the member preference. A
removed member counts as a member for the preference
('counts_as_member_for_the_claim').
** With a request
'apply_editability_rule': a Final claim elsewhere wins and the
request is dropped; otherwise this occurrence becomes Final, a
Tentative occurrence elsewhere is write-protected with its
content subtree, and the title and body are reread from disk.
** The cascade
'pass_editableViewRequest_to_content_children': a Final
occurrence puts the request on each member content child; each
applies the rule at its own visit.
** The budget
Charged once per extension, in the editable path. At zero, a
non-root occurrence is write-protected before its extension. The
diff view charges only requests outside U.
** Write protection's side effect
Every path that write-protects an occurrence also makes its
member children non-members: the request, the rule, the budget,
the yield, the conversion of a write-protected retainee. The tail
checks the same invariant once more against the graph.

* A folder's members
** The shared core
Every partner folder, in every view, ends in
'reconcile_partnerFolder_children_against_goal_list_with_deleted_extraIds'
('server/to_org/complete/partner_folder/child_data.rs:220'). In
order:
*** normalize
A member whose node vanished, while the relationship still names
its ID, becomes an unknown phantom.
*** adopt
'mark_goal_children_as_folder_members': for each goal item that
no managed child stands for, a non-member standing for its node
(editable preferred) becomes a member.
*** the goal-list reconciler
Managed: what 'skgid_a_folder_child_stands_for' resolves: a
member, a removed member, an unknown phantom, a historical
phantom. Key: the resolved ID. Unmanaged, left in place:
non-members (notes, retained copies, chain nodes), restricted
vognodes, deleted phantoms, nested folders, properties,
tombstones. Order: 'FollowsGoal' for the editable policies,
'Unmoved' for the write-protected ones. A stale or duplicate
member is converted with its drawn chain
('convert_unwarranted_member_with_its_chain'). A missing item is
created ('viewnode_of_goal_item'): a write-protected member, a
removed member, an unknown phantom, or a historical phantom.
*** apply the shapes
'apply_goal_shapes': a matched child takes its item's shape:
member (editability kept), removed (non-member, write-protected),
or the model's kind and change facts ('give_model_facts_to').
** The goal list of each kind
*** the generic folders
'partnerFolder_members_in_graph': for a folder of recorded
relationships, the viewparent's own list with unresolved IDs
kept; for a folder of recorders, the recorders sorted by ID. A
write-protected set is reordered by
'view_order_preserving_goal_list': the members already standing,
in buffer order, then the rest.
*** the subscribee folder
Its own reconciler in a content view
('reconcile_subscribeeFolder_children'): the subscriber's
'subscribesTo'. It also ensures the hidden-outside folder exists
and stands last, creating an empty one for the prune to remove.
*** the two filter folders
'goal_list_for_hiddenInSubscribee_folder': the subscriber's hides
that the subscribee contains.
'goal_list_for_hiddenOutsideOfSubscribee_folder': the hides that
no subscribee contains.
*** over the straddle
'straddle_goal_item' from 'GraphStraddle::partners': a node only
in HEAD is a historical phantom; a relationship only in HEAD is a
removed member; else a member. Described under the diff view.
** The containerward chain under members
After the folder's visit,
'reconcile_containerward_chain_under_member' for each member: a
model of the member with its chain freshly drawn
('attach_containerward_chains_under_members'), reconciled by the
shallow-model reconciler, selecting every child that is not a
member, converting when stale only a drawn chain node or a
relRepo move. A note the user typed under a member stays.
** The fillers
A folder request ('(folder REL)') is fulfilled by
'build_and_integrate_folder', which creates the folder and fills
it through the shared core; the subscribee folder and the
hidden-in folder have their own fillers; the alias and flags
folders are built by hand ('build_and_integrate_aliases',
'build_and_integrate_flags'). A filler skips a folder that
exists.
** Warnings
'push_repair_warnings' after the generic reconciler for any
policy but EditableSet, and after the two filter folders always,
when a sink exists: reinstated, retained, removed stale, removed
duplicate.

* Property folders
The alias folder is refreshed from the node's aliases, managed
entries 'Property::Alias' only, order 'FollowsGoal', stale
entries entombstoned. The ID folder from the node's IDs,
'Unmoved'; in a content view it never runs, since
'remove_id_folders' detached the folder before the walk. The
flags folder from the true flags, 'Unmoved'. None is
self-deletable but the ID folder. In the diff view a changed
folder is filled by the step and skipped at its own visit; an
unchanged one is refreshed here.

* The diff view's part
** The mode
'CompletionContext::mode' is a 'CompletionMode'
('server/update_buffer/completion_mode.rs'): 'ContentView', or
'DiffView { straddle, buffer_kind }'. The two hooks in
'server/diff_view/visit.rs' match on it, and each visit asks it
what the mode allows: substitution, default folders, the
subscribee folder's own reconciler, the budget charge, the
alignment. The buffer's U is read from the pruned change view's
sets at every visit that charges the budget. Until commit
'7d79a925' the mode was two option fields, called the switch,
and the same choice stood again in 'PartnerFolderGoalSource' and
in three constant fields of 'VisitSettings'; the enum replaced
all three.
** Before the extension: the alignment
At an editable node, over its children in buffer order, creating,
moving and refolding nothing: an unrestricted child whose
relationship the worktree dropped becomes a non-member, whatever
it was; a non-member standing for a node in the after list, with
no member of it yet, becomes the member; a historical phantom
whose node is back becomes an editable member. So the content
reconciler then keeps each viewnode, and a stale leaf member is
not entombstoned by it.
** After the extension: the step
'process_unrestrictedVognode_changes' ('changes.rs:156'), in
order:
*** a root tags itself
'tag_root': the node change, editable or not.
*** a write-protected occurrence
'convert_change_viewnodes' with an empty model (its change
viewnodes and removed members are converted), properties
unmarked, members tagged. Return.
*** fold state for a created node
Body folded unless the node is in C.
*** tag the members
'tag_content_members': each member, each non-member whose node is
content, and each unknown phantom gets its change facts. A member
outside U, not editable before the rerender, is write-protected
without losing its requests. Under each, a relRepo move is
reconciled as a one-item model.
*** the removed members
'reconcile_removed_content_members': a model of removed members
and historical phantoms from the interleaved diff of the two
content lists, reconciled among the non-members and historical
phantoms, converting stale ones only if they are the diff view's;
then each kept one is moved before its anchor, the one reconciler
that moves what it keeps.
*** the fronts, for a node in C
'model_of_front_change_viewnodes': old text with its body diff,
the home-skgrepo move, the "it acquired" folder, the changed
alias, ID and flags folders with their entries tagged, the
content and relRepo changeset markers; in the order-only buffer
its marker. Reconciled with 'is_front_change_viewnode' as
selector, descending into folders so entries and acquirees are
matched inside.
*** unmark the other property folders
'unmark_property_folders_except': entries lose their change
facts; a removed alias or ID entry is converted.
*** the changed partner folders
'changed_partner_folders': for each in-folder or out-folder whose
relationship changed, find the folder or append an empty one; its
own visit fills it.
*** placement
Fronts, then partner folders, then markers; kept children do not
move.
** The shallow-model reconciler
'reconcile_children_with_shallowModelSubtree'
('shallow_model_subtree.rs:105'): candidates are the selected
children with a key; for each model child in order, the copies of
its key are taken, the editable one else the first is adopted and
given the model's facts ('give_model_facts_to': title, home,
membership, write protection if the model has it, change facts),
the other copies are converted as duplicates, and a missing child
is a copy of the model subtree; then each untaken candidate for
which 'converts_when_stale' holds is converted with what goes
with it; created children are placed after their model
predecessors. 'match_key': a vognode by the ID it stands for, a
property by its kind and text, a folder by its kind.
** Folders over the straddle
'reconcile_partnerFolder_children_from_graph_straddle': goal
items from the union in interleaved-diff order; the shared core
keyed over the after-graph; then
'attach_chains_under_straddle_folder_members': under each
unrestricted child that is a partner, removed members included, a
relRepo move (always for in-folders, only when changed for
out-folders) and the chain over the union.
** C, R, U, D in completion
C gates the fronts, the changed folders and the body fold. U
decides write protection of members, whether a removed member or
old content is editable, and the budget exemption. R only feeds
D. D is the roots; completion itself does not read it, except the
integrate option, which appends the missing content origins.
Change facts read the straddle, not C.
** Readiness facts and restore flags
After the walk, 'set_ready_facts_in_viewforest' sets, on every
viewnode of the kinds it names, the ready objects and the leaves
from the store. Every reconciler that rematches a viewnode clears
them first. The restore flags are read by the save, never by
completion.

* The search buffer
The search buffer is the one buffer the walk does not build. The
server draws it by hand, in two phases, and registers it as a
view ('ViewforestId::SearchView', shown to the client as
"search:TERMS"), so that every later render treats it like any
other view: a save of it, a collateral rerender, rerender-all and
the skgrepo-set switch all run the walk over it.
** Phase 1: the hits
'handle_text_search_request'
('server/serve/handlers/text_search.rs:133'). The index is
queried; matches are grouped by node, scored, and filtered to the
restriction. A hit that an owned hit overrides, directly or
through a chain of overrides, is suppressed at the top level
('suppressed_result_skgids'); a foreign overrider suppresses
nothing. 'build_search_viewforest' then appends one root per hit,
in score order, up to the display limit: a write-protected
unrestricted vognode whose title is the best-matching title or
alias from the index ('mk_writeProtected_viewnode', membership
'Member'). When several titles or aliases of one node matched,
the others go into an alias folder built by hand under the root.
Viewnode IDs are assigned from a fresh record, the forest is
rendered and registered (a prior search view of the same terms is
replaced), and the text is sent as 'search-results'. No
completion runs.
** Phase 2: the enrichment
'spawn_enrichment_thread' computes, in the background, the
containerward role tree of every hit (one walk per hit,
'containerward_role_trees_by_skgid_from_skgids') and the graph
stats of the hits, their chain nodes and the members of their
override folders. The server then asks the client for a snapshot
of the search buffer, dirty or not, and
'enrich_search_buffer_snapshot' ('server/serve.rs:379') works on
that text: it parses it, drops unknown viewnode IDs, and
'insert_partner_folders_into_search_view'
('render_enriched_search_buffer.rs:23') prepends under each hit,
folded, its overridden folder, its overrider folder and its
containers folder, each only when it has a member. The override
folders hold the direct relatives, read from the live graph with
their titles. The containers folder's members and their chain are
drawn by a drawer of its own ('insert_containerward_tree'), with
titles from the search index and the skgrepo "search" as a
fallback when the index lacks the node, and with its own relRepo
and skgrepo gating. An empty containers folder is detached by
hand. Then the enrichment runs a tail of its own: graph stats
from the prefetch, 'mark_view_roots_as_members', viewnode stats,
the restriction, the exclusion of overPrivateText nodes when the
user declined them, the text-release decision, viewnode IDs, the
render, and the view's update. The passes for orphans and
membership claims do not run.
** What a save does to it
The client saves a search buffer as it saves any skg buffer,
under its viewforest ID, and the server runs the saved-view
pipeline over it. So the first completion of a search buffer
happens at its first save, or when it is collateral to another
save, or at rerender-all or a skgrepo-set switch. At that
completion each root is a write-protected unrestricted vognode,
so its visit takes the clobber path, and the title from the index
gives way to the graph's. The hand-built alias folder is
refreshed from the node's aliases, so it comes to hold all of
them, not the matching ones. The containers and overrider folders
are reconciled by the generic reconciler over the live graph,
their members adopted by key and kept where they stand
('Unmoved'). The chain under each member is reconciled, so a
chain node titled from the index takes the graph's title
('give_model_facts_to'). The overridden folder, an EditableSet,
follows the graph's order. An empty folder of a self-deletable
kind is pruned. Whether the save reads the overridden folder's
membership under a write-protected root is save extraction,
outside this report; it is the one place where the search buffer
puts an editable interface under a write-protected node. Nothing
of this was run; it follows from the reconcilers described above,
applied to the shapes phase 2 leaves.
** Where it departs from the rules
*** it is built without the walk
Both phases construct the forest by hand; the walk's goal lists,
selectors and conversions play no part until the first save.
*** its roots are write-protected members
A content view's roots are editable. A hit is a write-protected
root with membership 'Member'; a root prints no membership
anyway.
*** it has its own chain drawer and its own gating
'insert_containerward_tree' stands beside
'attach_containerward_chains_under_members', with separate
relRepo and skgrepo checks, and titles from the index.
*** it has its own tail
The enrichment repeats five of the tail's eight steps by hand and
skips the orphan and claim passes.
*** it has its own prune
An empty containers folder is detached by hand; the override
folders are not drawn when empty.
*** its alias folder shows matches, not aliases
Until the first save refreshes it.
*** suppression and the display limit are search-only inputs
No other buffer drops a root because another root overrides it.
** What one set of rules for every buffer would need
The walk already draws a content view from a stub forest of roots
with a '(folder containers)' request each. The search buffer
would fit the same shape: phase 1 would append one
write-protected root per hit, with folder requests for
containers, overriders and overridden, and phase 2 would complete
that stub by the walk and 'finish_viewforest' instead of its own
drawers and tail. The search-specific parts would shrink to three
inputs: the order of the hits, the suppression, and which title
the hit shows. The two-phase protocol could stay as it is, since
phase 2 would still run after the snapshot. This paragraph is a
proposal, not a description.

* What becomes of a child nobody wants
One place for the outcomes, as the code decides them.
** a stale member holding something
Retained as non-member, keeping its editability, losing its
change facts; if it is write-protected, its members become
non-members.
** a stale leaf
A tombstone, pruned at the end.
** a duplicate
As a stale one, plus write protection; its subtree stays under
it.
** a stale folder member
As above, after its drawn chain is converted postorder; a member
holding only its chain goes.
** a stale change viewnode
Converted with what goes with it (its folder's members, the drawn
chain); what the user typed under it keeps it retained.
** a member whose node vanished while the relationship stands
An unknown phantom, by normalization.
** a member whose node this save deleted
A deleted phantom at its own visit; its folders die as
generalized orphans; its members become non-members in the tail.
** a folder whose ancestor died
Entombstoned at its visit; members converted, nested folders left
to die at theirs.
** a now-restricted node, at a skgrepo-set switch
A restricted vognode; a leaf one pruned by the prepass.
** a note the user typed
Never converted as stale by any reconciler; converted as a
duplicate only when another copy of its key is adopted.
** an empty folder
Pruned if self-deletable (write-protected kinds, the filter
folders, the ID folder); kept if an editable interface
(subscribee, overridden, alias, flags).

* An example, walked through
Let the worktree before the save hold: k contains l and m; m
contains mc; b contains k. HEAD plays no part in a content view.
The user has k's view open, with its containers folder:
#+begin_example
  * k
  ** ☮ These contain it.
  *** ☮ (Cb) b
  ** l
  ** m
  *** mc
#+end_example
In a second view the user moves m from k into l and saves. The
worktree now holds: k contains l; l contains m; m contains mc; b
contains k. The first view is collateral. Its rerender:
** level one: k
No deletion, no request. The rule: k unclaimed, Tentative. The
editable path: goal list l. Managed: l and m, both members. l is
matched. m is stale; it holds mc, so it is retained as a
non-member, editable, change facts cleared. 'FollowsGoal' and the
regrouping put the folder first, then the non-member m, then l.
** level two: the folder, m, l
The folder: ancestry fine; goal b; b matched; b's chain
reconciled (b has no container). The retained m: editable; the
rule finds nothing to take and claims Tentative; its goal is mc,
matched. l: Tentative; its goal is m, no member m stands under
it, so an editable member m is created.
** level three: b, mc, the new m
b: write-protected, clobber. mc under the retained m: Tentative,
no content. The new m: an editable member whose node a Tentative
non-member holds, not its ancestor, so the retained m yields: it
and mc are write-protected and leave the map; the new m claims
Tentative, its goal mc, created editable.
** level four, the prune, the tail
mc under the new m: Tentative, no content. Nothing is childless
and deletable. Roots are members; no orphan; k is editable, so
its claims are trusted; stats; the two created viewnodes get IDs:
#+begin_example
  * k
  ** ☮ These contain it.
  *** ☮ (Cb) b
  ** ⊥☮ m
  *** ⊥☮ mc
  ** l
  *** m
  **** mc
#+end_example
'tests/retain_or_entombstone_then_prune.rs' pins this outcome
('a_retained_editable_copy_yields_the_claim_to_a_member_met_later').

* Where the boundaries blur
** The three words, and what the code calls things
You asked about reconciler, refresher and completer. The code has
no refresher: "refresh" appears in comments for the property
folders' reconcilers and the text resync. It has one completion,
the walk, and one word "complete" misused for the older
reconciler. It has two reconcilers, which differ in interface,
not in rule: the goal-list one takes a flat list and a selector
closure; the shallow-model one takes a scratch tree, a selector,
an ownership predicate and a descend predicate. Both convert by
'convert_unwarranted_child'; both adopt the editable copy else
the first; both place created children after their model
predecessor. The fillers are a third interface for the same work:
build a folder by hand when a request asks, and the next visit
reconciles it anyway. So the boundary is between three interfaces
to one rule, and the vocabulary follows the interfaces. The
search buffer's enrichment is a fourth interface: it draws
folders, a chain and a tail by hand, and the walk reconciles them
at the first save.

** Observations I would check before relying on them
Each was traced by an agent and not run. Each could be a defect
or a misreading.
*** adoption keys by raw ID, the reconciler by collected skgid
'adopt_nonMember_children_that_are_content' compares raw
't.skgid' ('content.rs:306'), while the reconciler and the
pre-fetch compare 'collected_skgid'. A non-member shown as a
substitute might not be adopted for its original, and a second
occurrence created beside it.
*** 'give_model_facts_to' may write-protect a removed member
When a removed member's node leaves U, the model is
write-protected and the kept viewnode takes that
('shallow_model_subtree.rs:301'), against the comment at
'changes.rs:325' and the doc that nothing editable is
write-protected again. Members get the guard; removed members and
old content do not.
*** removed members under a write-protected node end two ways
'convert_change_viewnodes' converts them, then
'tag_content_members' re-tags a retained one as removed; a leaf
is a tombstone, a branch a removed member again.
*** created phantoms may be reported as reinstated
'partner_folders.rs:80' passes the summary to the warning for
non-EditableSet folders; with a sink, a created historical
phantom or removed member would be reported as a reinstated
member.
*** a member shown by an extra ID gets no chain in the diff view
'attach_chains_under_straddle_folder_members' keys by raw ID
('partner_folders.rs:139'), the core by resolved ID.
*** the hidden-outside folder warns though its policy is editable
'hiddenoutsideof_subscribeefolder.rs:84'; the policy doc says
editable policies do not warn.
*** 'build_child_data' prefers a sibling's title
A created or reshaped member may inherit a stale title from
another occurrence of its ID ('child_data.rs:163').
*** a user's non-member of a container's ID is reshaped
'give_model_facts_to' under a member
('shallow_model_subtree.rs:140') would retitle and write-protect
a user-typed non-member whose ID equals a container's.
*** a pasted copy under a member has a chain node's shape
'is_drawn_chain_node' cannot tell it from a drawn one, so it is
converted as stale when the container goes (noted in
'20_review.org' already).
*** comments cite files that do not exist
'to_org/complete/sharing/mod.rs' ('subscribee_folder.rs:59'),
'reconcile_hiddenIn_folders' ('partner_folder/mod.rs:305').
