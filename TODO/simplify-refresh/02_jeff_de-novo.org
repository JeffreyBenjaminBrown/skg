* Rebuild buffer-refresh from scratch.
** shape
The input of the refresh is a graph -- the truth -- and a buffer.
The output of refresh is a graph.
The graph is org format plus metadata for every headline.
** principles
Where the buffer contradicts the graph, it is corrected or removed.
It must not lie.
It should recompute every value from the graph that the graph dictates.
It should not show restricted data -- nodes or relationships,
although if the relationships are hard to hide, whatever,
perfect privacy is not critical.
* kinds of thing, and what they should do
** tombstones
Things should be tombstoned if they are unsayable.
(Let's say 'entombstone' only when the shorter form
could be confused with a noun).
Tombstones need be collected and pruned only once,
after all other computations that produce the new buffer.
** phantoms
Things deleted this session are currently one kind of phantom.
Things dredged up from version history are another.
We should use one type for those two things,
and distinguish them only by their
  Permanence = version-controlled | session-lifetime
That permanence should show up in the heralds somehow.

They should be left in place,
and
  skg-request-phantom-tombstone ;; entombstone one phantom
  skg-request-buffer-phantoms-tombstone ;; all of them
available to the user.
(Tombstones are collected postorder after those requests are fulfilled,
before redisplay.)

Phantoms should be recalculated from among their parents' children,
in-place (to make sure we don't detach interesting descendents).
** unknown
This should be distinct from phantoms. I believe it already is.
It is not tombstoned -- that would be an edit.
** folders
I think these rules apply to all folder kinds!
*** restore members
if any are missing
*** reorder members
to match the truth, but only if there is one
(the graph says, not the view).
*** retain nonmembers, with as little change as possible
*** merge
If the ID for a member is repeated among its siblilngs,
those instances should be unified.
Buffer-refresh must be able to
trust that the children of anything write-protected are not marked members.
That makes collecting the children of siblings with like IDs is safe:
#+begin_example
  * n
  ** (editable) x
  *** y
  ** (write-protected) x
  *** (nonmember) y
#+end_example
becomes
#+begin_example
  * n
  ** (editable) x
  *** y
  *** (nonmember) y
#+end_example
which, when refresh recurses to process the children of x, then becomes
#+begin_example
  * n
  ** (editable) x
  *** y
#+end_example
When so unifying, if any collection is editable,
their union is editable.
** diff data
This can safely be recomputed in-place
(to make sure we don't detach interesting descendents).
Every diff-specific node -- not just phantoms, but folders
and maybe anything else -- should have an
unusual-colored pac-man ghost ᗣ character as its first emoji.
