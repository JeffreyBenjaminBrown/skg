* Words this plan uses
** compound partner folder
A partner folder whose membership joins more than one
relationship, as against the ordinary partner folders, which
read one relation role. Today there are two, and each joins
three nodes: a subscriber N, a subscribee S and a hidden node
H, by "N subscribes to S", "N hides H" and "S contains H". The
name joins two phrases the code already uses: "partner folder"
and the "compound filter folders" of three comments
('server/types/viewnode.rs' line 409,
'server/update_buffer/viewnodestats.rs' lines 401 and 431),
which the slice will turn into "compound partner folders".
The *hidden-in compound partner folder*
('hiddenInSubscribeeFolder') stands under S inside N's
subscribee folder and holds N's hides that S contains, in the
order of N's hides list. The *hidden-outside compound partner
folder* ('hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder') stands in N's
subscribee folder beside the subscribees and holds N's hides
that no subscribee contains. Their goal lists are
'goal_list_for_hiddenInSubscribee_folder' and
'goal_list_for_hiddenOutsideOfSubscribee_folder' in
'server/to_org/complete/partner_folder/goal_list.rs'.
** the retired word
"Filter folder", "filter policy" and "derived filter", when
they name one of these two folders or the policies of both,
are retired by this plan; "compound filter folder" becomes
"compound partner folder". "Derived filter" goes with the rest
because it names the hidden-outside folder, in five comments
and one docstring, in other words. "Filter" keeps its other
senses, which this plan does not touch: Rust's
iterator method; the save pipeline's noop filter
('filter_wouldbe_noop_nodeInstructions'); search filtering in
Tantivy and 'text_search.rs'; Emacs's and Neovim's process
filters; the tracing 'EnvFilter'.
