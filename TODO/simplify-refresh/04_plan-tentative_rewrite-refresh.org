* Words this plan uses
** the refresh
The one operation that takes a buffer (parsed and validated)
and the truth and returns the corrected buffer. It replaces
today's "completion", "reconciliation", "rerender" and
"enrichment", which name the same thing done in several
places. In code: 'refresh_viewforest'.
** the truth
The gated in-Rust graph: the worktree's graph as the skgrepo
restriction allows it to be seen. It answers, for a gnid,
'present', 'hidden' or 'absent'. For the diff view the truth
also includes HEAD, through the graph straddle.
** mode
'CompletionMode::ContentView' or 'CompletionMode::DiffView'
('server/update_buffer/completion_mode.rs'). It decides two
things: whether the goal of a parent includes what HEAD alone
warrants, and whether change viewnodes are drawn.
** gnid, vnid
A *gnid* is a graph node's ID (today 'skgid', the type 'ID');
a node's primary gnid is its *pid*, the others its extra IDs.
A *vnid* names one viewnode for the buffer's lifetime.
** occurrence
A viewnode standing for a node the truth has (today
'Vognode::Unrestricted', 'UnrestrictedVognode'). It is
either *editable* or *write-protected*; one occurrence per
node is
editable.
** ghost node, ghost warrant
A *ghost* is a viewnode standing for a node HEAD has and the
truth does not, drawn and retained only in the diff view.
A *ghost warrant* is a position warranted by HEAD alone: a
ghost under a parent that contained it at HEAD, or an
occurrence whose relationship to its parent HEAD had and the
worktree lacks (today's "removed member"). Today's deleted
and historical phantoms both become ghosts; "phantom" is
retired.
** unknown
A viewnode standing for a gnid that its parent's list names
and no node answers to. It has no text; it claims membership,
since removing it would edit the list.
** tombstone
A viewnode that says only "something stood here". It holds
the children of the viewnode it replaced; childless, it is
pruned at the end of every refresh, roots included.
** folder
A viewnode grouping the children of one kind of relationship
of its viewparent: the twelve partner folders (ten relation
roles and the diff view's two acquisition folders) and the
three property folders. The two *compound partner folders*
('hiddenInSubscribeeFolder', 'hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder')
join three nodes; the others read one relation role. "Filter
folder" is retired (plan 03). In code: one enum 'Folder'.
** member
A child of a folder or of an occurrence's content. A property
folder's members are its *property members* (alias, id,
flag); "entry" is retired.
** change viewnode
A headline the diff view adds to describe a change: the old
text, a body diff, a home-skgrepo move, a relRepo move, a
changeset marker (today 'Property::OldText' and the four
beside it). In code: 'ChangeViewnode'.
** claims membership, noClaim
A child *claims membership* when the save will read it as an
entry of its viewparent's list. It is a declared fact: the
bare atom 'noClaim' (today 'nonMember') says no, its absence
says yes. It is meaningful only under an editable occurrence
or an editable folder; under a write-protected parent no
child claims, whatever it carries, and the printer writes no
atom there. In code: the bool 'claimsMembership'.
** is warranted, noWarrant, ghostWarrant
A child *is warranted* when the truth relates it to its
viewparent as its position implies: content under an
occurrence, a partner of the folder's relation in a folder,
a container of the node above it in a chain, a property of
the node in a property folder. In the diff view, HEAD alone
may warrant it (a ghost warrant). A derived fact, recomputed
at every refresh and written as the bare atom 'noWarrant'
or 'ghostWarrant' among the view facts; nothing written means
warranted by the truth. Roots carry no warrant fact. In code:
'isWarranted : Warrant' with 'ByGraph', 'ByHead', 'No'.
** declared parts, derived facts
The two classes of metadata. *Declared parts* are what the
client is responsible for and the save or the refresh reads:
the kind, 'vnid', 'gnid', 'skgRepos', 'noClaim',
'writeProtected', 'overridesHere', the requests. *Derived
facts* are what the server computes and nobody reads back:
everything in '(view ...)' and '(diff ...)'. Today's words
"save input" and "display fact" are retired.
** goal
What the truth says a parent's children should contain: for
an occurrence's content, its 'contains' list in file order;
for a folder, its partners in the folder's relation (ordered
when the viewparent's own file records them, unordered when
recorders do); for a property folder, the node's aliases, IDs
or true flags; in the diff view, plus what HEAD alone
warrants. Each goal item names a gnid or a property value.
** extent
How much of the goal is drawn: all of it for a folder and for
an editable occurrence's content (within the node budget);
none of it, beyond what the buffer already shows, for a
write-protected occurrence's content and for the chain under
a member.
** reinstate
To draw a goal item that has no child.
** the ladder
The fixed order of corrections the refresh applies to a
headline, weakest first, given below. A headline that no rung
can make true is *unsayable* and is entombstoned.
** FoldAndFocus, RelationshipEditRequest
'FoldAndFocus { focused, folded, bodyFolded }' bundles the
three position atoms (rename it 'BufferState' if it grows).
'RelationshipEditRequest { RelRepo (SkgrepoName), RestoreRel }'
is the request an occurrence, an unknown or an alias member
may carry about its relationship; a node's own edit request
('NodeEditRequest') stays inside 'Editability::Editable'.
** How to say it in messages
A headline is named as a subtree by its vnid and title. A
correction is reported, not warned: the refresh returns the
list of what it changed (reinstated, entombstoned, marked
noClaim, merged) and the client decides what to say.

* Agreed behavior
** The model, stateless
Everything below is decided from the buffer and the truth as
they are now, never from what they were at an earlier save.
*** inputs and output
The refresh takes a validated viewforest (the parse already
minted a gnid for every new headline and gave every headline
a repo; validation refused a buffer with two editable
occurrences of one gnid, 'Multiple_Defining_Viewnodes'), the
truth, the mode, the node budget, and, in the diff view, the
straddle and the readiness store. It returns a viewforest and
the list of corrections. Edit requests pass through it
untouched; view requests are fulfilled and dropped.
*** the kinds
Occurrence, ghost, unknown, tombstone, folder, property
member, change viewnode, buffer root. The first three stand
for a gnid; in code they are the three cases of 'Vognode'.
*** the ID rule
For a headline that stands for a gnid, the refresh asks four
questions in order and stops at the first yes. Does the truth
have the node? Then the headline is an occurrence. Otherwise,
does the list of the headline's parent name this gnid? Then it
is an unknown. Otherwise, is the mode the diff view, and does
HEAD have the node? Then it is a ghost. Otherwise it is a
tombstone. A hidden node answers no to every question: the
truth answers 'hidden' rather than 'present', and the gated
goal names no hidden member; so its headline is entombstoned,
whatever text it carried.
*** the two flags of a child
Claim and warrant are independent. Under an editable parent
at rest they coincide: the refresh marks 'noClaim' on every
child it keeps that is unwarranted, and the next save makes
every claim warranted. They differ only for what the user
typed since the last save: a new child (claims, not yet
warranted) and a child the user marked 'noClaim' to detach it
(warranted, no claim). Under a write-protected parent the
claim is always no and nothing is written; the warrant is
computed and written as for any child.
*** unsayable: the ancestry table
'required_ancestry' ('server/update_buffer/ancestry.rs')
gives each folder kind the chain of ancestor kinds it may
stand under. It will gain rows for the property members
(under their property folder) and for every change viewnode
(under an occurrence or a ghost, as the diff view draws
them), and its 'HistoricalPhantom' row will say 'Ghost'. A
headline whose kind allows no viewparent of the kind it has
is unsayable and is entombstoned; its children stay under the
tombstone. A change viewnode whose change no longer exists is
unsayable too. An occurrence is never unsayable.
*** the ladder, per parent, in order
A parent's own kind is settled before its children are
visited; the walk is level order from the roots. For each
parent P with children C:
**** 1. re-kind
Each child that stands for a gnid becomes what the ID rule
says, where it stands, keeping its vnid, fold state, children
and, for an occurrence, its editability. Each child that
describes its viewparent (a folder, a property member, a
change viewnode) is entombstoned if the ancestry table
forbids its position or, for a change viewnode, if the change
is gone.
**** 2. merge
Siblings standing for one gnid are unified, in every sibling
set, content and folders alike (IDs are unique in each list,
which 'DuplicatedContent' validation guarantees, so a merge
never edits a list). The first in position survives; it is
editable if any was; it keeps its vnid and fold state; the
others' children are appended to it, and each appended child
is marked 'noClaim' unless the truth warrants it under the
survivor, whatever the survivor's editability.
**** 3. the goal and the warrant
The goal is computed for P (see "goal"). Each child's warrant
is set: 'ByGraph' when the truth's part of the goal names it,
'ByHead' when only HEAD's part does, 'No' otherwise.
**** 4. claims
If P is an editable occurrence or an editable folder, every
unwarranted child that claims membership is marked 'noClaim'.
If P is write-protected, no claim atom is written for any
child.
**** 5. reinstate
Every goal item without a child is drawn, within the extent
rule: all items for a folder and for an editable occurrence's
content (each occurrence whose content is drawn charging the
budget once, as today; when it is spent, newly drawn
occurrences are write-protected); no items for a
write-protected occurrence's content or for a chain. A
reinstated item becomes an
occurrence, an unknown or a ghost by the ID rule. Under a
folder, each reinstated member that is an occurrence carries
its containerward chain, as today; a chain node under a
member is write-protected and, by the rule above, is never
reinstated once drawn.
**** 6. reorder
Where the goal has an order (content; aliases; the outbound
folders subscribee, overridden and hidden-from-subscribers),
the warranted children take that order and every other child
stays immediately after the sibling it followed. Where the
goal has no order (the folders of recorders), nothing moves.
**** 7. substitution
In a content view an overridden node is shown as its
overrider with '(overridesHere ID)', as today, except at a
root, in a write-protected folder and in an overridden folder.
When the override relationship is gone, the shown headline
loses 'overridesHere', becomes unwarranted (and so, under an
editable parent, 'noClaim'), and the overridden node is
reinstated as a member beside it. The diff view substitutes
nothing, as today.
**** 8. requests
A view request on a child is fulfilled at the child's visit
and dropped: 'editableView' makes it the node's editable
occurrence (first wins among occurrences the refresh itself
draws; the input has at most one) and extends its content;
'writeProtectedView' and 'writeProtectedSubtreeView' make it
write-protected, alone or with its content subtree; 'flags'
and '(folder REL)' add the folder, filled from the goal;
'tombstone' (new; diff view only) entombstones a ghost. 'fork'
is the save's, as today. An edit request is carried through
unchanged unless the child's new kind has no slot for it
(a 'delete' on what became a ghost or a tombstone), in which
case it is dropped and reported as a correction.
*** editability and the budget
The input has at most one editable occurrence per gnid; the
refresh asserts this ('debug_assert') and does not re-check.
Among the occurrences the refresh draws, the first drawn is
editable only when its parent is editable content and the
budget allows; a repeat is write-protected. The "yield" rule
(a non-claimant giving its claim to a member met later) is
dropped: the user asks for editability where they want it.
*** restriction
The refresh sees the gated graph; no rule inside it mentions
the restriction. The graph's one addition is the three-valued
answer 'present', 'hidden', 'absent' for a gnid, so that a
hidden node's occurrence can be entombstoned rather than
mistaken for a vanished one. A tombstone reveals nothing of
what stood there; there is one kind of tombstone.
*** ghosts and the diff view
In a content view the goal is the truth's alone and no ghost
is drawn or kept. In the diff view the goal also holds what
HEAD alone warrants: a ghost for a node HEAD has and the
worktree lacks, under the parent that contained it at HEAD,
with HEAD's title and body; and a ghost warrant on an
occurrence whose relationship HEAD had and the worktree
lacks. A ghost is retained in place until the user
entombstones it ('tombstone' view request, stamped by the
client commands 'skg-request-ghost-tombstone' on one and
'skg-request-buffer-ghosts-tombstone' on every ghost in the
buffer), and it becomes an occurrence again, in place, when
its node is back. Every ghost accepts 'restoreNode'; an
occurrence with a ghost warrant accepts 'restoreRel'. There is
no permanence: every ghost is in HEAD.
*** change viewnodes
In the diff view each occurrence's change viewnodes are
recomputed in place from the straddle: kept while the change
exists, created when missing, entombstoned when the change is
gone (the tombstone holds whatever the user drew under one).
The three kinds of change, the leaves and the readiness are
written as '(diff ...)' facts, as today; folders carry no
'changed' spelling, since the clients compute the folder's
label from its members' facts.
*** the tail
After the walk, once: compute the derived facts (the props,
rels and birth of each occurrence; 'cycle'; 'repoBoundary';
'relRepoRaised'; 'omittedBody'; each child's warrant atom);
prune every childless tombstone, roots included, so a buffer
may end with no roots; assign a vnid to every viewnode that
lacks one; render.
*** a fresh render, and the search buffer
A fresh content view is the refresh of a stub: one root per
requested node, an editable occurrence with a '(folder
containers)' request. The diff view's first drawing is the
refresh of its stub over the straddle. The search buffer's
enrichment is the refresh of a stub whose roots are the hits
as write-protected occurrences, each with a '(folder
containers)' request; the two-phase protocol with the client
(results, then enrichment) is unchanged.
*** the fixpoint
For every buffer b, refresh (refresh (b)) equals refresh (b)
up to vnid assignment. 'tests/idempotence' will assert it over
the fixtures of every slice from 9 on.

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
Facts about the code as it stands, with file and function,
that an agent would otherwise rediscover or trip over.
** validation already guarantees one editable occurrence
'contradictory_instructions.rs' (under
'server/from_text/buffer_to_viewnodes/validate_tree/') refuses
a buffer with two defining occurrences of one gnid
('Multiple_Defining_Viewnodes'), and 'validate_tree.rs'
refuses two editable-view requests for one gnid
('MultipleEditableViewRequestsForSameId'). The refresh may
assume one editable occurrence per gnid in a saved buffer,
and every other input it gets is the server's own output. Do
not re-check; assert.
** the save reads claims only under editable parents
'server/from_text/local_fieldintent_collection/traverse.rs'
collects a parent's list from the children that claim
membership, and only when the parent is editable
('is_editable'); it pushes an unknown's gnid into the list
("its raw ID is load-bearing") and passes over a ghost. The
shown overrider's 'overridesHere' is read through
'collected_skgid'. The save never collects the members of a
write-protected folder. So slice 0's rule "no claim atom
under a write-protected parent" changes nothing the save
does.
** IDs are unique in each list
'DuplicatedContent' ('BufferValidationError') refuses a node
with two content children of one gnid, and the save's lists
are sets of IDs. So the merge rule never edits a list.
** the deleted set lives in three files
'deleted_by_this_save_pids' and 'deleted_by_this_save_extra_ids'
are fields of 'RerenderAfterSaveContext'
('server/update_buffer.rs'), read in
'server/update_buffer/complete.rs' and documented on 'Phantom'
in 'server/types/viewnode.rs'. Nothing else reads them.
** the straddle is lazy and the diff view's
'current_graph_straddle_in_slot'
('server/graph_straddle/build.rs') rebuilds the before-graph
