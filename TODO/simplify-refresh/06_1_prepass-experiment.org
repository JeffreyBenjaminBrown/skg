#+title: Skipping the switch step: an experiment
* The question
A skgrepo-set switch runs 'convert_and_prune_for_skgrepo_switch'
(server/update_buffer/skgrepo_switch.rs) on each open view before
the ordinary refresh. Review item 1 in
05_1_review-reports/duplication.org says the refresh does all of
it already, except perhaps pruning an editable folder the step
emptied. Which tests change when the step is skipped, and how?
* Short answer
- No test changes. The whole nextest suite passes with the step
  skipped: 956 of 956, the same as the baseline.
- The buffers do change. With the step skipped, an editable
  folder that the switch empties stays, empty. That is the
  difference the review predicted, kind (a). No case of kind (b)
  turned up.
- That empty folder matters in one case I probed (not in the
  suite): an empty hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder, saved under
  the new restriction, deletes the hidden member from the
  subscriber's hidesFromSubs. With the step, the folder is pruned
  and the save keeps the member. So the refresh alone does not
  cover the switch yet; the suite has no test that would notice.
* What I ran and what I only read

  its own target directory. Both are deleted now.
- The full suite, unchanged, as the baseline (nextest).

- The four tests that drive the switch handler, before and after

- Three throwaway probe tests with my own fixture (below).

* The edit
In set_skgrepo_restriction (serve/handlers/skgrepo_sets.rs), the
prepass argument to 'prepare_rerender_views_with_runtime' went
from 'Some (&prepass)' to 'None'. Nothing else in the server
changed. The step's three unit tests (tests/unit/repo_switch.rs)
call the step directly, so this edit cannot affect them; they
passed both times.
* Which tests drive the switch
Grepping tests/ for the handler, the step and 'restrict' finds
these as the only callers of 'handle_skgrepo_set_request':
- hidden_nodes::a_save_under_a_restriction_keeps_the_hidden_
  members
- hidden_nodes::a_tightened_restriction_leaves_a_tombstone_over_
  the_hidden_nodes_children
- skgrepo_sets::override_substitute_across_skgrepo_switch_
  anonymizes_and_keeps_original
- viewnode_id_stability::viewnode_ids_survive_a_save_a_request_

- skgrepo_sets::all_tests, through
  skgrepo_set_switch_rerenders_views_and_cancels_stale_search_
  enrichment (an empty view, switched to "all": the step has
  nothing to do there)
Many other tests mention 'restrict', but they render or save
under a restriction and never switch; the full-suite run covers

** How the switched buffers differ
I compared the captured buffers before and after the edit. Apart
from random UUIDs of newly typed nodes, they differ in one
place: the hidden_nodes fixture, where r subscribes to the
private hs.
*** a_save_under_a_restriction_keeps_the_hidden_members
The view opened under "all" has a subscribeeFolder (vnid 0b)
holding hs. Switched to "public" (metadata trimmed):
#+begin_example
with the step:
 * (vnid 0a) node (gnid r) ... r edited
 ** (vnid 0d) node ... a typed child
 ** (vnid 0e) node (gnid a) ... a
 ** (vnid 0f) tombstone
 *** (vnid 0g) node (gnid s1) ... s1
 ** (vnid 0h) node (gnid b) ... b
without the step: the same, plus, after r,
 ** (vnid 0b) subscribeeFolder
#+end_example
The test still passes. It checks for no trace of hs and h, and
then saves, keeping hs in r.subscribesTo either way.
Classification: (a), an emptied editable folder that the step
pruned and the refresh keeps.
*** a_tightened_restriction_leaves_a_tombstone_...
Same difference: an empty subscribeeFolder (vnid 0b) under r
after the switch to "public". Switched back to "all", the buffers
differ more:
- With the step, the folder is gone, so the switch back does not
  bring it back, and hs is not drawn.
- Without the step, the kept folder refills:
#+begin_example
 ** (vnid 0b) subscribeeFolder
 *** (vnid 0h) node (gnid hs) ... hs secret
#+end_example
  So h, drawn again beside the tombstone, gets vnid 0i, not 0h.
The test asserts only that h is drawn and the tombstone stays,
so it passes. Classification: (a), plus the round-trip
consequence.

override_substitute_... and viewnode_ids_survive_...: identical

** What agrees
- Every occurrence of a hidden node is a tombstone or gone in
  both: hs, hh, ho pruned; h a tombstone holding s1.
- h's aliasFolder and its alias are gone in both. In the
  refresh, a folder or property member under a tombstone is
  unsayable, so the ladder entombstones it ('rekind_children')
  and the tail prunes it.
- The unknown missing-priv, recorded by h, is gone in both. In
  the refresh, the tombstone parent has no goal, so the ID rule
  answers Tombstone.
- r's hiddenFolder (non-editable, held hh) is gone in both: the
  tail's 'folder_goes_when_empty' prunes it.
- No kind (b) difference: nothing hidden is drawn in either.
** What differs: three emptied editable folders
subscribeeFolder, hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder (inside it)
and overriddenFolder stay, empty. Kind (a).
Switched back to "all", the refresh-alone buffer refills all
three (hs, hh, ho drawn again). The step's buffer has lost them.
** An aside, the same in both
The unknown missing-pub (recorded by r, in public) is drawn
under "all" and gone under "public" in both variants. I did not
look into why.
** Saving the switched buffer: the hide is lost
A second probe, on a fresh copy of the fixture each time: switch
to "public", edit r's and c's titles, save under "public", read
the graph.
#+begin_example
with the step:
  r.subscribesTo [hs]  r.hidesFromSubs [hh]  c.overrides [ho]
the refresh alone:
  r.subscribesTo [hs]  r.hidesFromSubs []    c.overrides [ho]
#+end_example
So the empty subscribeeFolder and overriddenFolder save without
harm; the save keeps their hidden members. The empty
hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder deletes hh from hidesFromSubs.
** Control: a view drawn under "public"
A third probe opened r under "public", requested its
subscribesTo and hidesFromSubs folders and c's overrides
folders, saved, edited, saved again. The view shows an empty
subscribeeFolder and an empty overriddenFolder, but no
hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder. hidesFromSubs keeps hh. So
empty editable folders are normal under a restriction, and the
lost hide comes from the empty hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder,
which only the step-less switch produced here.
* Why the tail does not prune these folders (read)
'prune_childless_tombstones' (refresh/tail.rs) prunes, postorder,
every childless tombstone and every childless folder for which
'folder_goes_when_empty' (refresh/ladder.rs) holds. That is any
folder that is neither editable ('Folder::is_editable': the
subscribee, overridden, hiddenOutsideOfSubscribee and alias
folders) nor a flags folder. Its comment gives the reason: the
user could add members to these, and the save reads them. So the
refresh keeps them on purpose. A view drawn under a restriction
shows them empty, as the control shows.

The step prunes a folder of any kind, those included, when its
own prune emptied it ('should_prune', 'is_leaf && had_children').
That covers both review exceptions: the editable folders and the
flags folder. No probe emptied a flags folder.
* Why the hide is lost (read; matches the run)
'apply_hiddenoutside_edits' (resolve_visibility.rs) treats the
submitted hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder as the whole of the
replaceable hides. A hide is replaceable when the restriction
holds its relRepo ('restriction . contains_skgrepo
(&member . relRepo)'). hh is homed in private, but r records the
hide in public (relRepo public), so it counts as replaceable.
The submitted folder is empty, so hh is unhidden. The filter
looks at where the relationship is recorded, not at whether the
member is visible. The subscribesTo and overrides saves evidently
keep hidden members some other way; I did not trace how.

Whether this is a save bug, separate from the switch, depends on
whether a buffer can otherwise carry an empty
hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder while the subscriber hides a
hidden node, for instance one the user typed. I did not test
that.
* What I could not determine
- Whether some other editable-folder case loses data the same
  way: the alias folder of a visible node whose alias the
  restriction excludes (I found no easy way to record an alias
  in another skgrepo), or a flags folder.
- Why missing-pub goes under "public" (it does with and without
  the step).
- Whether keeping the empty folders is wanted. It makes a switch
  and its reverse round-trip (the folders refill), but it shows
  structure the view drawn fresh under the restriction would not
  show, and it triggers the lost hide above.
* What follows for review item 1
The refresh covers the entombstoning and the tombstone prune. It
does not cover pruning emptied editable and flags folders. Before
the step can go, one of these is needed:
- the refresh prunes an editable folder that the switch emptied
  (it would have to know what the folder held before), or
- the save keeps hidden hides when given an empty
  hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder, and empty editable folders
  after a switch are accepted.
Either way, a regression test is needed: switch, save, check
hidesFromSubs. The suite has none.
