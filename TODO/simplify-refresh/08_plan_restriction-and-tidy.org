* Words this plan uses
The glossary defines the refresh and its vocabulary. The words
below are new in this plan, or change with it.
** gnid-only vognode, 'GnidOnlyVognode', 'GnidOnlyKind'
A vognode that stands for a gnid and shows nothing of a node:
today two kinds, the unknown (the gnid resolves to no node)
and the Restricted vognode (the node is restricted). After
slice 0 they are one struct, 'GnidOnlyVognode', with a field
'kind : GnidOnlyKind { Unknown, Restricted }' naming why it
shows nothing; the wire atoms 'unknown' and 'restricted', the
heralds and the parser's refusals stay. The struct's name is
the printer's, which already used it for a borrowed view of
the pair; a comment on the struct says the kind is not named
"unshowable", since "unsayable" already names a descriptor
standing where its kind may not, and says the two reasons a
gnid's node cannot be shown. In prose, "a gnid-only vognode";
in user text, "a restricted headline" or "a reference to an
unknown node", never the type's name.
** record, visible, restricted
A record is a relationship as one node's file states it, in
one skgrepo's section; it is visible when the restriction
holds that skgrepo. A node is restricted when the restriction
keeps its home skgrepo out. A member of a goal is visible when
its record and its home both are. After slice 3 the code asks
the two questions by two methods of 'SkgrepoRestriction',
'record_is_visible' and 'node_is_restricted', and by nothing
else.
** buffer correction policy
What the caller of a refresh tells it about which changes are
not buffer corrections. After slice 3 it is an enum of the
situations a refresh is called in: 'ReportAll'; 'AfterSave',
with the gnids the save deleted and the gnids its nodeMerge
requests touched; 'AfterRestrictionChange'; and 'Unread', for
a buffer nobody reads corrections of. "Unread" is coined here:
it says the refresh corrects as always and nobody is told.
** orphan graphnode, orphan viewnode
"Orphan" always takes its noun: an orphan graphnode is a node
that no list contains; an orphan viewnode is a headline the
parser cannot place under a parent (deeper than its parent
allows). The weave's "orphan run" and the database's "orphan
record" keep their nouns.
** 'RerenderMode'
The two situations a rerender pass runs in, a content view or
a buffer of the diff view with its buffer kind, built from the
view being rerendered. It replaces 'RerenderOptions', which
repeated the variants of 'RefreshMode' under another name.
** the viewnode merge, 'viewnode_merge', 'ViewnodeMerged'
The second rung, which merges duplicate viewnode siblings. Its
function, its buffer correction kind and its test file take
that one name after slice 6; a comment on the function says
that only siblings standing for one gnid under one parent are
ever merged.
** How to say it in messages
A message to the user says "a restricted headline" or "a
reference to an unknown node", never the type; "orphan
graphnode" with its noun; "reads no claims" for a parent whose
claims the save does not read; "buffer correction", never
bare.

* Agreed behavior
What the code does when the plan is complete. Behavior not

** One gnid-only kind (slice 0)
Today 'Vognode::Unknown' and 'Vognode::Restricted' have the same
five fields, about 37 sites match them in paired arms, the
vognode is built in four places, and the two have drifted in
validation (a 'noClaim' unknown counts in the distinctness
checks and a 'noClaim' Restricted vognode does not; the
relation-folder check admits a Restricted vognode in every
folder and an unknown in the overridden folder only). After
slice 0 one struct, 'GnidOnlyVognode', will hold the five
fields and its kind, 'claims' and 'set_claim' will be methods
of the viewnode, the ID rule will answer 'GnidOnly (kind)' in
place of its two answers, every paired arm will be one arm,
and the two drifts will be one rule: a gnid-only vognode
counts in the distinctness checks when it claims, and every
relation folder admits one. 'ViewnodeMetadata' will keep the
parse's kind as 'kind : MetadataKind' with each kind's own
parts beside it, and 'viewnode_from_metadata' will be one
match on it. The containerward chain's builder will draw each
chain node by the ID rule and the refresh's constructors,
write-protected, with no disk read; 'graphnode_and_viewnode_
from_gnid' will go with its last caller.
** A view whose root the restriction keeps out (slice 1)
Today a narrowing that keeps out the node a view is rooted at
makes the root a tombstone, prunes its childless subtree, and
leaves the view empty; widening the set again brings nothing
back, since the ID rule's Restricted answer needs a parent's
goal to name the gnid and a root has none. On a copy of Jeff's
data the narrowest set emptied every large view for good.
After slice 1 a root the restriction keeps out will become a
gnid-only vognode of kind Restricted, since the view is the
user's own request and its gnid reveals nothing they did not
ask for, and an occurrence again when the set widens, in
place, with its children claiming.
** The diff view under a restriction (slice 1)
Today the diff view opens only under 'all', but a switch
rerenders its open buffers with an ungated straddle: a deleted
private node's ghost shows HEAD's title, and a restricted
member with a visible record is omitted. After slice 1 the
ghost question will ask the restriction about HEAD's node's
home, so that a restricted node is never a ghost anywhere, and
the diff view's goal will keep a restricted member with a
visible record as a gnid-only vognode of kind Restricted with
its change facts and no change viewnode under it, since its
old text would reveal the node. In a diff-view buffer, as in a
content view, the user will then see "restricted" and a gnid
where an occurrence with its title stood before the switch.
** An alias recorded in a restricted section (slice 1)
Today such an alias shows, text and all, under the restriction,
and the props count includes it. After slice 1 the property
goal will omit an alias whose record is restricted, as it
omits a relationship's, and the count will not include it.
** The dangling hide (slice 2)
Today a requested hidden folder holding a gnid-only vognode of
kind Unknown (a hide of a gnid that resolves to no node,
recorded in a visible section) makes the save refuse the
buffer, with a spurious clause about 'noClaim' in the message.
After slice 2 every relation folder will accept a gnid-only
vognode (the rule of slice 0), and the refusal, where it still
applies, will lose the clause.
** A headline typed under a tombstone (slice 2)
Today a bare headline typed under a tombstone is refused for
want of a skgrepo, since the inheritance of a skgrepo stops at
a tombstone. After slice 2 the inheritance will pass through a
tombstone to the nearest occurrence above, so that the
headline is saved as an orphan graphnode and warned of as one
typed under a write-protected occurrence is. The warning will
say "created an orphan graphnode" and will not say "not yet
saved" of a node the same save created.
** The buffer correction policy (slice 3)
Today the policy is a struct of a set and a bool consulted
where corrections are recorded, and four callers that draw a
buffer nobody reads corrections of pass the struct that
reports everything and discard the corrections. Three changes
of the user's own doing are reported: a nodeMerge's four
corrections (the merge of the acquirer's two occurrences, and
the members drawn after an acquisition); a gnid-only vognode
whose relationship a save removed, entombstoned and reported
by a vnid the same refresh prunes; and, after a narrowing, a
member whose record alone is restricted, reported "Marked
noClaim". After slice 3 the policy will be the enum of "Words
this plan uses"; the save's rerender will pass the gnids its
deletions and its nodeMerge requests touched, so that their
consequences are quiet; a narrowing's "Marked noClaim" for a
member whose record alone is restricted will be quiet under
'AfterRestrictionChange'; the three callers that draw a
buffer's first view will pass 'Unread' and discard nothing;
the integrate request, which appends roots and refreshes
twice, will pass 'Unread' for its first refresh and
'ReportAll' for its second, so that what the user's buffer got
wrong is still told; and the tail will drop any correction
whose headline it pruned. Every other correction is reported
as today.
** The restriction's two questions (slice 3)
Today the code asks "is this record visible" and "is this node
restricted" under eight names across the save and the refresh,
one of which ('member_is_visible') contradicts the glossary's
"visible member", and one of which ('restriction_keeps_out')
also says yes for an absent gnid; the save's spellings fall
back to the disk where the render side asks the graph alone.
After slice 3 the two methods of "Words this plan uses" will be
the only spellings, each taking the restriction as it is so
that no caller wraps an 'Option', and the disk fallback will
stay only where a caller shows it needs one.

** The flag request and the readiness block (slice 4)

** The switch and unsaved edits (slice 4)

** The clients fully aligned (slice 5)

what Neovim cannot do.
** The refresh renders nothing (slice 6)
Today the tail computes the derived facts, prunes, mints the
vnids and renders, and the search reruns the prune and renders
again after folding and excluding; the integrate path drops a
render. After slice 6 the tail will stop at the vnids and
return the forest with its corrections, and each caller will
render once when it is done; the search will still prune a
second time after its exclusion, by the same function.
** Editability during the walk (slice 6)
Today 'Walk' holds three sets ('editable_gnids',
'drawn_editable', 'had_editableView_request') updated by hand
at eight sites, and 'charges_node_budget' asks whether an
occurrence was made editable by this refresh's request, since

'charges_node_budget' will lose its second parameter; and the
only bookkeeping left will be which occurrence holds a gnid's
editability, one map from gnid to the position of that
occurrence.

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
** the straddle is not gated
'GraphStraddle' holds HEAD and the worktree under no
restriction; the ID rule's ghost question reads
'straddle . before . pid_of' ('server/refresh/id_rule.rs')
with no look at the node's home, and the diff view's goal is
built with 'restricted : false' ('server/refresh/diff_view.rs',
the comment "the diff view runs under no restriction"). Slice
1 gates both; it does not gate the straddle itself, which the
stage-and-commit path reads whole.
** a root has no parent goal
The ID rule's Restricted answer needs the parent's goal to
name the gnid; the buffer root has no goal, so a restricted
root falls to the tombstone answer. Slice 1 adds the root case
to the rule, not a special case elsewhere.
** a switch rerenders the diff view, which opens only under 'all'
'prepare_rerender_views_where' ('server/serve/
rerender_stream.rs') rerenders every open view, diff-view
buffers included, on a switch.
** the relation-folder validation lists its kinds by name
'server/from_text/buffer_to_viewnodes/local.rs' (the arms
around lines 351 to 379 at acf65e4e) says which kinds each
relation folder admits, and the message quotes the list. Slice
0's one rule rewrites those arms; slice 2's refusal text
follows.
** the skgrepo inheritance stops at a tombstone
'skgrepo_of_nearest_occurrence_ancestor' ('server/from_text/
buffer_to_viewnodes/add_missing_info.rs') passes through
ghosts, unknowns and folders and stops at a tombstone, a
property and the buffer root. Slice 2 lets it pass through a
tombstone and keeps the other stops.
** the orphan check sits beside the traversal
'orphan_warnings' and 'is_created_as_an_orphan'
('server/from_text/local_fieldintent_collection/mod.rs') read
claims through 'parent_reads_claims' and exclude the buffer
root; a root typed at top level is not an orphan and is not
warned of.
** four callers discard corrections
'render_initial_view' ('server/update_buffer.rs'),
'diffView_buffer_viewforest' ('server/diff_view/
pruned_change_view.rs'), 'draw_search_buffer'
('server/serve/handlers/text_search.rs') and the integrate
request ('server/diff_view/refresh.rs') pass
'BufferCorrectionPolicy::default ()' and drop the result.
Slice 3 gives the first three 'Unread', and the integrate
request 'Unread' for its first refresh and 'ReportAll' for its
second.
** the save knows its deletions and its merges
'SaveAffectedIds' ('server/serve/handlers/save_buffer.rs')
holds the save's deleted gnids; the nodeMerge requests are
read by the save ('server/nodeMerge/'), and their acquirer and
acquiree gnids are what slice 3 adds to the policy.
** the pass before the walk fulfills the text's requests
'fulfill_editableView_requests' ('server/refresh/mod.rs') runs
before the walk and write-protects the other occurrences of a
requested gnid, which may stand above the requester. Slice 6
keeps it, and makes the atom the carrier only for the cascade
and the drawn members.
** the editable-view request's cascade
'extend_requested_content' and 'draw_item' ('server/refresh/
visit.rs') give editability to claiming write-protected
children of a requested occurrence and to the members drawn
under it; 'extent.rs' and 'charges_node_budget'
('server/refresh/mode.rs') read 'had_editableView_request'.
Slice 6 replaces the set by the copied atom, consumed at the
child's visit.
** Neovim's edit DSL appends
'write_edit_request' ('nvim/lua/skg/metadata.lua') places the
form by 'parts' and then writes it through a DELETE plus a
merge whose 'apply_merge' appends an absent head; 'delete',

case file is run against the pure functions only
('tests/nvim/metadata_editor_cases_spec.lua'), which is why the

** the stats pass's quadratic site
