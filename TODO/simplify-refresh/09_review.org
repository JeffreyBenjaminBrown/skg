* Verdict
The plan did what it set out to do, and found one thing it
did not set out to find. Eleven slices in eight hours, 59
commits, every suite green at every slice's end but the one
failure older than the plan, eleven entries of the problems
file closed, the unknown and the Restricted vognode one kind,
the restriction's three leaks closed and its two questions
asked by two methods, the buffer-correction policy an enum of
four situations, both clients aligned module for module and
Neovim given the three commands it lacked, the refresh
rendering nothing and its editability carried by the request
atom, the restricted stats pass linear (4,000 children in
0.14 s where 2.85 s had been), every test index folder its
own per process, and the docs and glossary brought up to
date. The probes passed every behavior end to end but two,
and the reviewers grade most of plan 06's findings fixed.
What needs work is one data loss the plan's own test writing
uncovered and the probes drove through every variant: a
skgrepo narrowing then a skgrepo widening leaves a member's
'noClaim' where the record is visible again, and a save under
'all' then deletes the record, subscriptions and overrides
included, with nothing said; it predates the plan, and slice
3's silencing of the narrowing's marks made it quieter. The
fix is small and principled since slice 4 (Decide 1). The
review's own throwaway tests confirmed three defects more: an
editable occurrence written under an extra ID can lose its
editability unreported, the hidden-outside folder reads
restricted records, and a save under a skgrepo narrowing
deletes a restricted hide whose target the narrowing shows as
a subscribee's content, a second data loss (Decide 13). The
search that loss prompted, against Jeff's principle that the
restriction limits what can be edited as well as seen, found
eleven writes that touch what it hides, four of them leaks
(Decide 14). Beside them: the restriction's questions came
back under new words at a dozen sites, the editable-view
request atom carries a second meaning the integrate request
filters around,
Neovim shows its refusals as Lua errors, and the docs lag the
restricted root and the diff view under a skgrepo restriction.
Decide 1, 13 and 14 are the corrective slices; 2 to 12 are
the next tidy, most of it cheap.

*** The words
**** graphnode and ID
A graphnode has one pid and any number of extra IDs; every one
of its IDs names it. A nodeMerge gives the acquirer the
acquiree's IDs as extra IDs.
**** viewnode and occurrence
A viewnode is one headline of a view. An occurrence is a
viewnode standing for a graphnode, written in the buffer under
one of the graphnode's IDs, the one the user typed or the
server printed.
**** the editability map
The walk keeps one map from an ID to the viewnode that holds
that ID's editability, so that a graphnode has at most one
editable occurrence. The pass before the walk fills it by pid;
the walk's own builder of the map keys it by the ID as written.
That mismatch is the defect.

**** 1. the subscribee-visibility step's hide: run; leaks, loses

**** 2. the fork's clone: run; leaks
'C-c m f' ('skg-fork-node'; ':SkgFork' on '<localleader>mf')

file, which sits in a visible skgrepo, the forked graphnode's

buffer gives, not the graph's, and its warning says the
graphnode was left unchanged.
**** 5. a delete's propagation: run; a silent restricted write
A delete request's cleanup strips the deleted ID from every
recorder's file, restricted sections included; the refusal that
guards restricted sections covers the deleted graphnode's own
sections only.
**** 6. a nodeMerge: run; a silent restricted write
A nodeMerge admitted because the two homes are visible moves
the acquiree's restricted records into the acquirer's
restricted section and drops a restricted hide.
**** 7. an edit of a visible headline: run; a silent write
