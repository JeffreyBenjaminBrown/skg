* Glossary
** How to read it
Each entry gives the word as the survey uses it, the code's name
for it in single apostrophes, and any other words the code or the
docs use for the same thing. The official glossary is
'docs/glossary.org'; where this one differs, this one describes
the code and that one the intent. Terms are grouped: the tree,
membership and editability, the reconciler vocabulary, the graph
side, requests, and the renders. A list of synonyms and a list of
names that mislead close the document.
** The tree
*** viewforest, viewnode
A *viewforest* is the server's tree of one buffer. Its nodes are
*viewnodes* ('Viewnode' in 'server/types/viewnode.rs'), each of a
*kind* ('ViewnodeKind'). The tree's invisible root is the *buffer
root* ('BufferRoot'); its children are the buffer's *roots*, also
called *view roots*.
*** viewparent, viewchild, subtree, leaf
Positions in the viewforest, as against parents and children in
the graph. A *leaf* here is a viewnode with no viewchildren (the
code says "view-leaf"); a *branch* is one with some. "Subtree"
means a viewnode with everything under it.
*** occurrence
One viewnode standing for a given graphnode. A buffer may hold
several occurrences of one node; the code also says *copy* and,
for a second one met on the walk, *repeat*.
*** vognode
A viewnode that stands for a graphnode ('Vognode'). Three kinds:
*unrestricted* ('Vognode::Unrestricted', payload
'UnrestrictedVognode', the ordinary node the view shows),
*restricted* ('Vognode::Restricted', an anonymous placeholder for
a node in a skgrepo the restriction hides), and *phantom*. The
code often says just "Unrestricted" for the first.
*** phantom
A vognode for a node that is not a current member of the graph
('Phantom'). *Deleted* ('Phantom::Deleted'): this save deleted
it; it keeps its last title and body. *Historical*
('Phantom::Historical'): only the diff view; HEAD has the node,
the worktree lacks it; shows HEAD's text. *Unknown*
('Phantom::Unknown'): an ID some list names that no node answers
to; the docs call the ID *dangling* and the viewnode a
*placeholder*. Phantoms are save-inert.
*** non-vognode
Every other viewnode: a folder, a property, a tombstone, the
buffer root. They describe their viewparent rather than stand for
a node.
*** folder
An org headline that groups viewnodes. A *partner folder*
('ViewnodeKind::PartnerFolder', twelve kinds named by their wire
atom: 'containerFolder', 'subscriberFolder', and so on) holds the
viewparent's partners in one relationship role. A *property
folder* ('ViewnodeKind::PropertyFolder': 'Alias', 'ID', 'Flags')
holds entries describing the viewparent itself. "Col" is a
retired word for folder.
*** member of a folder, entry of a folder
A viewnode standing in a folder for one of the folder's goal
items. A partner folder has *members* (vognodes); a property
folder has *entries* ('Property::Alias', 'Property::ID',
'Property::Flag'). The code's 'FolderGoalItem' is one goal entry
of a partner folder, with a 'FolderGoalShape': 'Member',
'Removed' or 'Historical'.
*** folder policy
'PartnerFolder::policy' gives each kind a 'FolderPolicy':
*EditableSet* ('subscribeeFolder', 'overriddenFolder': the save
writes its membership), *EditableFilter*
('hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder'), *WriteProtectedSet* (the
inbound folders, 'hiddenFolder', the two acquisition folders) and
*WriteProtectedFilter* ('hiddenInSubscribeeFolder'). "In-folders"
and "out-folders" ('IN_FOLDERS', 'OUT_FOLDERS' in
'server/diff_view/changes.rs') are the folders of the node's
recorders and of the relationships it records.
*** the two filter folders, the two acquisition folders
The *filter folders* are 'hiddenInSubscribeeFolder' and
'hiddenOutsideOfSubscribeeFolder', whose membership is derived
from hide state. The *acquisition folders* are the diff view's
'acquiredByFolder' and 'acquiredFolder', recording a nodeMerge;
they carry no chain and have no member role.
*** property
A non-vognode leaf under a node or in a property folder
('ViewnodeKind::Property'): an alias, an ID, a flag, the old
text, a body diff, a home-skgrepo move, a relRepo move, a
changeset marker, or a removed alias or ID.
*** tombstone
A viewnode with no title, ID or change facts, shown DELETED
('ViewnodeKind::Tombstone', atom 'tombstone'). It stands where an
unwarranted viewnode stood and holds that viewnode's children. To
*entombstone* a viewnode is to make it one. Not to be confused
with a *deleted phantom*, which keeps its node's text.
*** the containerward chain, chain node
Under each member of a partner folder hang its containers as
write-protected non-members, under those theirs, up to the roots
or 'max_roleTree_depth'
('server/to_org/extend/containerward_chain.rs'). The nodes so
drawn are *chain nodes*; a node met again on the walk is drawn
once more as a leaf ('ContainerwardRoleTree::Repeated'). A chain
node's metadata says nothing of it; it is known by position
('is_in_containerward_chain') or by shape
('is_drawn_chain_node'). The docs' *role tree* is the tree the
walk computes; "graft" is the retired word for a chain node.
*** fold state, focus
Per viewnode: 'org_folded', 'org_body_folded', 'focused'. A
reconciler keeps them on any viewnode it keeps; the server sets a
fold state only on a viewnode it creates.
*** viewnode ID, viewforest ID
A *viewnode ID* ('ViewnodeId', wire atom 'vnid') names one
viewnode for the buffer's lifetime; the view's 'ViewnodeIdRecord'
knows them and assigns new ones at the end of every completion.
The *viewforest ID* ('ViewforestId') names the buffer. "View ID"
in older prose means the latter.
*** metadata, change facts, display fact
The *metadata* is the s-expression on a headline. *Change facts*
('ChangeFacts') are what the diff view records on a viewnode: the
three kinds of change, the leaves a request may name, the ready
objects. They are *display facts*: the view derives them and a
save reads nothing from them. Never shortened to "facts".
** Membership and editability
*** member, non-member
A child that *takes part in its viewparent's membership*, so that
the save reads it as part of the relationship the position
implies (content under a node, a partner in a folder), is a
*member*; one merely drawn there is a *non-member*. In code the
field is 'membership : Membership' with 'Member' and 'NonMember';
the wire atom is 'nonMember'. Retired words for the same thing:
"indep" (still in 'mark_erroneous_content_children_as_indep'),
"ignored" and "parentIgnored" (still in comments in
'content.rs'), "affectsParent false", and "orphan" for a member
whose parent died, which the tail makes a non-member.
*** editable, write-protected
An *editable* occurrence is the one the save reads the node's
text and content from; every other occurrence is
*write-protected* ('Editability::Editable',
'Editability::WriteProtected'; 'is_writeProtected'). Herald ☮.
*** editability claim, editable map
The record, in the *editable map* ('EditableMap' in
'server/to_org/util.rs'), that one occurrence is the node's
editable one. A claim is *Final* ('Finalizable::Final') when an
editable-view request made it, *Tentative* otherwise. The map is
built empty at every completion; only an editable occurrence
claims. "Visited" is the map's parameter name.
*** editability rule, first wins, yield, take the claim
The rule that decides, at each occurrence the walk reaches,
whether it is editable. *First wins*: a later occurrence of a
claimed node is write-protected
('make_writeProtected_if_repeat_then_extend_editable_map'). A
non-member that holds a Tentative claim *yields* it to an
editable member met later, and is write-protected with its
content subtree ('take_claim_from_tentative_nonMember'); the
member *takes the claim*. 'EditabilityOutcome::ClaimedElsewhere'
and 'MadeFinal' are the two outcomes at an occurrence that
carries a request.
*** editable-view request, EVR, the cascade
'ViewRequest::Editable', the request of C-c s d: make this
occurrence editable and Final, and extend its content editable.
The code abbreviates it *EVR*. A Final occurrence hands the
request to each of its member content children at its visit; that
is the *cascade*.
*** node budget, extension
The *node budget* ('CompletionContext::node_budget', starting at
'initial_node_limit') counts *extensions*: one editable node's
content list, drawn whole. When it reaches zero, every later
occurrence but a root is left write-protected. A folder fills its
members for free.
*** write-protect request
'ViewRequest::WriteProtected' (C-c s w) and
'WriteProtectedSubtree' (C-c s W): make this occurrence
write-protected, alone or with its content subtree.
*** clobber
To reset a write-protected occurrence's title and home skgrepo
from the graph ('makeWriteProtectedAndClobber' in
'server/to_org/util.rs', 'clobberWriteProtectedViewnode' in
'server/to_org/complete/contents.rs'). The write-protected path
of the content extension does only this.
*** settled, cascade (the flags)
Two booleans the ordinary node's visit passes to the content
extension: *settled* says the editability rule already ran for
this occurrence; *cascade* says it is Final and should hand the
request down.
** The reconciler vocabulary
*** completion, view completion, complete
*View completion* is the whole walk ('complete_viewforest'). The
verb "complete" also names the older reconciler
('complete_managed_children', 'complete_content_children'), which
does not walk anything: it brings one node's managed children in
line with a goal list.
*** visit, node update, dispatch
A *visit* is what happens to one viewnode when the walk dequeues
it ('dispatch_node_update', the code's "node update"). To
*dispatch* is to pick the visit's rule by the viewnode's kind.
*** reconcile, reconciler
To *reconcile* children against a goal is to keep the matched,
create the missing and convert the rest. The docs recognise two
*reconcilers*: *the older one*, 'complete_managed_children'
('server/update_buffer/util.rs'), which the content list and
every folder use, and *the diff view's*,
'reconcile_children_with_shallowModelSubtree'
('server/diff_view/shallow_model_subtree.rs'), which the diff
view's step and the chain use. This report calls them the
*goal-list reconciler* and the *shallow-model reconciler*. Every
function named 'reconcile_*_children' wraps one of the two.
*** refresh
A word of comments and docs, not of function names: to refresh a
property folder or a node's text is to re-read it from the live
graph ('sync_unrestrictedVognode_from_disk', the alias, ID and
flags reconcilers). It is reconciliation with a trivial selector.
*** extend, fill, draw, build and integrate
To *extend* a view is to draw more of it. A folder *fills* its
members. The de novo helpers *build and integrate*
('build_and_integrate_folder', 'build_and_integrate_aliases',
'build_and_integrate_flags'): they create a folder by hand,
without a reconciler, when a request asks for one. This report
calls them the *fillers*.
*** goal list, goal item, orderkey
The *goal list* is what the graph says the children should be:
for content, the node's 'contains' in file order; for a folder,
its partners in one role. A *goal item* is one element
('FolderGoalItem' for folders); the goal-list reconciler calls
the item's key its *orderkey* ('Orderkey'), a misleading name: it
is the key, not an order.
*** model, shallow model subtree
The diff view's form of a goal: a scratch tree of what the
viewnode should hold, *shallow* because it holds other vognodes
but not what their own visits add. The *model children* are its
entries; a created child goes after its *model predecessor*.
*** managed child, selector
A reconciler *manages* the children its *selector* admits (the
'selects' parameter of the shallow-model reconciler; the closure
over kind and membership in the goal-list one). Unmanaged
children are left where they stand. The shallow-model reconciler
has a second predicate, 'converts_when_stale', for which selected
children it may convert when stale; the docs say the reconciler
*owns* those. Selected but not owned is possible there.
*** key, match key, collected skgid, what a child stands for
A child's *key* is what it is matched by: for an occurrence, the
node's ID as the live graph resolves it (an extra ID collapses to
its pid); for a property entry, the entry; for anything else, its
kind. Names for the same idea: 'match_key' and 'MatchKey' in the
shallow-model reconciler, 'Orderkey' in the goal-list one,
'collected_skgid' (the original's ID when an overrider is shown
in its place), and 'skgid_a_folder_child_stands_for', the folder
reconciler's selector and key in one.
*** adopt
To *adopt* a child is to keep one already standing for a goal
entry instead of creating another, with its viewnode ID and fold
state. Three functions do it:
'adopt_nonMember_children_that_are_content' (content),
'mark_goal_children_as_folder_members' (folders), 'adopted_copy'
(the shallow-model reconciler). The docs also say "matched" and
"kept" for the same event.
*** warrant, stale, duplicate, unwarranted, invalid
The graph *warrants* a child when the goal has its key. A *stale*
child's key is gone; a *duplicate* is a second child of a matched
key. Both are *unwarranted* ('Unwarranted::Stale',
'Unwarranted::Duplicate'); the goal-list reconciler's local name
for stale is 'invalid_skgids', and the docs say *extra copy* for
a duplicate.
*** convert
To *convert* an unwarranted child is to apply the one rule
('convert_unwarranted_child'): retain as non-member if it is an
unrestricted vognode with a subtree, else entombstone. Variants:
'convert_unwarranted_subtree' and
'convert_unwarranted_subtree_taking_along' (the diff view's,
which first converts what was made below the child for the same
model), 'convert_unwarranted_member_with_its_chain' (a folder's),
'convert_duplicate', 'convert_change_viewnodes',
'convert_nonmember_unknown_children_to_tombstones',
'convert_now_restricted_phantoms'.
*** retain as non-member
The outcome of converting an unrestricted vognode that holds
something: it stays, as a non-member, with its title, ID, subtree
and the editability it had, and loses its change facts
('Conversion::Retained'). A duplicate is also write-protected.
"Parked" is the retired word.
*** taking along, goes with
What the diff view's conversion converts below a stale child
before converting the child: the diff view's own viewnodes, a
folder's members, drawn chain nodes
('goes_with_a_stale_viewparent'). A note the user typed does not
go with; it keeps the child retained.
*** prune, self-deletable
The *prune* ('prune_self_deletable_when_empty') is the one place
a viewnode is detached during completion: postorder, every
childless viewnode of a *self-deletable* kind
('is_self_deletable_when_empty': tombstone, deleted phantom, the
write-protected partner folders, the two filter folders, the ID
folder), never a root. The code also says *sweep*. Nothing
deletes itself; the name is figurative.
*** reinstated, created
A goal entry with no child is *created*; for a write-protected
folder the warning calls that member *reinstated*
('RepairKind::ReinstatedMember'), since the user must have
deleted its headline.
*** repair, repair summary, completion warning, warning sink
A *repair* is any conversion or creation in a write-protected
folder, counted in a 'RepairSummary' (created, retained,
entombstoned stale, entombstoned duplicates) and reported as a
'CompletionWarning::FolderRepair' through the *warning sink*,
which only the just-saved view has.
*** kept child order
'KeptChildOrder::Unmoved': kept children stay, created ones go
after their model predecessor. 'KeptChildOrder::FollowsGoal':
non-members and converted children first, then members in goal
order. The latter is for lists whose order the save writes.
*** normalize
'normalize_relationship_backed_content_unknowns' and its folder
twin: a member whose node vanished while the relationship still
names its ID becomes an unknown phantom, before the reconciler
would otherwise entombstone or recreate it.
*** erroneous content children
The content reconciler's name for member children whose key is
not in the goal ('mark_erroneous_content_children_as_indep');
they are made non-members. By the time it runs, the goal-list
reconciler has converted them already.
*** generalized orphan, required ancestry, dispose
A folder is a *generalized orphan* when some ancestor in its
*required ancestry* (a table per folder kind, 'required_ancestry'
in 'server/update_buffer/ancestry.rs') is of the wrong kind,
typically because the node died. Such a folder is entombstoned at
its visit and its children are *disposed*
('dispose_orphaned_folder_child'): a member is converted, a
nested folder is left to die at its own visit. The comments call
this *death-leafward*. A *dead parent* is a deleted phantom or a
tombstone.
*** deletion preflight, deleted by this save
The first thing an ordinary node's visit does: if this save
deleted the node ('deleted_by_this_save_pids'), the occurrence
becomes a deleted phantom and the visit ends.
*** completion mode, diff-view mode
The *completion mode* is the one field of the completion context
that says which kind of view the walk completes: 'mode', of type
'CompletionMode' ('server/update_buffer/completion_mode.rs'),
either 'ContentView' or 'DiffView { straddle, buffer_kind }'. In
*diff-view mode* the two hooks in 'server/diff_view/visit.rs'
change each visit as the survey's "The diff view's part"
describes, and the folder reconciler reads the straddle. Five
methods answer what the mode allows:
'subscribeeFolder_has_own_reconciler', 'substitution_enabled',
'default_folders_allowed', 'charges_node_budget' and
'align_content_members_before_extension'. Until commit
'7d79a925' the mode was two option fields, 'straddle' and
'diffView_buffer_kind', Some together, which the code and the
docs called *the switch*; that word is gone. It is not the
skgrepo-set switch, which is a request that changes the
restriction and rerenders every open view.

The diff view's pass at an editable node, before the content
reconciler, ('align_content_members_with_straddle'): make members
whose relationship the worktree dropped non-members, adopt a
non-member standing for a content node, and turn a historical

*** the per-visit step, change viewnode, front
The *per-visit step* ('process_unrestrictedVognode_changes', "the
step") is what the diff view adds at the end of an editable
node's visit. A *change viewnode* is one it adds: old text, a
move, a changeset marker, a changed folder, a removed member. The
*fronts* ('is_front_change_viewnode') are the change viewnodes
that stand ahead of the content.
*** tag, unmark
To *tag* a member is to set its change facts
('tag_content_members', 'tag_root'); to *unmark* a property

('unmark_property_folders_except').
*** removed member
A non-member unrestricted vognode carrying '(rel removed)': the
one encoding of a relationship that HEAD has and the worktree
lacks ('is_unrestricted_removed_member'). The docs also say
*former member*. It counts as a member for the editability claim.
*** fresh node, default folders
A *fresh node* is one the completion creates; on a de novo render
('create_partnerFolders_for_fresh_nodes') or at an editable-view
request, it gets its *default folders*: a subscribee folder when
it subscribes, and the hidden-outside folder that goes with one.
*** override substitution, raw, shown raw
When a node is overridden, a content view shows the overrider in
its place (*substitution*), recording the original in
'overridesHere'. A node shown as itself is *raw*; it is *shown
raw* in a write-protected folder, an overridden folder, or at a
root ('is_overridden_shown_raw'). The diff view substitutes
nothing.
** The graph side
*** graph, in-Rust graph, graph snapshot, live graph
The *in-Rust graph* ('InRustGraph') is the index of the worktree.
A completion reads one *snapshot* of it ('graph_snap'), taken
once per save. Docs say *live graph* and the diff view says
*after-graph* for the same thing: the worktree.
*** HEAD, before-graph, straddle, union
The *before-graph* is the in-Rust graph built from each owned
skgrepo at HEAD. The *graph straddle* ('GraphStraddle { before,
after }') holds both; its *union walk* answers questions over
their union, each node and relationship with its *existence*:
'Added' (only after), 'Removed' (only before), none (both).
*** the three kinds of change, node change
A node's existence, its other properties ('(properties changed)',
herald M), and its relationship to the viewparent
('GraphStraddle::node_change'). A phantom never carries
'(properties changed)'.
*** change-forest leaf, aspect
The unit of readiness: one changed aspect of one node or
relationship. 'ChangedAspects' groups the leaves that mention a
node; the step reads them to decide what to draw for a node in C.
*** C, R, U, D, content origin
The pruned change view's sets ('prunedChangeView_sets_of_buffer')
are four: *C* the changed nodes, *R* the roots' ancestors on the
containerward walk, *U* the editable set (C, the walk, R), *D*
the roots in order, each a *content origin*. The order-only
buffer's sets are empty.
*** pid, skgid, extra ID, resolve, unresolved
A node's primary ID is its *pid*; it may carry *extra IDs*, which
*resolve* to the pid through the graph ('pid_of'). An ID that
resolves to nothing is *unresolved*; the docs say *dangling*. A
*raw* ID is one before resolution.
*** recorder, partner, member role
A relationship is recorded in one node's file, its *recorder*;
the other node is its *member*. A *partner* ('Partner') is the
other end of a relationship from a given node, with its existence
and relRepo. A folder's *member role* ('relation_member_role')
says which role its members play.
*** relRepo, home skgrepo
A relationship's *relRepo* is the skgrepo that records it; a
node's *home skgrepo* is the one holding its file. A *move* of
either is a change viewnode.
*** skgrepo restriction, gated, omit
The *skgrepo restriction* ('SkgrepoRestriction', chosen by the
skgrepo-set switch) hides nodes whose home is outside the chosen
set. Accessors ending in '_gated' drop what it hides;
'omit_restricted_members' drops them from a goal list. A
restricted node already shown survives as a restricted vognode.
** Requests
*** view request, edit request
A *view request* ('ViewRequest': 'Editable', 'WriteProtected',
'WriteProtectedSubtree', 'Folder', 'Flags', 'Fork') asks
completion to draw or change something and is consumed when
fulfilled. An *edit request* ('(editRequest ...)', the restore
requests, the readiness request) asks the save to change the
graph or the store; 'consume_edit_requests_after_save' clears
them all before completion.
*** readiness request, gesture, restore request
A *readiness request* marks change-forest leaves ready; a
*restore request* ('restoreNode', 'restoreRel') asks the save to
undo a deletion. Completion reads neither; it reads the store's
marks back as change facts.
** The renders
*** de novo render, stub
The first drawing of a content view ('render_initial_view'): a
*stub viewforest* of one root per requested node, each a plain
editable vognode with a '(folder containers)' request, completed
by the same walk with default folders on. The diff view's first
drawing completes a stub over the straddle
('complete_viewforest_with_graph_straddle').
*** rerender, saved view, collateral view
A *rerender* ('rerender_view') recompletes a standing viewforest.
After a save, the *saved view* is the buffer the user saved,
rerendered from its parse; a *collateral view* is any other open
view the save touched, rerendered from the server's copy.
*** rerender-all, skgrepo-set switch, prepass
*Rerender-all* recompletes every open view. The *skgrepo-set
switch* does the same after a *prepass*
('convert_and_prune_for_skgrepo_switch') that converts now-hidden
nodes and prunes what that empties.
*** the contexts
'RerenderAfterSaveContext' carries what the save established (the
graph snapshot, the deleted sets, the straddle slot).
'CompletionContext' carries one walk's state (editable map,
budget, flags, sink, mode). 'RerenderOptions' carries what
differs between the renders.
*** the tail, finish
'finish_viewforest': what runs after the walk and the prune for
every render, through the rendering of the text.
*** stats, heralds
'graphnodestats' are facts about a node from the graph (how many
containers, and so on); 'viewnodestats' are facts about a
viewnode's position (cycle, herald strings). Both are display
facts set by the tail.
** The search buffer
*** hit, search view
A *hit* is a node the search index matched; the *search view* is
the buffer of hits, registered as 'ViewforestId::SearchView' and
named "search:TERMS" to the client. Its hits are its roots.
*** phase 1, search results; phase 2, enrichment
The server answers a search twice: at once with the hits
('search-results', *unenriched*), and later with the hits plus
their folders and stats ('search-enrichment', *enriched*). The
*enrichment thread* computes the chains and stats in the
background and leaves them in the *enrichment slot*; the
*snapshot* is the buffer text the client sends back for the
second phase to work on.
*** suppression
A hit that an owned hit overrides, directly or through other
overrides, is dropped from the top level; if the owned hit
overrides it directly, it reappears in that hit's overridden
folder.
*** display limit, prominence
'SEARCH_DISPLAY_LIMIT' caps the roots; *prominence* is the score
multiplier that ranks roots, cycle roots and mentioned nodes
higher. Neither affects completion.
*** "search" as a skgrepo
The skgrepo name the enrichment writes on a chain node the index
does not know, so that the node renders at all.
** Synonyms and near-synonyms
*** words for "bring in line with the graph"
complete, reconcile, refresh, fill, extend, build and integrate,
sync, clobber, normalize, align. They differ in scope, not in
kind: every one computes what should be there and changes what
is.
*** words for "non-member"
non-member, 'nonMember', 'NonMember', indep, ignored,
parentIgnored, affectsParent false, orphan (when under a dead
parent). All one flag.
*** words for "the goal"
goal list, goal items, model, shallow model subtree, "what it
should hold", contains list (for content), partners (for a
folder).
*** words for "the key"
key, match key, orderkey, collected skgid, the ID a child stands
for, resolved ID, pid.
*** words for "keep a child that is already there"
adopt, match, keep, take, reuse. The docs settle on "adopt".
*** words for "a child nobody wants"
stale, invalid, unwarranted, extra copy, duplicate, erroneous
content child, orphan, dead.
*** words for the two outcomes
retained as non-member, 'Retained', parked (retired); tombstone,
entombstoned, 'ChildlessTombstone'; and for a vognode the save
deleted, deleted phantom, "deletednode"
('mutate_unrestrictedVognode_to_deletednode').
*** words for the graphs
live graph, after-graph, worktree, graph snap, 'runtime.graph',
'straddle.after'; HEAD, before-graph, 'straddle.before'.
*** words for the walk and its unit
completion, view completion, BFS, level order; visit, node
update, step (only the diff view's addition at the end of a
visit).
*** words for the search buffer's two answers
search results, phase 1, unenriched; search enrichment, phase 2,
enriched; and for the text the client sends back, snapshot,
buffer snapshot.
*** words that are NOT synonyms, though they look it
"deleted phantom" and "tombstone" (the first keeps the node's
text, the second nothing); "removed member" and "deleted node"
(the relationship went, or the node went); "reinstated" and
"adopted" (drawn anew, or taken back); "changeset marker" and
"commit-readiness marker"; "selected" and "owned" in the
shallow-model reconciler; "chain node by position" and "by
shape"; "the completion mode" (a field of the completion
context, once called the diff view's switch) and "the skgrepo-set
switch" (a request).
** Names that mislead
*** 'complete_managed_children'
A reconciler, not a completion. 'complete_content_children'
likewise.
*** 'reconcile_content_children' and its caller
The caller is 'extend_true_content_at_unrestrictedVognode'. The
first computes the goal and calls the reconciler; the second is
the whole content visit, write protection and clobber included.
"True content" means content as against folders.
*** 'mark_erroneous_content_children_as_indep' and a twin
The twin is 'mark_orphans_under_dead_parents_false'. Both make
members non-members; "indep" and "false" are retired names for
'nonMember'.
*** 'mark_view_roots_as_members'
Clears the 'nonMember' mark on roots; a root is a member of
nothing (your point under '20_review.org').
*** 'validate_membership_claims'
Validates nothing it reports; it silently flips claims a
write-protected parent does not support.
*** 'make_writeProtected_if_repeat_then_extend_editable_map'
Also takes the claim from a tentative non-member and makes a
write-protected occurrence's children non-members.
*** 'is_self_deletable_when_empty'
Nothing deletes itself; the prune does.
*** 'Orderkey'
The key, not an order.
*** 'server/to_org/complete/contents.rs'
Holds only the clobber of a write-protected viewnode.
*** 'ChildData', 'build_child_data'
The facts needed to create or reshape one folder member, read
from the live graph and disk, preferring an existing sibling's
title.
*** 'death-leafward'
The ancestry check: a folder dies when an ancestor did.
