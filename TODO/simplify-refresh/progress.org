*** Changes to the plan's envisioned order

Decision, 2026-10-26, by the orchestrating agent (Claude, Fable)
on the recommended option below: the printer keeps writing
'noClaim' wherever a child does not claim until slice 9, which

Slice 0 is therefore DONE with this one rule deferred.

*** Choices determining the future
**** the printer rule conflicts with the save's refusals
The slice says "the printer writes 'noClaim' only under an
editable parent", and the pitfall "the save reads claims only
under editable parents" concludes that this "changes nothing
the save does". That reads 'traverse.rs' only. Other readers
take the claim of a child of a write-protected parent:
- 'errors_for_writeProtected_occurrences'
  ('server/from_text/write_protected_edits.rs') refuses a save
  in which a write-protected occurrence has a child that claims
  ('MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence'), and compares a
  write-protected occurrence's partner folders with the graph
  by their claiming children ('partnerFolder_members_in_view'),
  excluding a removed member by its non-claim;
- 'is_in_containerward_chain' knows a chain node by its
  non-claim, and the folder reconcilers and the diff view use
  it;
- both clients' readiness walks traverse only children that
  claim.
A child with no atom reads as claiming. So once the printer
drops the atom under write-protected parents, the server's
own rendering is refused when saved back.
**** the evidence
A throwaway printer change that dropped the atom under every
write-protected occurrence, run against the whole Rust suite:
55 of 918 tests failed, 35 of them with
'MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence' on a buffer the server
had just rendered (for example the test
'the_removed_partners_buffer_saves_back_unchanged' in
'tests/pruned_change_view/buffer.rs').
The change was reverted; the log is
'tests/results/slice0/nextest-experiment-printer-rule.log'.
**** the options
- Recommended: keep printing 'noClaim' wherever a child does
  not claim until slice 9, where the ladder replaces the
  editability helpers, and decide there what replaces the
  refusals. The rest of slice 0 stands as committed.
- Or do it now: read every child of a write-protected parent as
  not claiming, whatever it carries; delete the refusal
  'MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence' (typing a child under
  a write-protected occurrence, and write-protecting one with
  members, then save silently; the child shows ⊥); judge a
  write-protected folder's members by '(rel removed)' instead
  of the claim; and find chain nodes by position.
