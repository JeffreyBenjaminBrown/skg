*** Choices determining the future
**** the printer rule conflicts with the save's refusals
The slice says "the printer writes 'noClaim' only under an
editable parent", and the pitfall "the save reads claims only
under editable parents" concludes that this "changes nothing
the save does". That reads 'traverse.rs' only. Other readers
take the claim of a child of a write-protected parent:
- 'errors_for_writeProtected_occurrences'
  ('server/from_text/write_protected_edits.rs') refuses a save
  in which a write-protected occurrence has a child that claims
  ('MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence'), and compares a
  write-protected occurrence's partner folders with the graph
  by their claiming children ('partnerFolder_members_in_view'),
  excluding a removed member by its non-claim;
- 'is_in_containerward_chain' knows a chain node by its
  non-claim, and the folder reconcilers and the diff view use
  it;
- both clients' readiness walks traverse only children that
  claim.
A child with no atom reads as claiming. So once the printer
drops the atom under write-protected parents, the server's
own rendering is refused when saved back.
**** the evidence
A throwaway printer change that dropped the atom under every
write-protected occurrence, run against the whole Rust suite:
55 of 918 tests failed, 35 of them with
'MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence' on a buffer the server
had just rendered (for example the test
'the_removed_partners_buffer_saves_back_unchanged' in
'tests/pruned_change_view/buffer.rs').
The change was reverted; the log is
'tests/results/slice0/nextest-experiment-printer-rule.log'.
**** the options
- Recommended: keep printing 'noClaim' wherever a child does
  not claim until slice 9, where the ladder replaces the
  editability helpers, and decide there what replaces the
  refusals. The rest of slice 0 stands as committed.
- Or do it now: read every child of a write-protected parent as
  not claiming, whatever it carries; delete the refusal
  'MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence' (typing a child under
  a write-protected occurrence, and write-protecting one with
  members, then save silently; the child shows ⊥); judge a
  write-protected folder's members by '(rel removed)' instead
  of the claim; and find chain nodes by position.

** Slice 4: the grammar's shape
*** What's done
The printer writes, and the parser reads, one flat shape:
'(vnid X)' first, the position atoms, the kind as a bare atom,
every part beside it at the top level, in any order on read.
'(gnid X)' replaces '(id X)'; '(skgRepos (nodeRepo R)
[(relRepo R)])' replaces '(repo R)' and the relRepo of the
node's viewStats, the unknown's viewStats and the alias's top
level. The parser classifies each element (position, kind or
part), checks the parts against the kind ('parts_of_kind') and
refuses the retired spellings naming 'METADATA_GRAMMAR'; the
legacy rejection of top-level 'id', 'repo', 'view', 'code' is
gone. 'fork.rs' writes its two headlines to the grammar
('fork_clone_headline', 'forked_original_headline'), without
the dead 'parentOverrides' ('TODO/problems.org' entry moved to
'TODO/DONE/problems.org'). 'metadata_canon.rs' puts a vnid
first. 'shared/metadata-fields.json' lost 'fields_at'. New
tests: 'tests/metadata_grammar.rs'.
*** Deviations from the plan's "how"
**** the kinds with a parameter keep their small forms
"Every kind a bare atom" names 'flag', 'oldText', the two moves
and 'changesetMarker', but the plan gives their parameters no
part to stand in. They keep '(flag F)', '(oldText ASPECT)',
'(homeSkgrepoMove (old R) (new R))', '(relRepoMove ...)' and
'(changesetMarker KIND)', the form's head being the kind, as
'(FOLDER changed)' stays until slice 5.
**** overridesHere moved to the top level now
It is a declared part, which the shape puts beside the kind,
and no later slice names its move, so it left '(viewStats ...)'
in this slice, written after 'writeProtected'.
**** a headline naming no kind is a node
As '(skg focused)' was before: a client marks a new headline
before it has metadata. So '(skg (diff (rel added)))' parses
now (a node with a diff); its refusal test became a

**** the herald table gained a WHEN directive
With the parts flat, the lens could no longer tell a node's

every rendering case renders as before. Both clients' lens
engines learned it. Slice 6 may keep or replace it.
**** the clients
Both clients read parts at the top level and find the kind by
its atom (looked up in 'shared/metadata-fields.json'); new
metadata is '(skg node (skgRepos (nodeRepo R)))'; the editors
show 'skgRepos' and 'nodeRepo' where 'repo' stood, the fields at
level 2 beside the kind. A fold command on a headline with no
metadata still writes a kindless '(skg folded)', which the
server reads as a node. The clients do not refuse the retired
spellings themselves; they no longer match them, and the server
refuses them.

*** What's done

'(movedLikeAncestor X)'). The readiness requests are '(ready
...)' and '(unready ...)' in '(editRequest ...)'. A folder is
always its bare atom. The parser skips '(view ...)' and
'(diff ...)' whole and refuses the retired spellings. The

*** Deviations from the plan's "how"
**** a lost alias, ID or unknown carries noClaim
With '(diff ...)' skipped, the save no longer saw the
'(rel removed)' by which it skipped a lost alias or ID, and a
dangling partner the worktree lost (the census of a throwaway

'tests/results/slice5/nextest-experiment-parser-skips-diff.log').
So 'PropertyMember::Alias', '::ID' and 'Unknown' gained
'claimsMembership', which the stats pass sets to "HEAD alone
does not warrant it" and the printer writes 'noClaim'; the
save reads the claim (alias reader, ID check, write-protected
folder comparison, and traverse, which now passes over an
unknown that claims nothing). 'shared/metadata-fields.json'
offers 'noClaim' on those three kinds.
**** where the property members' warrant lives
'isWarranted : Warrant' on each 'PropertyMember' variant, set
by the stats pass. 'ghostWarrant' is still derived from the
change fact '(rel removed)' ('Viewnode::is_ghostWarranted'),
until slice 11 gives 'Warrant' its 'ByHead'.
**** restoreRel: the parser no longer checks (rel removed)
The position check could not move to 'ghostWarrant' with
'(view ...)' skipped, and the server writes no ghostWarrant on
