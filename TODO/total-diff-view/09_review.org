****** TODO [Claude] the danger is real, in two forms, and the plan now names the one path
Yes. A validator that works out for itself what a folder should hold
would be a second copy of what the folder reconciler already works

one-liner: it reads the relation in the right direction (outbound

slice 0 of the plan now says: lift that computation out of

restriction, call it from the reconciler as before, and call the same
function from the validator, with the save's restriction. One path,
two callers.

The second form is the TDV's. A folder drawn in a TDV buffer lists
the union of both graphs, so under a write-protected occurrence it
can hold a member whose relationship exists only in the before-graph,

* Know: what you will meet while dogfooding
** Write-protecting is a request now, and it does not save
C-c s w writes the request and C-c s W its subtree form; like C-c s d
they leave saving to you, so that C-c s w on one occurrence and C-c s
d on another can go into one save, which is how editability moves.
The requests show the heralds 'req:write-protected' and
'req:write-protected-subtree' until the save, and both clients cycle
them in the viewRequests field.
** What write-protection does to children
The occurrence's direct children become non-members ('⊥'), keep their
own editability and subtrees; the subtree form does the same all the
way down. The same now holds for everything the server
write-protects: the draw rule's subtree, and an occurrence
write-protected because it repeats an editable one, so a pasted copy
of an editable subtree saves and comes back write-protected with
non-member children.
** When editability moves back, the children are adopted
A request on a write-protected occurrence whose non-member children
are its content makes them members again, keeping their fold state.
The same adoption ends the old double drawing of a content node
parked under an editable parent.
** What a save refuses under a write-protected occurrence
A member child (typed there), a changed title or home skgrepo, an
alias or flags folder that differs from the graph, and the two
requests together with an editable-view request on one occurrence
('WriteProtectAndEditableViewRequestsOnOneOccurrence'). Hand-editing
'writeProtected' into a headline with drawn members is refused by the
member rule. Nothing is normalized any more.
** A cascaded editable-view request loses to a write-protect request
The validator refuses the two on one occurrence when you write them;
when the cascade hands an editable-view request to a child that
carries your write-protect request, the write-protect request wins.
** The TDV follows U only when you ask
After a save, a changed node the buffer shows only as a
write-protected leaf stays so, and the summary counts it; C-c r u
makes it editable, appends every missing origin as a root, and
rerenders. The command refuses on a modified buffer ("Save the buffer
first."), outside the 'all' restriction, and on a buffer that is not
a TDV buffer. Dropped roots now stay dropped after a save and are
counted unshown until you ask.
** Neovim's chord table
Server messages that name C-c s d, C-c s w, C-c s s and C-c g RET show
the Neovim bindings; C-c s r stays verbatim, since Neovim has no
relRepo command.
