** Scope
Seventeen slices in two parts. Slices 0 to 9 are housekeeping: the
renames the post-mortem settled, the diff view's own documentation,
the glossary and user-doc corrections, the removal of copies, a few
small reifications, the client helpers, the missing tests, startup

10 to 16 change structure and behavior: the diff view gets a module of

relRepo moves. The parts may be run as one run or as two
('bash/run-plan-slices.sh 0 9', then '10 16'); the order of the slices
is the order they must land in.

* Words this plan uses
The glossary ('docs/glossary.org') defines most of these; the rest are
defined here, and slice 3 adds them to the glossary.
- The diff view (until this plan, "the total diff view", "TDV") is the
  view opened by C-c v d: up to four buffers, "all changes", "merges",
  "skgrepo changes" and "order-only changes", each drawn over the
  graph straddle. A buffer kind is which of the four a buffer is
  ('TdvBuffer' today, 'DiffViewBufferKind' after slice 0).
- The graph straddle ('GraphStraddle', 'server/graph_straddle/') holds
  the before-graph (the owned skgrepos as committed at HEAD) and the
  after-graph (the live graph) and compares them. A node's presence is
  Both, OnlyBefore or OnlyAfter. A partner of a node in some role is
  one entry of the union of the two graphs' lists, with its presence
  and its relRepo in each graph ('Partner'); 'Partner::changed' is
  true when the relationship exists in one graph only or its relRepo
  differs.
- C is the set of changed nodes; R, U and D are the sets of the pruned
  change-spanning viewforest (PCSVF) built over C ('PcsvfSets' in
  'server/pcsvf.rs'; the glossary entry "C, R, U, D"). C has two
  halves ('ChangedPids'): nodes whose own files record a change, and
  nodes whose set of containers, mentioners, subscribers, overriders
  or hiders changed. The second half is found from the first: for each
  recorder in the first half, every partner whose presence is not
  Both. A relRepo move puts only the recorder in C.
- A change-forest leaf ('ChangeLeaf' in 'server/change_forest.rs') is
  one change a user can mark commit-ready. Its key ('LeafKey') is
  either a node and an aspect of it ('NodeAspect': existence, text,
  home skgrepo, an alias, an extra ID, a flag) or a relationship (a
  relation, its recorder, its member) and an aspect of it
  ('RelationshipAspect': existence, relRepo). The recorder of a leaf
  is the node whose file records it. The type that holds every leaf,
  grouped by recorder, is 'ChangeForest' today and 'ChangeLeaves'
  after slice 0; "change-forest" stays the glossary's name for the
  mental picture (changed nodes, their changed aspects, the leaves).
- A mark is a user's declaration that a leaf is commit-ready, stored
  with the leaf's fingerprint in the readiness store
  ('server/readiness_store.rs', persisted to 'readiness-marks.json').
  A readiness request ('(readyRequest OBJECT...)' or
  '(unreadyRequest OBJECT...)' in a headline's metadata) is what a
  readiness gesture (C-c r r, C-c r h and their recursive forms)
  writes before saving; the save consumes it and marks or unmarks the
  leaves the headline names. The readiness summary is the save's
  count of ready and changed files.
- A diff object (until this plan, "axis") is what a '(diff ...)' fact
  or a readiness request names: the node itself, or the node's
  relationship to its viewparent; the wire atoms are 'node' and
  'rel', and a request may also say 'all', meaning every leaf of an
  aggregate one level down. A diff aspect is what a leaf is about
  (existence, text, home skgrepo, an alias, an extra ID, a flag,
  relRepo). The two diff objects are exactly the node and its
  relationship to its viewparent; a leaf about a relationship has the
  relationship as its object, never the member.
- An occurrence of a graphnode is a viewnode that stands for it (an
  unrestricted vognode). A buffer has at most one editable occurrence
  of a node; the others are write-protected, and the server draws no
  content under a write-protected one. A member is a child viewnode
  whose 'affectsParent' is true; a non-member has it false. Under a
  write-protected occurrence every child is a non-member, and the
  save refuses a member child there.
- The editable map, the draw rule, the cascade and the node budget are
  the rule that decides which occurrence of a node is editable
  ('server/to_org/expand/editable.rs', 'server/to_org/util.rs',
  'server/update_buffer/complete.rs'): the map records, as the BFS
  visits occurrences, which node has claimed an editable occurrence;
  the draw rule makes the first unclaimed occurrence editable and
  write-protects later ones; an editable-view request cascades to the
  node's content children within 'initial_node_limit'. In the diff
  view a node of U is never charged against the budget.
- A reconciler brings a parent's children in the viewforest into
  agreement with what they should be. The older one,
  'complete_relevant_children' ('server/update_buffer/util.rs',
  'complete_managed_children' after slice 0), takes a predicate for
  the children it is responsible for (its managed children: the
  members of a content list or partner folder, the entries of an
  alias, flags or ID folder), a goal list of keys and a constructor,
  and keeps, creates, reorders or detaches those children. The diff
  view's reconciler, 'reconcile_children_with_fresh_drawing'
  ('server/to_org/render/fresh_drawing.rs'), takes a fresh drawing (a
  scratch tree of what the parent's change viewnodes should be),
  matches managed children to it by match key, copies the fresh facts
  onto a kept child, descends where asked, and detaches the rest.
- The per-visit step ('process_unrestrictedVognode_changes',
  'server/to_org/render/changes.rs') runs at every editable node's
  visit in a diff-view buffer, after the content reconciler, and draws
  the node's change viewnodes: its old text, its home skgrepo move,
  its "it acquired" folder, the alias, ID and flags folders that
  changed, the partner folders whose membership changed, its
  changeset markers, and its removed content members.
- A historical phantom is a viewnode standing for a node that only the
  before-graph has, drawn with HEAD's title and body. A removed member
  is an unrestricted vognode drawn as a non-member with the fact
  '(diff rel-)', because its relationship to its viewparent exists in
  the before-graph only. A former member is any viewnode standing for
  such a before-only relationship, phantoms included.
- A viewnode is in content position when its parent is the buffer
  root, an unrestricted vognode, or a historical phantom that is
  itself in content position.
- A member role tree (until this plan, a "continuation") is the role
  tree drawn under a member of a changed partner folder in the diff
  view, so that the user sees where the member sits. Its nodes are
  role grafts: viewnodes the role-tree engine created with
  'Birth::RoleGraft (role)'. A viewnode's birth is not written into
  the buffer today, so after a save every viewnode is born
  'Unremarkable'.
- A home skgrepo move (until this plan, a "home move") is a change of
  a node's home skgrepo since HEAD. A relRepo move is a change of a
  relationship's relRepo since HEAD. The skgrepo-changes buffer shows
  the nodes whose home skgrepo moved and, after slice 16, the
  recorders of relationships whose relRepo moved.
- A stranded relationship is one whose relRepo was at its default
  before a home skgrepo move and is off its new default after it. The
  save's strand analysis ('server/from_text/strands.rs') raises the
  inbound strandeds of a privatizing move by itself and asks the user
  about the strandeds of a publicizing move. "Strandeds" is the noun
  for stranded relationships; "strand" stays a verb only.
- The LCS placement puts a removed entry where it stood by the longest
  common subsequence of the before-list and the after-list
  ('compute_interleaved_diff' in 'server/types/list.rs').
- A gitrepo is a git repository; a skgrepo is a directory of '.skg'
  files inside one. Stage-and-commit (C-c r c) stages the changed
  '.skg' files of every owned skgrepo and commits each gitrepo with
  one message; it asks for a typed "yes" when not every change is
  marked ready.
- An org fold state is whether an org headline or its body is folded
  in the client; the viewnode fields are 'folded' and 'body_folded'
  today ('org_folded' and 'org_body_folded' after slice 0) and the
  wire atoms '(folded)' and 'bodyFolded' stay as they are. To
  viewforest-unfold a node is to draw its content (the sense of U).
- The three suites are 'cargo nextest run', 'bash/emacs-tests.sh' and
  'bash/nvim-tests.sh'. At the start of this plan they pass with 772,
  366 and 17 tests.

** Stage-and-commit stops for pre-staged entries (slice 9)
Before staging anything, the server checks the index of every owned
gitrepo. If any gitrepo has entries staged already, the answer is a
confirmation of a second kind, listing each such gitrepo and its
staged paths, and nothing is staged or committed. The client shows the
list and asks the high-friction question (the typed word "yes", as the
not-all-ready confirmation already does); only a confirmed resend
proceeds, and then the commit of that gitrepo includes what was staged
before, as a git commit always records the whole index. The existing
confirmation flow carries this with a second reason: the answer is
'((needs-confirmation . t) (pre-staged ((GITREPO PATH...) ...)) ...)',
and a resend with '(confirmed . t)' covers both reasons. Both COMMANDS
files and the API section say so, and say that only '.skg' files are
staged by the command itself.

** The server says which leaves a headline names (slice 12)
When the server draws a headline that names change-forest leaves, it
writes the fact '(leaves N OBJECT...)' beside '(ready ...)': N is the
number of leaves a request naming every listed object would touch, and
the OBJECTs are those a request on this headline may name ('node'
and/or 'rel' for a vognode or phantom, each worth one leaf; 'all' for
an aggregate, worth N; none for a headline that names exactly one
leaf, which a bare request marks). The server's 'leaves_named_at' is
the one rule; the fact is display-only, and the parser accepts and
ignores it. Each client reads the fact: the objects it may request,
whether each is ready (from '(ready ...)'), and N for the wide-unmark
warning, which now fires when an unmark would touch more than one
leaf, as the server counts. The clients' tables of headline kinds and
their 'single' atom go, and so does the second implementation.
** The change leaves are the one source of "changed" (slice 13)
C is derived from the change leaves: the out-half is the recorders of
leaves; the in-half is every member named by a relationship leaf whose
relationship exists in one graph only and whose node exists in some
graph. The per-visit step draws a node's markers and change folders
from the leaves that mention it, grouped by aspect, instead of reading
'NodeChanges' and partner presence a third time. A new aspect is then
added in one place, the leaf keys, and C, the drawing and readiness
follow. The leaf builder's scan over the union of the two graphs'
nodes skips a node that is equal in both graphs, except a node that
records a member whose ID resolves to a different pid in the two
graphs (a nodeMerge since HEAD), which must still be compared; those
nodes are found from the extra-ID differences. The full scan in
'changed_pids' goes with it, so a save made while a diff-view buffer
is open no longer pays for a comparison of every node.
** One ordering and one encoding (slice 14)
Every drawing of the before/after union places a removed entry by the
LCS: the partner folders drawn over the straddle interleave their
removed members where they stood, as content and the alias, ID and
flags folders already do, instead of appending them. A removed
relationship has one encoding everywhere: an unrestricted vognode
drawn as a non-member with '(diff rel-)'. The write-protected-set
folders and a historical phantom's old content draw their removed
members that way too, and the validator's clause that discounts
members marked removed goes, since a removed member is never a member.
'is_unrestricted_removed_member' is the one reader.
** The two reconcilers obey one rule about the user's structure (slice 15)
A reconciler detaches only what it drew; what the user added survives.
The older reconciler already obeys it (a stale member branch survives
as a non-member; a stale restricted branch is deadened so that the
orphan pass keeps what is under it; only a childless stale child is
detached). The diff view's reconciler now obeys it too:
- Within a descended subtree, an unrestricted vognode born
  'Unremarkable' that is not a managed kind and carries no
  '(diff ...)' fact is the user's and is left where it stands; a role
  graft ('Birth::RoleGraft') is the server's. For that distinction to
  survive a save, the server writes a role graft's birth into its
  metadata as '(birth roleGraft ROLENAME)' (the form the parser's
  grammar comment already names, inside the '(node ...)' sexp) and
  the parser reads it back into 'Birth::RoleGraft'; the clients
  rewrite metadata structurally and strip only what they know, so the
  atom passes through a save unchanged.
- The stale treatment of the older reconciler (survive as a plain
  non-member occurrence with the diff facts cleared; deaden a property
  or folder so the orphan pass keeps its children; detach what has
  nothing of the user's under it) is lifted into one function that
  both reconcilers call.
- A comment at the per-visit step says which reconciler runs when and
  that they share the rule.
Concretely, after this slice: a note the user adds under a member of a
changed folder, under a historical phantom, in the "it acquired"
folder, or under a removed member survives every rerender, and
survives the removed member going stale.
** The skgrepo-changes buffer widens (slice 16)
The skgrepo-changes buffer's C is the recorders of leaves whose aspect
is the home skgrepo or relRepo: the nodes whose home skgrepo moved,
plus the recorders of a relationship whose relRepo moved. A node in
that C is drawn as in any buffer, with all of its changes. The buffer
keeps its name and 'DiffViewBufferKind::SkgrepoChanges';
'pcsvf_sets_for_home_moves' becomes 'pcsvf_sets_for_skgrepo_changes'.
The readiness summary does not change: it is computed over every
leaf of the change leaves whatever buffer is open, and a relRepo move
is one leaf in its recorder's file, already counted today. Only the
buffer's own count of changes not editable in it shrinks.
