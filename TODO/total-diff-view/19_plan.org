* Words this plan uses
Terms the glossary defines already are used as it defines them:
viewforest, viewnode, occurrence, editable and write-protected,
member and non-member, folder, partner folder, property folder,
viewparent, phantom (deleted, unknown, historical), restricted
vognode, change viewnode, removed member, changeset marker, the

the straddle (the before-graph, HEAD's, and the after-graph, the

kinds of change, the restore requests. Terms this plan coins or
fixes:
** non-member, 'nonMember'
A non-member is a vognode child that takes no part in the
membership its viewparent represents. On the wire it will carry
the bare atom 'nonMember' where today it carries '(affectsParent
false)'; a member carries nothing, as today. The value 'na',
which roots carry today, will go: a root carries nothing.
** retain as non-member
To retain as non-member a stale vognode with a subtree is to keep
it in place as a non-member with its title, ID, subtree and the
editability it had, so that the content reconciler can adopt it
back when its membership returns. The verb is always spelled out
this way; "retain" alone and "park" are not used.
** tombstone, entombstone
A tombstone is a viewnode with no title, no ID and no change
facts, shown as DELETED, left where a viewnode the graph no
longer warrants stood and holding that viewnode's children; the
save discards anything typed on its line. To entombstone a
viewnode is to make it one. A childless tombstone is removed by
the prune before the buffer is rendered. In code the kind is
'ViewnodeKind::Tombstone' and on the wire the atom 'tombstone';
until slice 8 the code says 'DeadViewnode' and 'deadViewnode'.
** retain as non-member or tombstone, then prune
The rule, named for what it does: a reconciler never detaches; a
stale vognode with a subtree is retained as non-member,
everything else stale (a leaf, a non-vognode) is entombstoned,
and after every completion the postorder prune removes childless
tombstones. The extra copies of a duplicate are retained
write-protected. The glossary's entry is "the one rule: convert,
then prune" today and will be renamed in slice 8.
** key, warrant, stale, duplicate, adopt
A child's key is what a reconciler matches it by: a node's ID for
an occurrence, the entry text for an alias or ID entry, the kind
for a folder or a change viewnode. The graph warrants a child
when the model its reconciler builds from the graph has an item
with the child's key; a stale child is one whose key the model
lost; a duplicate is a second child of a key already matched. A
reconciler adopts a child when a key its model has is already
among the node's children, whoever drew the child: it takes that
child instead of drawing a second.
** change facts, commit-readiness marker
The change facts are a viewnode's '(diff ...)' parts: the three
kinds of change, the leaves, the readiness. The words are never
shortened to "facts". A commit-readiness marker is the '(ready
...)' part and its herald; it is never called a bare "marker",
since a changeset marker is another thing.
** foldering, the containerward chain
To folder a role tree is to draw every partner as a member of a
partner folder of its role, never as a graft directly under a
vognode; after this plan every role tree but the contentward one
is foldered. The containerward chain is the one tree that goes on
below a folder: under a member of a containers folder its
containers hang directly as non-members, under each of those its
containers, up to the roots, as the pruned change view draws a
root's containers today. A chain node carries no change facts and
takes no commit-readiness marker; a folder's member does both.
** editability claim
The record, in the editable map, that one occurrence of a node is
the editable one, so that no other occurrence in the buffer may
be. The word is "editability claim", not "editable claim".
** viewnode ID, 'vnid', viewforest id
A viewnode ID identifies one viewnode within one buffer for the
life of the buffer. On the wire it will be the atom '(vnid X)', X
one or more lowercase letters and digits as today; to the user it
is "viewnode ID"; in code 'ViewnodeId' and 'viewnode_id'. The
identifier of a whole buffer, 'ViewId' today, will be
'ViewforestId' in code and "viewforest id" in prose; its wire key
'view-id' stays. Until slice 1 the code says 'Vid', 'vid' and
'ViewId'.
** How to say it in messages
A message names each viewnode it concerns as a subheadline of its
own: the title when there is one, then "graphnode ID: X", then
"viewnode ID: X" with the hint that 'C-c g v' finds it, or "not
yet saved" for a headline typed since the last render.

** Adoption by key among all siblings (slice 2)
*** The rule
Every reconciler matches its model against all the children of
the node, whoever drew them, by key; a child of a key the model
has is adopted; a second child of a matched key is a duplicate,
converted by the rule's duplicate clause (the editable copy is
kept, else the first in buffer order; the rest are retained
write-protected if they have a subtree, entombstoned if not). No
subtree moves.
*** The diff view's reconciler
'reconcile_children_with_shallowModelSubtree'
('server/diff_view/shallow_model_subtree.rs') takes as candidates
only the children whose owner is the caller's; it will take every
child the kind selector admits.
'align_content_members_with_straddle'
('server/diff_view/changes.rs') counts a non-member as a member
or a removed member only when the diff view owns it; it will
count any non-member whose ID is in the before-list or the
after-list, so that a copy the user pasted is set aside as the
removed member rather than doubled. The duplicate clause of
'complete_managed_children' ('server/update_buffer/util.rs') runs
in the diff view's reconciler and in the role-tree attach
('server/diff_view/partner_folders.rs', the member role trees)
too.
*** Property folders in the diff view
A property folder (aliases, IDs, flags) in a diff-view buffer is
matched by kind among all siblings, so that the folder the user
requested becomes the changed one when the property changes; it
is refreshed from the after-graph at every visit (today
'server/diff_view/visit.rs' returns at once for a property
folder, which leaves a requested folder stale); and it stays
until the user deletes it, marked changed while its property
differs between the graphs and unmarked after, never pruned by
the diff view.
*** Tests
The four duplicates of
'tests/results/review18/throwaway-folders/report.md' and
'throwaway-grafts/report.md' become tests: the requested alias
folder before the aliases change; a containerward graft beside a
removed member of the same node; a pasted write-protected copy
beside a removed member; copies of a write-protected graft. Each
asserts one child per key and a clean unchanged save. A test
shows a requested alias folder refreshed in a collateral rerender
and kept after the change ends.

** A copy retained as non-member keeps its editability (slice 3)
*** The rule
'convert_unwarranted_child' ('server/update_buffer/util.rs')
write-protects every retained vognode and makes its members
non-members. It will leave an editable vognode editable, with its
members as they are, and leave a write-protected one
write-protected, with its members made non-members as today (a
write-protected occurrence may hold no member). The extra copies
of a duplicate are write-protected whatever they were, since the
save refuses two editable occurrences of one node
('Multiple_Defining_Viewnodes').
*** The editability walk
'apply_editability_rule' ('server/to_org/extend/editable.rs')
gives the claim to the first occurrence met in the breadth-first
walk when neither carries a request. It will prefer a member to a
non-member: a non-member tentative occurrence yields to a member
met later, whose content subtree is then extended, and the
non-member is write-protected as a yielding occurrence is today.
The outcome 'EditabilityOutcome::Deferred' is renamed
'ClaimedElsewhere', and its branch makes the yielding
occurrence's member children non-members
('make_member_children_nonMembers'), which it does not today, so
that the save no longer refuses the buffer with
'MemberChildOfWriteProtectedOccurrence'.

** Every role tree is a folder: the server (slice 4)
*** The rule
A partner stands only in a partner folder of its role, as a
write-protected member; a vognode child of a vognode is content
or a non-member note. Every role tree but the contentward one is
write-protected throughout: a folder's members and the chain
below them. A folder's member takes a readiness gesture, as every
folder member does today; a chain node carries no change facts
and takes none. Only a folder's members show change.
*** The containers folder and the chain
Every fresh content view puts a containerward request on its
root, which 'fulfill_root_containerward_requests'
('server/update_buffer.rs') fulfils by drawing the whole
containerward tree directly under the root. It will draw a
containers folder on the root instead, with the chain under each
member: the member's containers as non-members under it, theirs
under them, breadth-first to 'max_roleTree_depth' as today, every
node write-protected. The pruned change view draws a root's
containers this way already ('docs/diff-view.org', "Stub roots");
the content view will do the same, and the folder request
'(folder containers)' will draw the chain under the folder's
members too, in every view. A chain is drawn when the folder is,
and extended by no later request; a request on a chain node for
its containers draws a containers folder under it like any other.
*** The nine role-tree requests
The request '(viewRequests (roleTree ROLENAME))' and its engine
('server/to_org/extend/role_tree.rs': the paths, branches and
cycles, the containerward attach,
'mk_writeProtected_from_viewnode' with a birth) go. The server
will refuse the request as an unknown view request. Eight of its
roles are reached by the folder requests that exist ('(folder
containers)', '(folder mentioners)', '(folder overrides)',
'(folder hidesFromSubs)', '(folder subscribesTo)', each building
both folders of its relation where both exist); the ninth, the
mentioned role, has no folder and will have no request. The
search buffer ('render_enriched_search_buffer.rs') draws a
containerward tree and two override chains as grafts under each
hit; it will draw a containers folder with the chain, an
overrider folder and an overridden folder under each hit, folded.
*** The member role trees of the diff view
'attach_memberRoleTrees_under_straddle_folder_members'
('server/diff_view/partner_folders.rs') hangs, under a changed
folder's member, the member's role tree with the containerward
attach under each graft, and 'tag_before_only_role_grafts' marks
before-only grafts '(rel removed)'. It will hang the chain alone
under a containers folder's member, nothing under any other
folder's member, and tag nothing: a chain node carries no change

*** The command that integrates unshown changes

('server/diff_view/pruned_change_view.rs') makes a change shown

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
Facts about the code as it stands, each with its place, that an
agent would otherwise rediscover or trip over.
** The saved view is rebuilt from the parsed buffer
After a save, the saved view's forest is the one rerendered from
the parsed text ('server/update_buffer.rs', the saved-view
branch), so every per-viewnode state that must survive a save
rides in an atom; the parser treats an unknown atom as a hard
error. Collateral views and "rerender all views" re-complete a
clone of the stored forest instead, with its change facts. An
ego_tree 'NodeId' is never stable across a save; a viewnode ID
is.
** The parser builds the twin struct
Headlines parse into the maybe-placed viewnode
('server/types/maybe_placed_viewnode.rs') and are placed into
'Viewnode' later; a field removed or renamed goes on both, and
the 'Viewnode {' struct literals in 'server/' and 'tests/' each
follow (plan 15 did this by script; do the same).
** The completion runs every reconciler in one BFS, then prunes
'complete_viewforest' ('server/update_buffer/complete.rs') visits
nodes breadth-first, each visit running its node's reconcilers,
and calls 'prune_self_deletable_when_empty' at the end, before
the buffer is rendered; the prune is postorder, collapses a chain
of childless tombstones in one pass, stops at a kind it does not
prune, and never prunes a view root. The viewnode IDs are
assigned after the prune, in 'finish_viewforest'.
** Where a candidate is chosen and where it is converted
'reconcile_children_with_shallowModelSubtree'
('server/diff_view/shallow_model_subtree.rs') filters candidates
by 'child.owner == owner && selects(child)' and converts the
unmatched ones; 'convert_unwarranted_subtree' there decides by
owner what under a stale child stays. The older reconciler
('complete_managed_children', 'server/update_buffer/util.rs')
chooses by shape: a member of the content list, an entry of the
folder's kind, a child 'skgid_a_folder_child_stands_for' gives an
ID (a member, a removed member, an unknown or historical
phantom). The folder reconcilers already adopt a returning key
('mark_goal_children_as_folder_members',
'server/to_org/complete/partner_folder/child_data.rs') and so
does the content reconciler
('adopt_nonMember_children_that_are_content',
'server/update_buffer/reconcile/content.rs').
** Change facts last until a child is found stale
'parse_change_fact_part' ('parse_metadata_sexp.rs') keeps the
'(diff ...)' parts on vognodes and phantoms; nothing strips them
before the reconcilers run; 'convert_unwarranted_child' clears
them, 'replace_kind' clears them, 'adopt_model_facts' replaces
them on matched children. So a removed member still says '(rel
removed)' when the diff view finds it stale after a commit, and
only after conversion do a retained removed member, a graft whose
birth was cleared and a pasted write-protected copy share one
shape. A pasted copy of a removed member carries the same change
facts as the original, since 'tag_content_members' tags by ID.

Only in the diff view: the candidate filter and the stale-subtree
conversion above, 'align_content_members_with_straddle'
('changes.rs'), and the hand-over of the content reconciler's

reconciler, the folder reconcilers and the role-tree attach stamp
owners and never read them. 'set_owners_in_parsed_viewforest'
('server/types/views_state.rs') gives a headline whose viewnode
ID the table does not know the owner 'User'; of two headlines
with one ID the first in buffer order keeps it
('uninterpreted.rs').
** What the birth is read for today
'relationship_to_viewparent' ('readiness_requests.rs'), read by
the leaves and ready facts, the readiness requests and the

('shallow_model_subtree.rs'), read by the folder reconciler's

('pruned_change_view.rs'); the parser's 'restoreRel' placement

** How role trees are drawn today
Three ways. The root's containerward tree: 'render_initial_view'
('update_buffer.rs') puts 'ViewRequest::RoleTree(CONTAINER)' on
every root, 'extract_view_requests'
('reconcile/view_requests.rs') strips it from roots, and
'fulfill_root_containerward_requests' draws the whole tree
breadth-first ('insert_full_containerward_role_tree_recursive',
'role_tree.rs', to 'max_roleTree_depth') with no check for a tree
already there. A '(roleTree ROLE)' request elsewhere: the path
engine ('build_and_integrate_role_tree_then_drop_request',
'role_tree.rs') draws a path to the first branch or cycle,
reusing any existing child of the wanted ID
('find_child_by_skgid',
'server/types/tree/viewnode_graphnode.rs') without a birth, and
then the attach hangs containers under grafts born of the role,
for every role but container. The diff view's member role trees:
'attach_memberRoleTrees_under_straddle_folder_members' in a
scratch tree, then a reconcile against children owned by the
attach. The pruned change view already draws a root's containers
as a folder with the chain. Partner folders are matched by kind
alone in the diff view ('child_folder_or_new', 'changes.rs');
property folders by owner.
** The save's rules for members and copies
A write-protected occurrence may hold no member

** The editability walk
'apply_editability_rule' ('server/to_org/extend/editable.rs')
runs in the breadth-first completion with a map rebuilt every

write-protects a prior 'Tentative' occurrence's content subtree;
a request met after a 'Final' one is dropped and the occurrence
write-protected ('Deferred'), without its members made
non-members. Of two tentative occurrences the first met wins.
