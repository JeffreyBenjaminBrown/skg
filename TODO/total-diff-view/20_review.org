** TODO [Claude] A regression: 'restoreRel' refused in a folder
'is_in_containerward_chain' climbs through non-member vognodes to
a member of a chain-carrying folder and does not ask whether that
member is write-protected, so a non-member under a subscribee
extended as editable counts as in the chain, and 'restoreRel' is
refused there. A throwaway probe confirmed the concern as a
regression. In the all-changes buffer, with the subscribee
folder's member t made editable, u, a removed member of t, is
drawn under it as a non-member carrying '(rel removed)', and
'restoreRel' on it is refused as a chain node, while the same
request on u under the root t is accepted; after one unchanged
save the root's u is gone, its changes having moved to the
editable occurrence, so nothing in the buffer can then restore
u's membership. The cause is 'restoreRel_stands_where_it_may'
('local.rs'), whose chain test reads position alone and ignores
change facts; both clients' guards do the same. The fix is one
condition in three places, a non-member carrying '(rel removed)'
being a removed member wherever it stands, with the probe's case
as the test. I would run it now, as a slice 14, if you say so.

** TODO [Claude] What a stale child takes with it
Without owners the diff view takes along, with a stale child, its
folder members and its drawn chain nodes, and leaves the rest.
Two behaviours differ from the owner rule: a folder you requested
under a stale child goes with it, which I would keep, since a
folder under a stale child is clutter you can request again; and
a write-protected copy you paste under a folder member reads as a
chain node. A throwaway probe confirmed it: a pasted childless
copy under a folder member is converted and pruned at the first
save, while the same copy with a note under it is kept as a
write-protected non-member holding the note, and the same copy
pasted under a content member stays where it is. I would keep
this: the childless copy is a shell, and anything you type under
it keeps it.
