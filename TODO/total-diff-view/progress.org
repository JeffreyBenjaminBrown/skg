#+title: Total diff view (TDV) — progress notes
* Slice 1: deviations
** a rule holds its 'node_heralds' key, not its style name
'HeraldRule' carries 'node_heralds_key'; 'HeraldRule::style' looks the
name up. Holding the key lets the consistency check report a styled
rule with no entry, instead of the table panicking while it is built.
The served sexp still carries the uppercased style name.
** qualified 'node_heralds' keys
An atom styled differently in different places gets a qualified key:
'affectsParent_false', and 'editRequest_relRepo' and 'editRequest_flag'
for the requested forms. The ☮ on a write-protected label or flag row
takes 'writeProtected''s style. Later slices that add atoms (the diff
atoms, the labels) add entries the same way.
** 'ready' is listed before its atom exists
The plan names 'ready' go, so 'node_heralds' has it now. The check only
requires that every styled rule has an entry, not the converse. The
retiring 'staged' and 'unstaged' keep go and stop until slice 9.
* Slice 2: deviations
** foreign skgrepos: read at the worktree in both halves
The plan excludes foreign skgrepos from both halves. Excluding them
literally would leave every owned-to-foreign relationship dangling in
both graphs, and a foreign-home node with owned sections titleless. So
the before-graph reads each foreign skgrepo from its worktree, as the
live graph does. Foreign sections are then the same in both halves and
never produce a change; foreign skgrepos need not be gitrepos.
*** consequence: a sibling of the HEAD reader
'read_graph_snapshot_maybe_cached (config, Head)' reads every skgrepo
at HEAD. The pair instead uses 'read_owned_head_graph_snapshot_maybe_cached',
which shares that reader's per-skgrepo loop and its cache, keyed by
'owned_head_identity' (the owned skgrepos' HEAD commits). That key
ignores foreign files. A foreign file changed on disk after the server
started but before the first pair is built would differ from the live
graph, and its node would enter C. Nothing in the session writes
foreign files.
*** quasi-duplication
'owned_head_identity' resembles 'validate_skgrepos_for_selection' plus
'head_cache_identity', with the refusal text the plan wants. Those two
retire with the diff report in slice 9.
** 'from_head_and_live (config, live_graph)'
It takes the live graph rather than the runtime generation, so tests
need no Tantivy index.
** partner identity and role naming
'partners (pid, role)': 'role' is the role the PARTNER plays, as in
role trees ('CONTAINER' gives the containers, '(Contains, Second)' the
content). Each graph identifies a member by its own resolution of the
stored ID, as its recorderward relmaps do, so the outbound and inbound
answers agree. The queried ID is a primary ID, unresolved.
*** consequence for nodeMerges
A node that lists an acquiree B by B's old ID resolves that ID to B at
HEAD and to the acquirer A in the live graph. Its content then shows
B as OnlyBefore and A as OnlyAfter, and the node is rightward in C,
although its file did not change.
** what 'node_changes' and 'changed_pids' report
'NodeChanges' has 'title_changed' and 'body_changed' ('text_changed'
is their disjunction), so slice 6 can choose "old title", "old body"
or both. Aliases are compared by text. A node not in both graphs has
empty 'NodeChanges'; its change is its existence. Leftward excludes
IDs that neither graph has as a node (dangling references).
** deferred to later slices
'order_only_pids' needs the change-forest, so slice 3 adds it. The
pair lives in 'ViewsState::graph_pair', and 'current_graph_pair_in_slot'
reuses it, re-pairs it with a newer live graph, or rebuilds it when an
owned HEAD moved (which covers stage-and-commit). Building it on a TDV
request and dropping it when the last TDV buffer closes come with the
TDV buffers (slice 5).
** the test helpers were moved, not copied
'tests/git_diff_view/common.rs' is now 'tests/shared/git_fixture_helpers.rs',
used by both the git diff view tests and 'tests/graph_pair.rs', so it
is not duplicated. The fixtures are in 'tests/graph_pair/fixtures/'.
* Slice 3: deviations
** the new property atoms wait for their kinds
'(oldText)', '(bodyDiff)', '(homeMove ...)', '(relRepoMove ...)',
'(changesetMarker ...)' and '(historical)' mark viewnode kinds that
slice 4 creates, so slice 4 parses and serializes them with the kinds.
'(movedLikeAncestor ...)' is a vognode fact slice 7 computes. A
folder ('PartnerFolder' is a fieldless enum) cannot yet carry
'(changed)' or an aggregate '(ready ...)'; those come when slice 5
draws folders because of a change.
** where the facts sit on the wire
'(diff ...)', '(ready ...)' and the readiness requests sit inside
'(node ...)', '(deleted ...)' and '(unknown ...)', and at top level on
the alias, id and flag leaves (the form the existing '(staged ...)'
takes there). The parser refuses them at top level on anything else.
The folder atoms are bare, like the existing folder atoms:
'containerFolder', 'mentionerFolder'.
*** which viewnodes carry them
'ChangeFacts' (presence and ready) and 'readiness_request' are on the
unrestricted vognode, the deleted and unknown phantoms, and the alias,
id and flag leaves. Not on 'PhantomDiff', which retires in slice 9.
*** what a request names
On a vognode, 'node' is its existence leaf, 'rel' the relationship to
its viewparent (content, or the relation of the partner folder it is
in), 'all' every leaf the node owns ('leaves_under'), and no axis both
'node' and 'rel'. On a single-axis leaf (alias, id, flag, deleted,
unknown) no axis names its one leaf. A request that names no current
change is dropped with a warning. Until slice 4 draws the old-text
property, a text leaf can be marked only through 'all' on its node.
** change-forest details
*** roots
The roots are the rightward nodes that own a leaf. A leftward-only
node owns none: its change is its recorders' relationship leaves.
*** fingerprints
A new or deleted node's existence leaf hashes the node's own content
(title, body, aliases, extra IDs, flags, home), so editing a new node
lapses its mark. A relationship's existence leaf hashes which graphs
hold it; its relRepo leaf, the two relRepos. 'leaf_fingerprint'
returns 'Option<u64>': None when the change does not exist.
*** files
A node aspect is recorded in the node's home file, a home move in both
homes' files, a relationship in its relRepo's file (both, for a
relRepo move). A file is order-only when its node is in both graphs,
owns no leaf, and differs between them.
*** foreign changed files
'GraphPair::foreign_changed_files' is counted when the pair is built:
the '.skg' files that differ between HEAD and the worktree in each
foreign skgrepo that is in a gitrepo with a HEAD commit.
** the marks file
JSON object keys must be strings, so '<data_root>/readiness-marks.json'
holds a list of '{ "leaf": KEY, "fingerprint": N }' entries
(documented in 'docs/api-and-formats.org').
** the role-tree engine takes a partners source, not a bare function
'build_and_integrate_role_tree_with_skgrepo_set' takes
'RoleTreePartners' ('LiveGraph' or 'GraphPair'). Over the live graph
the engine keeps its two gatings: the role tree's steps see only what
the skgrepo restriction does, while the containerward trees attached
beneath are walked ungated and gated where drawn. One function could
not carry both. The generic engines are 'paths_to_first_nonlinearities_via'
and 'full_containerward_role_tree' (over a 'PartnersOf');
'full_containerward_role_tree_from_inRustGraph' keeps its name as the
live-graph wrapper.
** a partner folder over a graph pair
'reconcile_partnerFolder_children' takes a 'PartnerFolderGoalSource'.
Over a pair, a member whose node exists only in the before-graph is
drawn as an unknown phantom, tagged node- rel-, until slice 4's
historical phantom replaces it. Nothing uses the pair source yet;
slice 5 does.
** strands
*** where the client analysis was
It lived in 'elisp/skg-metadata.el' ('skg-set-repo'), not in
'skg-request-relRepo-info.el'. Neovim's 'set_repo' never had one, so
Neovim only gained the confirmation handling. The ERT tests of the old
analysis are gone; their scenarios are in 'tests/unit/strands.rs'.
*** what counts as stranded
A relationship touching a moved node is stranded when its relRepo was
its default before the save and the sticky-else-default rule leaves it
off its new default. One that would be left more public than its
member's new home is raised without asking: that is exactly an
inbound relationship of a privatizing move. A hide's default is the
hide rule's ('hide_skgrepo'). Mentions have no relRepo and are
skipped.
*** the round trip
The analysis runs before the Hoist gate, so recorders that
re-leveling adds are gated too, and its confirmation precedes every
other one. The confirmation carries IDs, no titles. The re-save
carries '(strands-reviewed . "true")' and '(relevel-strands ("REL"
"REC" "MEM") ...)'; the clients offer all, none, each in turn, or
quit. Documented in 'docs/api-and-formats.org' and 'docs/COMMANDS*.org'.
** smaller things
'NodeRelation' derives serde and gains 'from_relation_name', which
replaced a private copy in 'paths.rs'. The folder commands are 'C-c l
c' and 'C-c l m' in Emacs, '<localleader>cc' and 'cm' in Neovim.
* Slice 4: deviations
** wire forms
*** '(oldText ASPECT)'
The label ("old title and body", "old title", "old body") needs to
know which aspects changed, and an empty headline or body cannot say,
so the form names it: '(oldText titleAndBody)', '(oldText title)' or
'(oldText body)'. A body HEAD lacked is the empty string.
*** '(homeMove (old A) (new B))' and '(relRepoMove (old A) (new B))'
not '(homeMove OLD NEW)'. The herald engine's ANY/IT gives every
value of a form the same prefix, so two positional names cannot
render as "⌂ -A +B" or "relationship moved from A to B". The names
are styled 'normal' through the 'node_heralds' keys
'homeMove_skgrepo' and 'relRepoMove_skgrepo'.
*** the phantom
'(historical (id X) (repo R) ...)', the grammar of '(deleted ...)'.
Its heralds are a ☮, then ∃- and ~- with a ✓ after each ready axis.
** what the kinds carry
'Phantom::Historical' carries 'change_facts' (presence and ready) and
'readiness_request', like the deleted and unknown phantoms, in place
of the plan's 'presence'. Every new property but 'BodyDiff' does too;
'BodyDiff' is '{ diff }', its body. 'ChangesetMarkerKind' is 'Content',
'RelRepo' or 'OrderOnly'.
** the save consumes their readiness requests
'server/serve/handlers/readiness_requests.rs' names, for an
old text, its node's text leaf; for a home move, its node's home
leaf; for a relRepo move, the relRepo leaf of the relationship
between the member it hangs under and that member's viewparent; for
the content or relRepo changeset marker, the leaves of that aspect of
the content relationships its node records ('content_relationships_of');
for the order-only marker, nothing. A historical phantom's leaves are
keyed by its ID as written, a node of the before-graph: the
after-graph would resolve an acquiree to its acquirer.
** the builders
'server/to_org/render/change_viewnodes.rs' draws each kind from the
graph pair, for slice 5 to place: 'append_old_text' (with the body
diff, a ' '/'-'/'+' line diff), 'mk_home_move_viewnode',
'mk_relRepo_move_viewnode', 'mk_changeset_marker_viewnode',
'mk_historical_viewnode', 'append_historical_phantom' and
'append_acquired_folder'. 'GraphPair::acquirer_of' and 'acquirees_of'
derive the acquisition folders' members from the after-graph's extra
IDs.
*** a historical phantom's old content
A deleted member is a historical phantom with its own old content; a
surviving member is a write-protected vognode tagged rel-; a dangling
one is an unknown phantom tagged rel-. A node already drawn above
itself in the recursion is drawn without content. The vognodes under
a historical phantom keep affectsParent true (under a deleted phantom
they are demoted): they are drawn as its old content, and the save
reads nothing under a phantom.
*** the acquisition folders
"it was acquired by" (under the historical phantom) holds the
acquirer, write-protected. "it acquired" (under the acquirer) holds
bare historical phantoms, without their content, which is drawn where
the acquiree itself is drawn. The labels are lower-case, as the plan
writes them, unlike the other folders' sentences.
** where the validator allows them
A historical phantom may be a view root or a vognode's child, and may
hold vognodes and an "it was acquired by" folder. An old text, a move
or a changeset marker needs a vognode parent and is unique among its
siblings (a marker per kind); a body diff needs an old-text parent.
The acquisition folders need a historical phantom or an ordinary
vognode for a parent, as above.
** the rerender until slice 5
View completion leaves the new viewnodes alone (the acquisition
folders have no 'relation_member_role', so no reconciler runs on
them), except that the content reconciler moves a historical phantom
ahead of the live members it stood among. Positioning removed members
is slice 5's 'process_unrestrictedVognode_changes'. So the round-trip
test ('tests/tdv/roundtrip/view.rs') checks that the first save
changes no file and drops none of the new viewnodes, and that its
result is a fixed point of a second save.
** clients
Both clients read the ID and skgrepo of a historical phantom at point,
as they do a deleted one's. Emacs refuses a relRepo request on a
member of either acquisition folder.
* Slice 5: deviations
** names and shapes
'server/pcsvf.rs' has 'PcsvfSets { c, r, u, d }', 'pcsvf_sets (pair)'
(cached in the pair as 'GraphPair::pcsvf_sets', like 'changed_pids'),
'pcsvf_sets_over (pair, c)' for slice 7's subsets, and
'pcsvf_viewforest (runtime, pair, store, roots)', which returns the
text, pids and viewforest as 'multi_root_view_via_runtime' does; the
change-forest comes from the pair. There is no separate 'PCSVF' type.
The per-visit step is 'server/to_org/render/changes.rs'
('process_unrestrictedVognode_changes'), beside 'diff.rs'. The
containerward climb of 'server/prominence.rs' is lifted to a
containers function ('climb_containerward_to_cycle_via'); one climb
from a changed node gives both its walk and its origins, because the
walk stops at the first multiply-contained node.
** readiness is set after completion, not at each visit
'set_ready_facts_in_viewforest' ('readiness_requests.rs') sets every
'(ready ...)' in one pass over the completed viewforest, from the
same leaf naming the save's readiness requests use (refactored into
'leaves_named_at' and 'keys_in_forest'). An axis is ready when it
names at least one leaf and all of them are marked. Slice 6's save
reconcile can call the same pass.
** the folder's '(changed)' fact
The plan writes '(changed)'; a folder drawn because of a change is
written '(containerFolder changed)' in place of the bare atom, and
'(ready)' follows when every changed member is ready. The lens engine
matches a bare atom and a form with the same head separately, so the
changed label replaces the ordinary one instead of adding to it. The
fact is not stored: 'PartnerFolder' is a fieldless enum matched in
some 170 places, so the serializer derives both facts from the
folder's members ('folder_change_facts' in 'org_to_text.rs'): a member
with a presence sign, or with a relRepo move under it. The parser
accepts and drops them. Labels: "its aliases / IDs / flags /
containers / mentioners / subscribers / overriders / hiders /
subscribees changed", and "the nodes it overrides / hides changed",
each with "(this file)" or "(other files)". The new 'node_heralds' key
is 'changed' (message).
** removed members and what the save reads
*** a removed member that survives
is drawn under its former container as a write-protected vognode with
affectsParent false, tagged rel-. As affectsParent true the save would
re-add it. Likewise in the editable subscribee and overridden folders.
*** a removed dangling ID
is not drawn in an editable node's content or an editable folder:
the save reads an unknown phantom there as a member. Its leaf is
still under the content changeset marker.
*** lost aliases and IDs
The alias folder is read at save, and the ID folder is checked
against the node's IDs. Both now skip an entry tagged '(diff rel-)'
('visit_aliasFolder', 'validate_tree.rs'), as the ID check already
skipped the old view's removed entries. This is the one place the
save reads a presence fact. Entries carry their + or - on the rel
axis.
** folders and continuations
The partner folders of a node in C are drawn when a partner's
relationship is only in one graph (and, for the node's own lists,
also when its relRepo moved); the BFS fills them from the pair. In
the pair-driven reconciler, a member only in the before-graph is now
a historical phantom, and each member (each changed one, in an
out-folder) gets the continuation: a full containerward tree under a
container, the folder role's role tree under any other. A relRepo
move hangs under its member in content and in folders. The
Subscribee folder goes through the generic reconciler when the pair
is set. Alias, ID and flags folders are filled at the node's visit;
their own reconcilers do nothing when the pair is set. The default
HiddenInSubscribee folder is not created in a TDV.
** override substitution
is off in a TDV, as in the old diff view: the content is the union of
the stored lists.
** the request
The merges, skgrepo-changes and order-only buffers answer
'(empty . t)' until slice 7 draws them. A refusal is a content
response with the error and no content; the clients stop the
sequence at the first refusal. The empty answer is
'((empty . t) (errors ()) (warnings ()))' after the response type.
'ViewState' records 'tdv_buffer' ('TdvBuffer'), set by
'register_tdv_view'; closing the last such view drops the pair.
** clients
Both clients chain the four requests (each one-shot handler sends the
next after it returns, since the handler map holds one handler per
response type). Neovim names the buffers "skg://diff-view: ...", as
its content views are "skg://...". 'C-c v d' and '<localleader>vd'
now run the new command; the old toggle is unbound until slice 9
removes it, and 'docs/COMMANDS*.org' say so.
** an ego_tree pitfall
'prepend_id' and 'insert_id_before' (ego-tree 0.6.3) read the old
first child or previous sibling before detaching the node they move,
so moving a node already in that place links it to itself and a
traversal never ends. 'changes.rs' detaches first. Noted in
'TODO/problems.org'.
