* Scope of these rules
When I ask you to execute a plan or implement a change, continue until
the agreed work and verification are complete. I won't review an execution
assignment until the plan is executed, unless something goes sideways.

A request only for a review, diagnosis, discussion, or plan is complete
when you deliver that result. Do not begin implementation merely because
the resulting plan contains work to do. When planning, use the decision
criteria below to identify what I ought to weigh in on.

* Reasons to stop during execution
** An architectural decision we did not anticipate
Stop when completing the goal requires a material choice outside the
agreed design. Explain the concrete consequences and recommend an option.
** A stumper mystery
This means at least one goal from the plan that you don't see how to
achieve, or cannot achieve without doing something ugly.
* Resolve ordinary implementation choices yourself
Deviations from the plan on *how* to achieve its goals are not reasons to
stop. Record meaningful deviations in progress.org, leaving the original
plan as is, and continue. I trust you to make those choices.

If a question turns out to be trivial, do not stop for it. If you are
uncertain but the choice is easy to reverse, use your best judgment.
If there are no substantive questions and agreed work remains, keep going.
Once the work and verification are complete, report completion.

* Reporting a substantive stop
Report what's new in the task's progress.org under these headlines:
** What's done and went well
** Changes to the plan's envisioned order
** What's imperfect about what's done
Do not list work merely because it is unfinished but still part of the plan.
** Choices determining the future

* A stop report does not require a continuous ledger
Create or update progress.org when a substantive stop needs that report,
or when recording a meaningful implementation deviation. This instruction
already authorizes that concise report; do not ask permission to write it.

Ordinary execution does not require a chronological ledger, dashboard, or
ongoing bookkeeping at every step. Those are optional workflows described
in [[file:optional-workflows.org]], and require the agreement specified there.
